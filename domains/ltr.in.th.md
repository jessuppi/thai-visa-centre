# ltr.in.th

`ltr.in.th` is a privately operated Long-Term Resident (LTR) visa information website associated with [Agents Co., Ltd.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md) and [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md). Its published legal policies identify AGENTS CO., LTD. as the contracting business, while its homepage promotes Thai Visa Centre's immigration services and compares the agency with businesses certified by Thailand's Board of Investment (BOI).[^1][^2][^3]

## Commercial Promotion and Certified-Agent Comparisons

The website provides extensive information about Thailand's LTR visa program, reproduces government announcements, and offers an unofficial visa qualification test. It prominently advertises Thai Visa Centre as a **"Trusted LTR Visa Agent"**, accompanied by customer testimonials and claims that the agency has served more than **100,000 expatriates** and is Thailand's leading visa agent. These promotional assertions are made by the commercially connected website and have not been independently substantiated.[^1]

Thailand's Board of Investment maintains an official list of certified agencies authorized to provide LTR visa application assistance. Its published list includes organizations such as Siam Legal, Thai Relo Services, Star Visa Service 14, and other approved providers. **Neither Thai Visa Centre nor AGENTS CO., LTD. appears on the current published list.** The distinction matters because BOI certification involves a formal approval process and defined responsibilities rather than simply possessing experience assisting visa applicants.[^4]

Notably, `ltr.in.th` itself acknowledges this distinction. An October 2026 comparison table explicitly identifies Thai Visa Centre as **"Not BOI certified"** while describing it as the leading uncertified agent by customer reviews. The website claims the agency has **4,240 reviews across Google, Facebook, and Trustpilot**, compared with a combined **4,112 Google reviews** attributed to selected certified agencies.[^1]

**This comparison uses inconsistent measurement criteria.** Thai Visa Centre's total combines three review platforms, while the competing certified agencies are primarily assessed through Google business listings. The figures also concern general business reviews rather than independently verified LTR visa applications, approvals, customer outcomes, or service quality. The website excludes True Digital Park's broader Google business-review total from its calculation because those reviews do not specifically concern its visa services, but it does not demonstrate that Thai Visa Centre's own aggregate reviews specifically concern LTR visa assistance.[^1]

The presentation therefore risks implying that review volume establishes superiority over officially certified competitors. **The BOI's certification status and a commercial agency's customer-review totals measure fundamentally different things.** The available evidence does not establish that Thai Visa Centre provides inferior services or that any reviews are fabricated. It does establish that the website promotes a commercially associated agency through a comparison whose underlying figures are not directly equivalent.[^1][^4]

The website also operates a traveler discussion forum containing hundreds of comments, including contributions from an account identified as **"THAI VISA CENTRE - visa agent"** with a verified administrator designation. This gives the agency a prominent position within a discussion platform where prospective customers seek immigration advice. The authenticity and independence of other participants' testimonials cannot be established from the published material alone.[^1]

The website's Privacy Policy identifies AGENTS CO., LTD. as the responsible contracting entity and permits collecting contact information, account details, and information submitted through website forms. It also identifies public databases and joint marketing partners as potential sources of personal information. Its Terms & Conditions acknowledge that some external links may generate affiliate commissions, although they do not establish whether referrals to Thai Visa Centre are commission-based.[^2][^3]

Thailand's BOI allows eligible applicants to submit LTR applications directly through its official online system without paying an endorsement fee. Approved applicants generally pay **50,000 baht for visa issuance in Thailand**, while optional commercial assistance may involve additional charges. The BOI also publishes its own list of certified agents for applicants seeking professional representation.[^4][^5]

**The central concern is the promotion of an uncertified commercial visa agency through an information website that uses government program material, customer testimonials, and uneven competitor comparisons.** Although the website discloses its unofficial status and Thai Visa Centre's lack of BOI certification, its promotional rankings are not supported by independently verified, comparable measures of LTR visa service performance.

The arrangement warrants further scrutiny of **advertising transparency, corporate relationships, review authenticity, and the distinction between official accreditation and commercially promoted reputation**.
