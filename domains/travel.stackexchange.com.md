# travel.stackexchange.com

Travel Stack Exchange is a community-operated travel discussion website within the Stack Exchange network, operated by Stack Exchange, Inc.

## Commercial Promotion and Affiliation Disclosure

Travel Stack Exchange's published participation guidelines permit contributors to reference their own commercial products, services, and websites in relevant circumstances, provided that they disclose their affiliation within the contribution itself.[^1]

The guidelines discourage contributors from repeatedly answering questions for the purpose of promoting their own businesses and direct parties primarily interested in commercial promotion toward the company's advertising services.

These requirements are particularly relevant to immigration discussions in which commercial visa agents may recommend their own services or affiliated immigration websites while appearing to provide independent travel advice.

## Deceptive Traffic Generation

The Stack Exchange network's Acceptable Use Policy separately prohibits undisclosed affiliate marketing, deceptive methods of generating revenue or website traffic, and attempts to mislead users into surrendering sensitive personal information.[^2]

The policy also prohibits links created to facilitate fraud or scams.

These provisions provide a documented framework for evaluating immigration-related recommendations, particularly those directing travelers toward unofficial visa application portals or websites requesting passport information.

## Enforcement and Accountability

Stack Exchange reserves the right to remove contributions and restrict or terminate accounts for violations of its Acceptable Use Policy. The company also provides an appeal mechanism for affected users.[^2]

Whether these commercial disclosure and consumer-protection standards have been consistently enforced in discussions involving Thai immigration services remains an open investigative question.

No commercial relationship between Thai Visa Centre and Stack Exchange has been independently established.
