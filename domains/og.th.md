# og.th

`og.th` is a commercial Thai cannabis delivery website. Its published legal terms identify **OG Thai Co., Ltd.** as the operator.[^1]

## Online Cannabis Checkout Presented as Medical Treatment

OG.TH continues advertising nationwide cannabis ordering and delivery despite Thailand's June 2025 restrictions on online cannabis sales.[^2][^3]

Its published delivery guide tells customers to select cannabis products from a catalog, complete checkout, and receive delivery at their chosen location.

The website advertises credit-card payments, PromptPay, bank transfers, cash on delivery, and other conventional retail payment methods.[^2]

These are descriptions of an online commercial purchasing process, not merely informational material about medical cannabis.

### The Government Prohibits Online Sales

Thailand's Ministry of Public Health restrictions, effective June 26, 2025, prohibit online sales of cannabis flower and require medical prescriptions for lawful patient access.[^3]

The Department of Thai Traditional and Alternative Medicine reaffirmed these restrictions during 2026 enforcement announcements, identifying online and electronic sales among the violations that can result in regulatory penalties.[^10]

**A valid medical prescription does not, by itself, establish that an operator may sell cannabis through an otherwise prohibited online retail channel.**

### Rebranding Orders as Treatment Requests

OG.TH separately publishes a telemedicine compliance document identifying Herb Plug Thai Traditional Medicine Clinic as the medical provider and GACP Co., Ltd. as its operator.[^4]

That document describes an integrated system in which patients obtain medical consultations, receive P.T.33 prescriptions, submit treatment requests through OG.TH, and have approved requests fulfilled.

It presents the activity as regulated medical dispensing rather than ordinary cannabis retail.

However, the public delivery guide continues describing customers selecting products, checking out, making payments, and receiving shipments.

**The distinction between a medical treatment request and an online commercial sale is central to determining whether OG.TH's business model complies with Thailand's restrictions.**

OG.TH does not publicly explain how its advertised checkout process is legally distinguished from the online cannabis sales prohibited by the Ministry of Public Health.

### A Telemedicine License Is Not a General Online-Sales Authorization

OG.TH claims that Herb Plug Clinic is authorized to provide telemedicine consultations under the Department of Thai Traditional and Alternative Medicine.[^4]

Even assuming that authorization is valid, permission to conduct medical consultations is a separate matter from authorization to sell controlled cannabis products through electronic commerce.

The website does not identify a regulatory exemption expressly permitting its advertised nationwide online checkout and delivery arrangement.

The clinic's published standard operating procedures describe prescription controls and dispensing records, but these explanations are provided by the commercial operator itself.

**OG.TH's public documentation does not establish that its telemedicine authorization overrides the government's prohibition on online cannabis sales.**

The actual transaction workflow and the relevant regulatory permissions would need examination before determining whether prohibited online sales are occurring.

## Conflicting Telemedicine Authorization Dates Across Languages

OG.TH's multilingual compliance pages have displayed inconsistent information concerning Herb Plug Clinic's telemedicine authorization.

As checked on November 17, 2026, the English, Thai, Japanese, and Russian pages displayed **November 20, 2027** as the telemedicine permission's expiry date.[^4][^5][^12][^13]

An older retrieved copy of the Chinese-language page, crawled approximately two months earlier, instead displayed **October 31, 2026**.[^11]

The Chinese-language copy may since have changed; its current live value was not independently confirmed. The previously documented discrepancy between the English and Thai pages has been corrected, and the Japanese and Russian pages now also agree with the 2027 date.

The October 2026 date has passed. The available page content does not explain whether the November 2027 date reflects a renewal, a corrected translation, or a different authorization period.

None of these website statements independently verifies the current telemedicine authorization with the licensing authority.

**The differing published dates and missing independent regulatory verification warrant clarification, but the older Chinese-language copy cannot by itself establish an ongoing live discrepancy.**

## Biometric Verification and Unresolved Medical Data Processing Arrangements

OG.TH's published telemedicine standard operating procedure describes extensive collection of sensitive personal information through Herb Plug Thai Traditional Medicine Clinic.[^4]

The documented workflow includes identity authentication, facial matching, medical histories, medication records, allergies, clinical assessments, and prescription records.

### Thailand-Only Hosting and External Biometric Processing

The clinic expressly states that its data is hosted within Thailand on ISO 27001-certified infrastructure.

However, the same document identifies Sumsub as its external identity-verification provider, responsible for identity authentication, liveness detection, and facial matching.

Sumsub's published service Privacy Notice permits international transfers of personal information where necessary to provide its services, subject to applicable safeguards.[^14]

These general provisions do not establish that OG.TH customer information is actually transferred outside Thailand.

Nevertheless, OG.TH does not explain whether its domestic hosting assurance extends to biometric information processed by Sumsub, its subcontractors, or associated verification infrastructure.

**Hosting the clinic's own application within Thailand does not necessarily establish that every external processing activity also occurs within Thailand.**

### Privacy Documentation Does Not Reflect Medical Operations

OG.TH's general Privacy Policy describes ordinary account information, contact details, commercial transactions, and information obtained from external marketing partners.[^15]

Its separate Data Protection Policy, last updated June 9, 2022, describes the company's business activities as centered on travel and accommodation.[^16]

Substantially similar wording appears in the privacy documentation examined in the [WEED.TH corporate investigation](../companies/weed-th-co-ltd.md).

The continued availability of this generic documentation is particularly noteworthy given OG.TH's subsequent expansion into medical consultations, biometric authentication, and prescription management.

### Unresolved Data Protection Responsibilities

The clinic's telemedicine documentation describes controlled retention schedules but does not publish their specific durations.

The operator should clarify:

- Whether its Thailand-only hosting assurance includes external biometric verification.
- Which organizations retain identity documents and facial-verification records.
- Applicable retention periods for clinical and biometric information.
- Whether medical records are excluded from general commercial marketing activities.

The available evidence does not establish unauthorized international data transfers, improper medical-data disclosure, or inaccurate security certification claims.

It establishes that the operator's specific medical-data and domestic-hosting assurances are not fully explained by its publicly available general privacy documentation.

## Relevance to Thai Visa Centre

These questions extend beyond an independent cannabis retailer.

Thai Visa Centre's official website promotes OG.TH and other cannabis-related services, while GACP's documented corporate history connects the business to the property investigated by immigration police in August 2020.

OG.TH also uses the same disputed criminal-case reference circulated by Thai Visa Centre when responding to negative customer reviews.

The business connections are documented elsewhere in this investigation.

**The unresolved issue here is whether a company commercially promoted through Thai Visa Centre is using a medical-compliance framework to market an online purchasing model that Thailand's cannabis restrictions expressly prohibit.**

The telemedicine authorization history adds another reason to seek independent regulatory documentation rather than relying exclusively on the operator's published assurances.

## Misleading Cannabis Card Claims

OG.TH also promotes [WEED.TH](weed.th.md)'s privately issued medical cannabis card, claiming legal protection and acceptance at licensed dispensaries throughout Thailand.[^6]

The card itself is not a government medical credential. Legal authorization depends on a valid medical prescription, not membership in a private cannabis discount program.[^3]

## Tripadvisor Listing

OG.TH also appears on Tripadvisor as a Bangkok attraction. A September 2026 complaint on Tripadvisor's support forum accused the business of using the attraction listing for search-engine promotion rather than representing a genuine tourist attraction.[^7][^8]
