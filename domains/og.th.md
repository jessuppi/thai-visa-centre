# og.th

`og.th` is a commercial Thai cannabis delivery website. Its published legal terms identify **OG Thai Co., Ltd.** as the operator.[^1]

## Online Cannabis Sales

OG.TH advertises cannabis ordering, online checkout, and delivery services, including nationwide shipping and same-day delivery.[^2]

However, Thailand's June 2025 cannabis restrictions prohibit online sales of cannabis flower and require valid medical prescriptions.[^3]

**The website's advertised ordering and delivery system raises questions about compliance with these restrictions.**

## Conflicting Medical Licensing Claims

OG.TH identifies **GACP Co., Ltd.** as the operator of its Herb Plug Thai Traditional Medicine Clinic and claims authorization to provide telemedicine consultations.[^4]

However, its published licensing information is inconsistent:

- **English page:** Telemedicine permission valid until November 20, 2027.
- **Thai page:** Telemedicine permission valid until October 31, 2026.

The Thai-language expiry date has already passed, while the English page continues advertising a later expiration. Neither date has been independently verified with the licensing authority.[^4][^5]

## Misleading Cannabis Card Claims

OG.TH also promotes [WEED.TH](weed.th.md)'s privately issued medical cannabis card, claiming legal protection and acceptance at licensed dispensaries throughout Thailand.[^6]

The card itself is not a government medical credential. Legal authorization depends on a valid medical prescription, not membership in a private cannabis discount program.[^3]

## Tripadvisor Listing

OG.TH also appears on Tripadvisor as a Bangkok attraction. A September 2026 complaint on Tripadvisor's support forum accused the business of using the attraction listing for search-engine promotion rather than representing a genuine tourist attraction.[^7][^8]
