# agents.co.th

`agents.co.th` presents itself as the corporate website of [Agents Co., Ltd.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md), a Thai company offering tourism technology and travel-assistance services.

The website promotes Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) submissions, eSIM distribution, and immigration reporting services. It links to [tdac.agents.co.th](tdac.agents.co.th.md) for TDAC applications and [90day.in.th](90day.in.th.md) for 90-day reporting services.

In a [May 2025 ASEAN NOW discussion](../threads/2025-05-01-asean-now-fake-tdac-emerges.md), [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) publicly identified both tdac.agents.co.th and [tdac.in.th](tdac.in.th.md) as websites operated by Agents Co., Ltd.

The company's website identifies **AGENTS CO., LTD.**, registered in Thailand on **December 12, 2019**, under company registration number **0115562031107**. These details are provided by the company itself and do not independently establish historical domain ownership or government affiliation.
