# thim.in.th

`thim.in.th` is a privately operated immigration-information website registered by [Agents Co., Ltd.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md), a company associated with [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md). The website uses the name and branding of Thailand's THIM immigration platform to attract travelers and promote commercial Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) submission services.[^1][^2]

## May 2026 Registration and Commercial Use of THIM Branding

According to THNIC WHOIS records, **AGENTS CO., LTD. registered `thim.in.th` on May 29, 2026**, immediately following public announcements concerning the Thailand Immigration Management System (THIM). The platform was **reportedly developed by Digital Identity Co., Ltd., a private technology company**, in partnership with Thailand's Immigration Bureau, using infrastructure supplied by Amazon Web Services (AWS). These arrangements were publicized in a May 28, 2026 announcement, although the development contract, procurement process, and precise division of responsibilities have not been independently verified.[^1][^3]

THIM's published Privacy Notice identifies the Immigration Bureau as the data controller and Digital Identity Co., Ltd. as the application developer and technical service provider. It also identifies KYCNow Co., Ltd. as a third-party processor involved in biometric and document verification. These disclosures indicate substantial private-sector participation in a system handling travelers' passport information, identity records, and potentially sensitive biometric data. However, the available materials do not independently establish the contractual terms, ownership arrangements, financial beneficiaries, or extent of private-sector access to immigration records.[^3][^4]

The independently operated `thim.in.th` website reproduces information about THIM, displays application imagery, and publishes government announcements concerning the platform. However, its prominent **"Apply for TDAC"** button directs visitors to `agents.co.th/tdac-apply`, a commercial submission service operated by AGENTS CO., LTD. The website promotes the company's submission assistance and live support, even though travelers can complete the underlying TDAC procedure through Thailand's free immigration registration system.[^2][^5]

The website acknowledges that it is not affiliated with the Thai government and provides a separate link to the official TDAC portal. Nevertheless, its use of the THIM name, application imagery, and immigration guidance creates a commercial entry point for travelers researching the immigration platform. The website also embeds Thai Visa Centre video content and displays immigration discussions involving an account identified as **"THAI VISA CENTRE - visa agent"** with an administrator designation.[^2]

**The relationship between the private businesses involved warrants closer scrutiny.** Digital Identity Co., Ltd. has publicly been identified as THIM's developer, while AGENTS CO., LTD. independently registered a domain matching the platform's name and used it to promote its own immigration services. The reviewed records do not establish whether these companies have any corporate, contractual, financial, or operational relationship. Nor do they establish whether the THIM development arrangement was competitively awarded, whether conflicts of interest were assessed, or what safeguards govern commercial involvement in immigration technology.[^1][^3][^4]

**The central concern is the convergence of private technology development, immigration administration, and commercial services marketed to foreign travelers.** The available evidence documents claims of private-sector involvement in THIM's development and AGENTS CO., LTD.'s separate use of the platform's name to attract visitors toward paid immigration assistance. It does not establish corruption, improper procurement, unlawful government impersonation, or coordination between the companies.

Nevertheless, the absence of independently verified procurement details and the broader commercialization of immigration-related services justify further investigation into **contract transparency, corporate relationships, data access, financial interests, and potential conflicts of interest**.

## Commercial Collection and Marketing Use of Immigration Data

The commercial implications of `thim.in.th` extend beyond its use of government immigration branding.

The website's prominent TDAC application button directs travelers to an AGENTS CO., LTD. submission form that requests extensive personal information, including passport numbers, dates of birth, telephone numbers, occupations, travel itineraries, accommodation addresses, and health-declaration information.[^6]

Although the submission form identifies itself as a private agency service, travelers using it disclose their immigration information to a commercial intermediary rather than directly to Thailand's Immigration Bureau.

### Personal Information Used for Affiliated Business Marketing

The Privacy Policy linked from `thim.in.th` expressly identifies the marketing of AGENTS' own services and those of its sister companies as purposes for processing personal information.[^7]

The policy also permits sharing personal information with companies under common ownership or control for marketing purposes.

AGENTS' separate Data Protection Policy, last updated April 2, 2025, explicitly reiterates this exception to its general restrictions on disclosing personal information to third parties.[^8]

Neither document identifies the specific sister companies eligible to receive this information or clearly distinguishes immigration application records from ordinary customer contact information for marketing purposes.

### Contrast With Official Immigration Data Processing

Thailand's Immigration Bureau states in its official TDAC FAQ that information submitted through its system is restricted to relevant immigration and border-security authorities and used for government border-control purposes.[^5]

The commercial intermediary's published policies establish substantially broader potential uses of personal information.

**Travelers completing the same immigration requirement through AGENTS are therefore subject to a different data-processing arrangement that expressly accommodates commercial marketing and affiliated-company information sharing.**

The Immigration Bureau acknowledges that authorized travel agencies may submit TDAC information on behalf of customers. However, authorization to submit an arrival card does not automatically establish authorization to use the underlying personal information for unrelated commercial purposes.

### Relevance to Historical Customer-Data Complaints

These provisions are particularly relevant to the previously documented [May 2022 customer complaint](../threads/2022-05-04-reddit-extending-visa-thai-visa-centre.md) involving a traveler who reported receiving Thai Visa Centre promotional emails referencing an earlier quarantine accommodation booking through `aq.in.th`.

The current policies do not establish the source of historical mailing lists or retrospectively establish consent for earlier marketing activities.

Nevertheless, they explicitly document a commercial framework permitting affiliated-company marketing using personal information collected through AGENTS' services.

The available evidence does not establish that passport numbers, health declarations, or other immigration records have actually been transferred to affiliated businesses for marketing purposes.

The unresolved questions concern which companies receive customer information, which categories of information are shared, how long immigration records are retained, and how travelers authorize or decline secondary commercial uses.

**The distinction between mandatory government immigration reporting and optional commercial data processing should be prominently communicated before travelers disclose their personal information.**

## Discussion-Based Customer Acquisition and Commercial Referrals

As examined on November 26, 2026, `thim.in.th` operates a public discussion platform alongside its guide to Thailand's official immigration application.

The website displayed 285 comments, including questions concerning passport verification, application errors, accommodation information, and arrival-card submissions.[^9]

However, visitors wishing to participate are instructed:

> Click comment and we will ask for your email, then OTP, then name.

Published comments carry email-verification labels and individual account identifiers.

**The website therefore provides AGENTS CO., LTD. with an additional mechanism for collecting verified contact information from travelers who are not necessarily purchasing its immigration services.**

### Integration With Existing Commercial Communities

The website also incorporates discussions from [asq.in.th](asq.in.th.md) and actively encourages visitors to join both Facebook visa-advice communities controlled by Thai Visa Centre.[^9]

This arrangement connects visitors researching a government immigration application with the agency's established commercial community network.

It is particularly relevant to the June 2023 disclosures concerning email-address collection following Thai Visa Centre's acquisition of an established Facebook visa-advice community.

### Commercial Assistance Prioritized Over Official App Downloads

Although `thim.in.th` reproduces official application screenshots and identifies Google Play and Apple's App Store among its sources, the examined page did not contain direct links to either official application listing.

Its prominent application button instead directs visitors to AGENTS' private TDAC submission service.

The official Android application is published under the Royal Thai Police developer account and explicitly identified as a government application.[^10]

The Tourism Authority of Thailand separately confirmed in June 2026 that THIM was optional and that travelers could continue using the existing government TDAC website.[^11]

The private website acknowledges both its unofficial status and the availability of the government TDAC portal. Nevertheless, its navigation prioritizes commercial assistance over direct access to the application whose name it uses.

### Personal Data Processing Responsibilities

The Privacy Policy linked from the website is AGENTS' general TDAC service policy, rather than a separate policy explaining the operation of the THIM discussion platform.[^7]

It does not specifically explain whether verified commenter email addresses are retained separately from immigration customer records or excluded from affiliated-company marketing.

The operator should clarify how discussion accounts are managed, whether their contact information is incorporated into shared customer databases, and how users can decline commercial communications without losing access to community discussions.
