# dailynews.co.th

`dailynews.co.th` is the online publication of **Daily News** (เดลินิวส์), a major Thai-language newspaper published by [Si Phya Publishing Co., Ltd.](../companies/si-phya-publishing-co-ltd.md). Its first newspaper issue appeared on March 28, 1964.[^1]

## April 2025: Private TDAC Website Presented as an Official Registration Channel

On April 12, 2025, Daily News published an article concerning immigration arrangements at the Second Thai–Lao Friendship Bridge in Mukdahan during the Songkran holiday.[^2]

The article attributed information to immigration authorities identifying two websites through which foreign nationals should register their Thailand Digital Arrival Cards:

- `tdac.immigration.go.th`
- `tdac.in.th/th`

The Thai text uses the word **เท่านั้น** (only), presenting these two websites as the designated registration channels.

However, the second website is a privately operated immigration-service platform associated with [Agents Co., Ltd.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md) and [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md).

The article does not distinguish the commercial operator from the government's official registration system.

## March 2026: Daily News Publishes Contradictory Immigration Warnings

On March 29, 2026, Daily News published a warning concerning unofficial TDAC websites and commercial services charging travelers for registration.[^3]

The article attributed the warning to Immigration Bureau deputy commissioner and spokesperson [Choengron Rimpadee](../people/choengron-rimpadee.md).

Unlike the April 2025 publication, the March 2026 article identifies only the Immigration Bureau's official TDAC portal as the registration website, while also directing travelers to the bureau's main government website for official links.

The newspaper additionally identifies several privately operated TDAC platforms as examples of unofficial commercial services.

This creates a documented contradiction between two publications from the same newspaper.

## November 2026: Earlier Information Remains Accessible

An examination on November 25, 2026 confirmed that the original April 2025 article remains publicly accessible and continues displaying both registration links.[^2]

The private link now redirects visitors to `tdac.agents.co.th/th`, where the operator explicitly acknowledges that it is a commercial intermediary rather than Thailand's Immigration Bureau.[^4]

The government's own TDAC guidance identifies `tdac.immigration.go.th` as its official submission portal.[^5]

**Daily News continues directing readers toward a private immigration service through an earlier publication that contradicts its own subsequent reporting about unofficial TDAC websites.**

No accompanying correction or clarification was identified in the retrieved April 2025 article.

The available evidence does not establish whether the incorrect link originated with immigration officials, a reporter, an editor, or another source. Nor does it establish that Daily News received compensation for including the commercial website.

## Historical Coverage of Thai Visa Centre

The broader investigation also preserves Daily News's August 2020 reporting concerning the police raid involving Thai Visa Centre.[^6]

Jesse Nickles alleges that the original coverage was subsequently removed from the newspaper's website. The circumstances and motivation surrounding its reported removal have not been independently established.

These historical records warrant further examination of the publisher's handling of immigration-related information, without presuming a commercial relationship or deliberate editorial misconduct.
