# weed.th

`weed.th` is a Thai cannabis marketplace operated by [WEED.TH Co., Ltd.](../companies/weed-th-co-ltd.md), incorporated on February 16, 2023.[^1]

The website advertises cannabis dispensaries, delivery services, and a privately operated medical cannabis card program.[^2]

## Medical Cannabis Card

WEED.TH promotes a **Digital Medicinal Cannabis Card**, marketed alongside medical consultations, prescriptions, and dispensary discounts.[^3]

However, **the card is not an official Thai medical credential and does not independently authorize cannabis purchases or possession**. WEED.TH acknowledges that the card is optional and cannot replace the legally required P.T.33 medical prescription.[^3][^4]

The associated cannabis delivery website `og.th` nevertheless advertises the card as providing legal protection and facilitating compliant cannabis purchases.[^5]

These claims risk misleading customers about the legal value of a privately issued membership card.

## Customer Data

The application process involves collecting personal and medical information, including questionnaire responses and video submissions.[^6]

WEED.TH's published privacy policies describe information sharing with service providers and marketing partners, raising additional questions about how sensitive customer information is handled.[^7]

## Online Cannabis Ordering Despite Thailand's Electronic-Sales Ban

WEED.TH presents its medical cannabis card and P.T.33 prescription system as evidence that its commercial services comply with Thailand's cannabis regulations.

However, the company's own websites continue advertising electronic cannabis ordering and delivery arrangements despite the government's prohibition on selling controlled cannabis products through online platforms.

### The Company Explicitly Advertises Delivery Orders

WEED.TH's medical-card homepage tells customers that participating dispensaries offer discounts and that cardholders can order delivery services directly through its platform.[^8]

Its dispensary partnership page is more explicit.

Under the question **"How can I sell products on WEED.TH?"**, the company describes a secure delivery-request system allowing participating shops to accept or reject customer orders.[^9]

WEED.TH explains that the system was developed specifically to support delivery requests, verify customers and dispensaries, and protect merchants against fraudulent or underage orders.

The company states that it does not charge commissions or markups on these transactions.

**These descriptions establish that WEED.TH advertises more than a passive dispensary directory or medical-prescription service. It promotes an electronic ordering system connecting cannabis customers with retailers.**

### A Separate Electronic Wholesale Platform

WEED.TH also operates `erx.weed.th`, which markets integrated software for cannabis dispensaries, including inventory management, checkout, customer-facing LINE menus, and wholesale supply access.[^10]

The platform explicitly advertises ordering from vetted Thai wholesale suppliers directly inside its management console, with shop credit tracked electronically.

It also offers public product menus synchronized with dispensary inventories.

The published description does not identify every product available through its wholesale-ordering system. It therefore does not independently establish that controlled cannabis flower is being sold through that feature.

Nevertheless, the platform's advertised capabilities raise an additional question about how WEED.TH distinguishes permissible dispensary-management software from prohibited electronic cannabis sales.

### The Government Prohibits Electronic Cannabis Sales

Thailand's Ministry of Public Health tightened cannabis regulations in June 2025, restricting cannabis flower to medical use and requiring prescriptions from authorized practitioners.

The restrictions also prohibit commercial sales of controlled cannabis products through electronic channels or computer networks.[^11]

The Tourism Authority of Thailand's government-published guidance separately confirms that online cannabis sales and commercial advertising are prohibited.[^12]

**A medical prescription addresses whether a patient is eligible to receive cannabis. It does not automatically authorize an otherwise prohibited online-sales method.**

WEED.TH's emphasis on prescriptions, customer verification, and licensed dispensaries therefore does not resolve the separate legal question raised by its advertised electronic ordering services.

### Rebranding Orders as Delivery Requests

The central inconsistency is WEED.TH's description of a system in which customers submit electronic requests and dispensaries accept or reject the resulting orders.

Calling those transactions *delivery requests* does not, by itself, explain why they would fall outside the prohibition on electronic cannabis sales.

The publicly accessible descriptions do not establish where payments are completed, when ownership of cannabis transfers, or whether the advertised systems are currently processing controlled cannabis transactions.

Those details are necessary before concluding that any particular sale violates Thai law.

**What is established is that WEED.TH publicly promotes cannabis delivery ordering while simultaneously presenting its medical-documentation system as evidence of regulatory compliance.**

The company has not clearly explained how its customer delivery workflow complies with the electronic-sales restrictions, or whether the wholesale platform excludes controlled cannabis products.

This distinction matters because the same business network also operates OG.TH, whose online cannabis checkout and medical-treatment representations raise comparable questions.

WEED.TH's published ordering functionality demonstrates that those questions extend beyond a single cannabis storefront and into the wider commercial infrastructure associated with Thai Visa Centre.
