# THAI.NEWS

**THAI.NEWS** (`thai.news`) presents itself as an independent Thailand news website but has repeatedly published promotional coverage of [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) and its associated business, [Agents Co., Ltd.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md).

## Thai Visa Centre Promotion

On October 21, 2023, THAI.NEWS published *How to select a Trustworthy Visa Agency in Thailand*. Rather than comparing agencies, the article exclusively promoted Thai Visa Centre, praising its alleged 17 years of experience, customer reviews, and immigration expertise.

Published under the generic byline "Editorial Team," the article supplied the agency's contact information and repeated favorable business claims without independent verification or an identifiable advertising disclosure.[^1]

## Contradictory TDAC Scam Warnings

On May 1, 2025, THAI.NEWS published an article condemning websites charging approximately **$10 for Thailand's free Digital Arrival Card (TDAC)**, describing such services as scams.

Remarkably, the same article explicitly defended websites operated by AGENTS CO., LTD., claiming they were legitimate and recommending Thai `.co.th` and `.in.th` domains as indicators of trustworthiness.[^2]

Just eight days later, on May 9, THAI.NEWS published a promotional announcement for AGENTS CO., LTD.'s own TDAC platform, advertising **$8 early-submission fees** and labeling its privately operated websites "Official TDAC form" and "Official TDAC information page."[^3]

On July 28, the publisher again called the company's service the **"official TDAC AGENTS portal"**, promoting additional insurance, eSIM, airport transportation, and VIP immigration services.[^4]

Thailand's Immigration Bureau provides TDAC registration free through `tdac.immigration.go.th`. AGENTS' paid early-submission service schedules applications for later government submission; it does not provide earlier government processing.[^6]

The distinction between advance scheduling and charging for ordinary submission matters. Nevertheless, THAI.NEWS's aggressive warnings against paid intermediaries contrast sharply with its favorable treatment of AGENTS' commercial alternative.

## Commercial Cross-Promotion

On October 18, 2025, THAI.NEWS published another promotional article advertising AGENTS CO., LTD.'s paid 90-day immigration reporting service through `90day.in.th`, with prices starting at 375 baht.[^5]

The news website's navigation also links directly to `90day.in.th`, `tdac.in.th`, `asq.in.th`, and `dtv.in.th`, websites associated with the broader immigration-service network.

THAI.NEWS condemns competing paid TDAC services while repeatedly promoting AGENTS CO., LTD.'s own paid alternative. Its coverage applies a blatant double standard, attacking competitors as scammers while presenting closely related commercial services as trustworthy and official.

## Editorial Transparency and Commercial Conflicts of Interest

THAI.NEWS repeatedly presents immigration-related commercial promotions in the format of ordinary news articles.

Its documented coverage includes recommendations of Thai Visa Centre, promotion of AGENTS CO., LTD.'s commercial TDAC platform, and advertisements for paid 90-day immigration reporting.[^1][^3][^4][^5]

These publications concern businesses within the same commercial network, rather than an unrelated selection of immigration-service providers.

### Commercial Recommendations Across Multiple Immigration Procedures

The documented publications establish a recurring promotional pattern:

| Publication date | Commercial service promoted |
| --- | --- |
| October 21, 2023 | Thai Visa Centre's visa assistance |
| May 1, 2025 | AGENTS-operated TDAC websites |
| May 9, 2025 | Paid advance TDAC submissions |
| July 28, 2025 | TDAC and associated travel services |
| October 18, 2025 | Paid 90-day immigration reporting |

The publisher's navigation also directs readers toward immigration-related websites operated by the same business network.

### Distinguishing Official Services From Private Intermediaries

The publisher's descriptions of commercially operated TDAC websites as official resources are particularly consequential because readers may encounter those recommendations while attempting to locate mandatory government immigration procedures.

Thailand's Immigration Bureau provides its official TDAC registration service free of charge.[^6]

The repeated promotion of privately operated alternatives through ostensibly informational publications creates uncertainty concerning whether those recommendations represent independent editorial assessments or commercial marketing.

### Undisclosed Commercial Arrangements

The available publications do not establish whether Thai Visa Centre or AGENTS CO., LTD. paid THAI.NEWS for individual articles, whether the publisher receives referral commissions, or whether the businesses share corporate ownership.

Nevertheless, the recurring promotional coverage and direct links to affiliated immigration services warrant examination of these relationships.

**Readers should be able to distinguish independently researched immigration news from promotional content supporting the commercial interests of the businesses being recommended.**

The documented publishing pattern establishes a basis for examining editorial independence without requiring an unverified conclusion about the publisher's legal ownership.

## Corporate Press-Release Material Presented as Editorial Coverage

A comparison of THAI.NEWS's May 9, 2025 article and The Thaiger's June 13, 2025 press release concerning AGENTS CO., LTD. reveals substantially overlapping commercial messaging.[^3][^7]

Both publications repeat the following claims:

- More than 99% of customers received their TDACs without delay during the May 7 government website disruption.
- Applications are typically processed within one to five minutes.
- Draft applications remain available indefinitely unless deleted.
- AGENTS complies fully with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).
- Thai commercial domain extensions provide reassurance concerning provider legitimacy.

Neither publication identifies independent supporting records for the reported performance statistics or a regulatory assessment establishing the company's claimed PDPA compliance.

### Different Editorial Disclosures

The Thaiger explicitly identifies its publication as a press release at the end of the article.

THAI.NEWS instead publishes substantially similar commercial claims under its generic Editorial Team byline, without an identifiable press-release or sponsorship disclosure in the reviewed version.

The publication dates establish that THAI.NEWS distributed this promotional messaging more than a month before The Thaiger.

### Implications for Independent Verification

The similarities are significant because favorable coverage across multiple news websites could create an impression of independent corroboration.

However, repetition of substantially identical corporate claims does not constitute independent verification of those claims.

The available evidence does not establish which organization originally prepared the promotional material, whether THAI.NEWS received compensation, or whether the publishers coordinated their coverage.

Nevertheless, the overlapping messaging and different editorial disclosures provide further grounds for questioning the independence of THAI.NEWS's coverage of AGENTS CO., LTD.
