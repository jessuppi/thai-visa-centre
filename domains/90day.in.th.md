# 90day.in.th

In a [May 2026 Reddit discussion](../threads/2026-05-reddit-thailand-90-day-online-reporting.md), [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) publicly acknowledged providing paid 90-day immigration reporting services through `90day.in.th`.[^1][^2]

The agency described fees of 375–500 baht for in-person submissions and document delivery. The website identifies [Agents Co., Ltd.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md) as the service operator.[^1][^2][^3]

The company's [agents.co.th](agents.co.th.md) website also lists `90day.in.th` among its immigration-related services, providing a separate public acknowledgment of the connection.[^3]

The discussion also identified `tm47.immigration.go.th` as Thailand's official government portal for 90-day reporting, distinguishing it from the privately operated paid service.[^1][^2]

## Formal THNIC Abuse Complaint (November 2026)

On November 12, 2026, Jesse Nickles submitted a formal complaint to [THNIC](../brands/thnic.md) concerning the publication of disputed criminal allegations on `https://90day.in.th/scam`. The website identifies [Agents Co., Ltd.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md), registration number 0115562031107, as its operator.[^4]

Nickles reported that the page accused him of operating fraudulent online accounts and conducting harassment campaigns, while asserting that an active criminal arrest warrant existed against him. The page also directed readers to a separate publication on [tvc.co.th](tvc.co.th.md) containing purported criminal-case documentation. Nickles disputed these allegations and raised concerns about the accuracy and authenticity of the associated claims and documents.[^4]

**The complaint specifically requested an independent investigation into whether `90day.in.th` was being used in bad faith under Sections 12.4, 12.5, and 14.1.4.2 of THNIC's Domain Name Registration Policy.** Nickles emphasized that he was requesting an assessment of domain use rather than asking the registrar to adjudicate the underlying criminal accusations.[^4]

Nickles also requested that THNIC register the matter as a separate complaint from his existing dispute concerning `tvc.co.th`, identify whether the domain holder had been notified, explain which enforcement provisions applied, preserve the complaint records, and provide an independent case reference and written determination.[^4]

The complaint extended the registrar's examination beyond `tvc.co.th` to a separately registered domain allegedly distributing the same disputed criminal narrative. It raised the question of whether THNIC would independently assess potentially abusive domain use across multiple websites associated with the same business network.[^4]
