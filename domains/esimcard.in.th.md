# esimcard.in.th

`esimcard.in.th` is a commercial retail telecommunications website operated by [Agents Co., Ltd.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md) and explicitly associated with [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md).[^1][^2]

The website identifies AGENTS CO., LTD. as its operator and publishes the company's travel-business licence number, 11/10602.

## February 2023 Commercial Promotion

On February 7, 2023, Thai Visa Centre used its designated Forum Sponsor account on ASEAN NOW to advertise discounted DTAC eSIM products through `esimcard.in.th`.[^2]

The agency advertised a price of 180 baht using the promotional code `THAIVISACENTRE` and claimed to have a special commercial arrangement.

Forum participants **isaanistical** and **SuperSilverHaze** objected to the advertising. Following a complaint that the discount code did not work, Thai Visa Centre corrected the code and explained that the discount was ordinarily offered to its existing clients.

**The exchange establishes that Thai Visa Centre was commercially promoting telecommunications products to its immigration customers by February 2023.**

This predates the 2025 wholesale eSIM distribution milestone published on AGENTS' corporate website.[^3]

## Retail and Wholesale Telecommunications Operations

The retail website advertises a ten-day unlimited 5G eSIM for US$19, discounted from US$29.[^1]

The separate [esim.agents.co.th](esim.agents.co.th.md) platform targets hotels, travel agencies, tour operators, and other commercial distributors.

Together, these websites document AGENTS' expansion into both direct-to-consumer telecommunications sales and wholesale distribution.

## Customer Identification and Financial Documents

The retail website's Terms & Conditions authorize AGENTS to request government-issued photographic identification and potentially a recent bank statement when it considers a purchase to present an elevated transaction risk.[^4]

Its Privacy Policy also permits collecting personal information from public databases and joint marketing partners.[^5]

These provisions demonstrate that the company's telecommunications operations may involve collecting customer information beyond what is necessary to deliver an electronic SIM card.

The published documents do not clearly distinguish payment-fraud verification from telecommunications subscriber-registration requirements.

## Commercial Claims and Regulatory Transparency

The website describes itself as Thailand's leading eSIM provider without publishing comparative sales figures or independent market-share evidence supporting that position.

It prominently displays its travel-business licence, but that licence does not itself establish authorization to operate a telecommunications service.

The contractual relationships between AGENTS, its telecommunications suppliers, and retail customers warrant clarification, particularly in light of the company's subsequent expansion into wholesale distribution.
