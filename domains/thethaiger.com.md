# thethaiger.com

`thethaiger.com` is the principal website of [The Thaiger](../brands/the-thaiger.md), operated by Singapore-registered [The Thaiger Pte. Ltd.](../companies/the-thaiger-pte-ltd.md).

Its Terms of Use identify immigration assistance, insurance, healthcare, and legal services among its third-party referral activities, while disclaiming responsibility for the services ultimately supplied by those providers.[^1]

## November 2026 Expansion Into Immigration Data Collection

The Thaiger's revised Privacy Policy, dated November 18, 2026, describes an optional mobile application feature called **My Docs**.[^2]

The feature stores account-associated information on the publisher's server at `api-app.thethaiger.com`, including:

- Visa categories and nationality.
- Immigration reporting and authorized-stay deadlines.
- Arrival-card and re-entry permit dates.
- Insurance coverage details and renewal dates.
- Preferences concerning commercial partner offers.

The publisher states that passport and identity-document numbers are excluded from server-side collection and that its separate document vault operates locally on the user's device.

## Commercial Offers Based on Immigration Deadlines

The revised policy explicitly describes displaying partner promotions when tracked obligations become due, provided users have opted into those offers.

The publisher also reserves the ability to share anonymized aggregate statistics concerning visa categories with commercial partners.[^2]

**The application therefore creates a commercial opportunity to reach prospective immigration customers at the time they need reporting, extension, or renewal assistance.**

This is particularly relevant given The Thaiger's documented historical promotion of Thai Visa Centre and AGENTS CO., LTD.

However, the Privacy Policy does not establish that either company participates in the My Docs referral program.

## Inconsistent Account-Linkage Explanations

Section 11 describes My Docs information as stored under a user's account but subsequently states that the information cannot be located using the user's name or email address.

Section 11.1 identifies the account information as including an email address and first name.

Furthermore, the publisher's separate account-deletion page provides an email-based procedure for requesting deletion of the account and its associated My Docs information.[^2][^3]

These explanations warrant clarification concerning how immigration records are associated with account identities and how users can exercise their data-protection rights.

## Commercial Accountability

The revised policy includes several privacy safeguards, including optional partner marketing, user-controlled deletion, and automatic removal of inactive My Docs records.

Nevertheless, the expansion raises questions concerning the selection of immigration-service partners, potential referral compensation, and the separation of editorial operations from commercial customer acquisition.

The available documentation does not establish unauthorized disclosure of immigration information or an unlawful commercial arrangement.

It does establish that The Thaiger has expanded from publishing immigration information into collecting immigration-related records and facilitating deadline-based commercial promotions.
