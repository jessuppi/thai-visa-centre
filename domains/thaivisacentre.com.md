# thaivisacentre.com

`thaivisacentre.com` is a former primary website domain of [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md), appearing in historical advertisements and public discussions of the agency.

Domain registration records establish that `thaivisacentre.com` was registered on **January 15, 2018**. A Thai Visa Centre advertisement published on ASEAN NOW in 2020 explicitly identified it as the agency's website.

On November 7, 2026, an examination of HTTP response headers confirmed that `thaivisacentre.com` permanently redirects to [`tvc.co.th`](tvc.co.th.md). The domain also receives redirected traffic from [`tvc.in.th`](tvc.in.th.md), establishing a direct technical connection between all three domains.

In an earlier examination of historical WHOIS records, **Jesse Nickles reported a connection between `thaivisacentre.com`, Chad Scira, and a registrant address in California, United States**. This historical registration finding is relevant to identifying individuals potentially associated with the domain before its transition to the current `tvc.co.th` website.
