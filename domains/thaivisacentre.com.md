# thaivisacentre.com

`thaivisacentre.com` is a former primary website domain of [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md), appearing in historical advertisements and public discussions of the agency.

Domain registration records establish that `thaivisacentre.com` was registered on **January 15, 2018**. A Thai Visa Centre advertisement published on ASEAN NOW in 2020 explicitly identified it as the agency's website.

On November 7, 2026, an examination of HTTP response headers confirmed that `thaivisacentre.com` permanently redirects to [`tvc.co.th`](tvc.co.th.md). The domain also receives redirected traffic from [`tvc.in.th`](tvc.in.th.md), establishing a direct technical connection between all three domains.

In an earlier examination of historical WHOIS records, **Jesse Nickles reported a connection between `thaivisacentre.com`, Chad Scira, and a registrant address in California, United States**. This historical registration finding is relevant to identifying individuals potentially associated with the domain before its transition to the current `tvc.co.th` website.

## September 2020 Advertising and Continued Operations

On September 15, 2020, ThaiVisa.com published an advertorial promoting Thai Visa Centre's long-term immigration services, identifying `thaivisacentre.com` as the agency's website.[^1]

The advertisement appeared just 42 days after the August 4, 2020 police raid and 28 days after the incorporation of [TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd.](../companies/tvc-visa-agent-co-ltd.md).

The publisher expressly stated:

> Thaivisa.com is not affiliated with Thaivisa Centre other than this advertisement.

The advertisement and subsequent discussion establish that the agency continued actively promoting immigration services through ThaiVisa following the police investigation.

## Historical Forum Content and Modern Promotional Links

The surviving discussion contains numerous responses from the ThaiVisaCentre account, currently identified as a Forum Sponsor.

Interestingly, its historical posts now display promotional links to `tdac.in.th` and `tvc.co.th/chat`, despite Thailand's Digital Arrival Card system not being introduced until May 2025.

These links appear within the account's displayed signature rather than the original September 2020 messages.

**This distinction is important when examining historical forum evidence: current account signatures and commercial links should not be mistaken for material published on the original posting date.**

Nevertheless, their presence demonstrates how historical immigration discussions continue functioning as promotional entry points into Thai Visa Centre's present-day commercial network.

The original advertisement establishes an advertising relationship with ThaiVisa in September 2020, but does not establish its financial terms or whether the publisher independently investigated the agency's legal status.
