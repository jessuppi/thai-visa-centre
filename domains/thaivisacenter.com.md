# thaivisacenter.com

`thaivisacenter.com` is an active website advertising Thai visa, work permit, and company registration services under the name **Thai Visa Center**, using the American spelling of "Center."

The website identifies **SAWADEE (2497) CO., LTD.** in its footer and maintains its own branding, contact information, and service pages.

It should not be confused with [`thaivisacentre.com`](thaivisacentre.com.md), which uses the British spelling and was previously the primary website of [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md).

On November 7, 2026, an examination confirmed that `thaivisacenter.com` remained independently accessible, returning HTTP 200, while `thaivisacentre.com` redirected permanently to [`tvc.co.th`](tvc.co.th.md).

The distinction is significant when examining Thai Visa Centre's historical claims about its years in business. The existence and history of the earlier **Thai Visa Center** website should not automatically be attributed to the similarly named **Thai Visa Centre** operation.
