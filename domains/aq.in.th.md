# aq.in.th

Thai Visa Centre has publicly acknowledged operating `aq.in.th` during the COVID-19 pandemic, when the website advertised expedited Thailand Pass applications and quarantine hotel bookings. The agency identifies AGENTS CO., LTD. as its digital arm responsible for developing ASQ hotel-booking systems. In early 2022, Reddit discussions questioned the website's misleading government-like presentation, undisclosed commercial interests, and favorable customer testimonials of uncertain authenticity.
