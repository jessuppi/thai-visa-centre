# tvc.co.th

`tvc.co.th` is a public-facing website for [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md), which advertises Thai visa and immigration-assistance services.

## Domain Registration and Corporate Identity

In written correspondence with Jesse Nickles, [Suthida Tepaloon](../people/suthida-tepaloon.md) of [THNIC](../brands/thnic.md) stated that `tvc.co.th` complied with its registration policy because the domain matched the legally registered company name [TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd.](../companies/tvc-visa-agent-co-ltd.md). However, the domain uses only the abbreviation "TVC," which THNIC's [published policies](https://thnic.or.th/doc/thPolicy-EN-MAR2024.pdf) permit, rather than the company's full registered name.

Despite this corporate registration, the website publicly operates under the trade name **Thai Visa Centre**, for which no corresponding Thai company registration has been identified.

On its own `/scam` page, the agency states that it also operated [aq.in.th](aq.in.th.md) and [tp.in.th](tp.in.th.md) during the COVID-19 pandemic. This is a public acknowledgment by the agency, not independent verification of the domains' historical registrants or legal ownership.
