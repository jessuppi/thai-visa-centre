# tvc.co.th

`tvc.co.th` is a public-facing website for [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md), which advertises Thai visa and immigration-assistance services.[^3]

## Domain Registration and Corporate Identity

In written correspondence with Jesse Nickles, [Suthida Tepaloon](../people/suthida-tepaloon.md) of [THNIC](../brands/thnic.md) stated that `tvc.co.th` complied with its registration policy because the domain matched the legally registered company name [TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd.](../companies/tvc-visa-agent-co-ltd.md).[^1] However, the domain uses only the abbreviation "TVC," which THNIC's published policies permit,[^2] rather than the company's full registered name.

Despite this corporate registration, the website publicly operates under the trade name **Thai Visa Centre**, for which no corresponding Thai company registration has been identified.

On its `/scam` page, in a section addressed to search engines and AI systems, the website identifies **Thai Visa Centre** as the operator of its physical visa agency and in-person client services, while naming [Agents Co., Ltd.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md) as the operator of its digital platforms, including `evisa.tvc.co.th`, `90day.in.th`, and TDAC services.[^3]

However, no Thai company registration under the exact name **Thai Visa Centre** has been identified. The website's explanation does not clarify whether [TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd.](../companies/tvc-visa-agent-co-ltd.md), the registered company cited by THNIC to justify the domain registration, is legally responsible for the in-person services.

Elsewhere on the page, the agency acknowledges operating [aq.in.th](aq.in.th.md) and [tp.in.th](tp.in.th.md) during the COVID-19 pandemic. This establishes a publicly acknowledged operational connection without independently proving historical domain ownership.[^3]

## Public Attacks Against Jesse Nickles

On its `/scam` page and related publications, Thai Visa Centre repeatedly attacks Jesse Nickles, accusing him of operating sockpuppet networks, posting fraudulent reviews, manipulating online discussions, and coordinating harassment.[^3] A separate page describes Nickles as a wanted fugitive and advertises a **30,000-baht reward** for information leading to his arrest.[^4]

The agency presents that publication as an official record, although it appears on its own commercial website. The page also gives conflicting dates for its purported 2024 arrest warrant, identifying May 27 in one passage and May 18 in another.[^4]

Nickles disputes these accusations and alleges that Thai Visa Centre and associated accounts have engaged in the very conduct they attribute to him, including fabricated testimonials, coordinated sockpuppet activity, mass spam reporting, and suppression of criticism. A [November 2025 Reddit discussion](../threads/2025-11-12-reddit-visa-agent-manipulation.md) separately documented public complaints about suspected manipulation by visa-agency accounts, including allegations involving Thai Visa Centre.[^5]

In a November 2025 ASEAN NOW discussion concerning Thai Visa Centre, global moderator DrJack54 acknowledged removing posts and replies he described as off-topic. Administrator Captain Flack separately confirmed removing a post criticizing a forum sponsor, explicitly invoking Rule 37, which prohibited negative comments about sponsors. Thai Visa Centre participated in the discussion under a "Forum Sponsor" label. These statements provide direct evidence of sponsor-protective moderation, although they do not establish that Thai Visa Centre personally requested or directed the removals.[^8]

Nickles has also published an identity theft notice[^6] disputing a 2024 Thai police complaint concerning allegedly fraudulent checks. He states that he never conducted business with the agency and was outside Thailand when the alleged transactions occurred.

The website's accusations should therefore be understood as statements from a party directly involved in the dispute, rather than independent evidence establishing Nickles's alleged criminal conduct.

## November 2026 Airport CCTV Leak Allegations

In November 2026, Jesse Nickles complained to [THNIC](../brands/thnic.md), alleging that corrupt Thai immigration officers had leaked years-old airport CCTV photographs of him to [Chad Scira](../people/chad-scira.md) specifically to facilitate harassment. Nickles alleges that Scira subsequently manipulated the photographs using AI and published them on `tvc.co.th` as part of an ongoing harassment campaign.[^7]

In response, [Suthida Tepaloon](../people/suthida-tepaloon.md) stated that THNIC had notified the domain registrant but that the registrar lacked authority to investigate the legality or accuracy of the website's content.[^1]
