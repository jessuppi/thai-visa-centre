# tdac.agents.co.th

In a [May 2025 ASEAN NOW discussion](../threads/2025-05-01-asean-now-fake-tdac-emerges.md), [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) publicly identified `tdac.agents.co.th` and [tdac.in.th](tdac.in.th.md) as websites operated by [Agents Co., Ltd.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md).

The company's [agents.co.th](agents.co.th.md) website also identifies `tdac.agents.co.th` as part of its Thailand Digital Arrival Card submission services.

The statement documents the agency's claimed operational relationship with the websites. It does not independently establish their legal ownership, precise launch dates, or any official government affiliation.

## Formal THNIC Domain Abuse Complaint (November 2026)

On November 12, 2026, Jesse Nickles submitted a formal abuse complaint to [THNIC](../brands/thnic.md) concerning `tdac.agents.co.th` and its parent domain, [agents.co.th](agents.co.th.md). The complaint identified [Agents Co., Ltd.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md), registration number 0115562031107, as the publicly identified operator of a private Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) submission service offering paid assistance, including an $8 (270 THB) early-submission option. Applications submitted outside the government's permitted registration window remain pending until they can be forwarded for official processing.[^1]

The complaint raised concerns about the collection of travelers' passport information, identity details, and travel information through a commercial platform associated with a mandatory government immigration procedure. Nickles specifically invoked **Section 12.11 of THNIC's Domain Name Registration Policy**, which requires domain holders to use registered domains and lower-level domains in good faith. He additionally cited Sections 12.4, 12.5, and 17 concerning registrar investigations, potential enforcement, and information-security threats.[^1]

Nickles requested that THNIC verify the registered holder's responsibility for the subdomain, examine whether its operation complied with Section 12.11, assess any information-security concerns, and consider the Immigration Bureau's public warnings about unofficial TDAC services. **He requested a formal case reference and a written policy determination**, explicitly distinguishing this complaint from his unresolved April 2025 report concerning [tdac.in.th](tdac.in.th.md).[^1]

The complaint placed the responsibility of the domain holder and its lower-level domain under direct scrutiny, rather than limiting the investigation to the original `tdac.in.th` website. It also raised the question of how THNIC evaluates commercial services collecting sensitive traveler information under its published registration policies.[^1]
