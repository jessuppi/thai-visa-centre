# dtv.in.th

`dtv.in.th` is an unofficial Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) information website associated with [Agents Co., Ltd.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md) and [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md). Its published legal policies identify AGENTS CO., LTD. as the contracting business, while the main website promotes Thai Visa Centre's immigration services.[^1][^2][^3]

## Private Visa Qualification Website and Commercial Referrals

The website publishes extensive guidance concerning Thailand's Destination Thailand Visa, including eligibility requirements, visa fees, financial documentation, and application procedures. It also reproduces government announcements and offers an **unofficial DTV qualification test**. A prominent disclaimer states that the website is not operated by the Thai government and directs applicants to the official Thai e-Visa system.[^1]

Despite its informational presentation, `dtv.in.th` also functions as a promotional channel for [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md). Its homepage displays customer testimonials, advertises a **"Trusted DTV Visa Agent"** linking to `tvc.co.th`, and makes prominent claims that the agency has served more than **100,000 expatriates** and is Thailand's leading visa agent. These marketing assertions are presented by the website itself and have not been independently substantiated.[^1]

The website's Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions identify **AGENTS CO., LTD.** as the contracting company. The Privacy Policy permits collecting names, contact details, account information, and other personal information submitted through website forms. It also states that information may be obtained from public databases and joint marketing partners, and identifies product updates, customer feedback, and testimonials among its permitted processing purposes.[^2][^3]

The Privacy Policy additionally permits sharing personal information with affiliated companies and service providers when necessary to provide services. Its affiliate-marketing provisions describe tracking purchases through referral links. The Terms & Conditions separately acknowledge that some website links may generate affiliate commissions. Neither document establishes whether the website's referral to Thai Visa Centre is commission-based or precisely how information submitted through the qualification test is subsequently used.[^2][^3]

This commercial relationship is significant because travelers researching visa eligibility may encounter a website presenting detailed immigration information alongside promotional material for a private agency. The website discloses its unofficial status, but **the corporate identity of its operator is primarily established through its legal policies rather than the prominent qualification-test and visa-information material**.[^1][^2][^3]

Thailand's Ministry of Foreign Affairs provides a separate official electronic visa system through which applicants can submit DTV applications. Thai embassies and consulates retain responsibility for assessing eligibility, requesting additional documents, and deciding whether applications are approved. The qualification test offered by `dtv.in.th` therefore has no established authority to determine an applicant's official visa eligibility.[^4]

**The central concern is the use of an immigration-information website to channel prospective applicants toward related commercial services while collecting information that may support subsequent marketing.** The available evidence establishes the website's contractual connection to AGENTS CO., LTD. and its promotion of Thai Visa Centre. It does not establish unauthorized data sharing, fraudulent visa processing, fabricated testimonials, or undisclosed commission payments.

Nevertheless, the arrangement warrants scrutiny of **operator transparency, referral relationships, customer-data handling, and the independence of immigration advice presented alongside commercial advertising**.
