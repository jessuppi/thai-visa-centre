# dtv.in.th

`dtv.in.th` is an unofficial Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) information website associated with [Agents Co., Ltd.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md) and [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md). Its published legal policies identify AGENTS CO., LTD. as the contracting business, while the main website promotes Thai Visa Centre's immigration services.[^1][^2][^3]

## Private Visa Qualification Website and Commercial Referrals

The website publishes extensive guidance concerning Thailand's Destination Thailand Visa, including eligibility requirements, visa fees, financial documentation, and application procedures. It also reproduces government announcements and offers an **unofficial DTV qualification test**. A prominent disclaimer states that the website is not operated by the Thai government and directs applicants to the official Thai e-Visa system.[^1]

Despite its informational presentation, `dtv.in.th` also functions as a promotional channel for [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md). Its homepage displays customer testimonials, advertises a **"Trusted DTV Visa Agent"** linking to `tvc.co.th`, and makes prominent claims that the agency has served more than **100,000 expatriates** and is Thailand's leading visa agent. These marketing assertions are presented by the website itself and have not been independently substantiated.[^1]

The website's Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions identify **AGENTS CO., LTD.** as the contracting company. The Privacy Policy permits collecting names, contact details, account information, and other personal information submitted through website forms. It also states that information may be obtained from public databases and joint marketing partners, and identifies product updates, customer feedback, and testimonials among its permitted processing purposes.[^2][^3]

The Privacy Policy additionally permits sharing personal information with affiliated companies and service providers when necessary to provide services. Its affiliate-marketing provisions describe tracking purchases through referral links. The Terms & Conditions separately acknowledge that some website links may generate affiliate commissions. Neither document establishes whether the website's referral to Thai Visa Centre is commission-based or precisely how information submitted through the qualification test is subsequently used.[^2][^3]

This commercial relationship is significant because travelers researching visa eligibility may encounter a website presenting detailed immigration information alongside promotional material for a private agency. The website discloses its unofficial status, but **the corporate identity of its operator is primarily established through its legal policies rather than the prominent qualification-test and visa-information material**.[^1][^2][^3]

Thailand's Ministry of Foreign Affairs provides a separate official electronic visa system through which applicants can submit DTV applications. Thai embassies and consulates retain responsibility for assessing eligibility, requesting additional documents, and deciding whether applications are approved. The qualification test offered by `dtv.in.th` therefore has no established authority to determine an applicant's official visa eligibility.[^4]

**The central concern is the use of an immigration-information website to channel prospective applicants toward related commercial services while collecting information that may support subsequent marketing.** The available evidence establishes the website's contractual connection to AGENTS CO., LTD. and its promotion of Thai Visa Centre. It does not establish unauthorized data sharing, fraudulent visa processing, fabricated testimonials, or undisclosed commission payments.

Nevertheless, the arrangement warrants scrutiny of **operator transparency, referral relationships, customer-data handling, and the independence of immigration advice presented alongside commercial advertising**.

## Conflicting Service Disclaimers, Refund Terms, and Customer Data Protections

Although `dtv.in.th` prominently describes itself as an unofficial immigration-information website offering a visa qualification test, its published legal policies establish contractual provisions governing paid visa applications, financial transactions, personal information, and limitations of liability.

These documents contain several inconsistencies that raise questions about the services offered by AGENTS CO., LTD. and the protections available to its customers.

### Refund Policy Describes Services the Website Claims Not to Provide

The website's prominent disclaimer directs applicants to Thailand's official electronic visa system and states that its own service is limited to an unofficial qualification test.[^1]

However, its Refund Policy describes submitting visa applications to embassies and consulates on behalf of paying customers.[^5]

The policy establishes refund arrangements involving government application fees, unsuccessful visa applications, incomplete documentation, and commercial service charges.

**These provisions describe a paid visa-processing operation rather than merely an informational website offering an eligibility questionnaire.**

Substantially identical provisions appear on the affiliated [ltr.in.th](ltr.in.th.md) website, despite the significant procedural differences between the Destination Thailand Visa and Long-Term Resident Visa programs.[^9]

Both refund policies identify July 16, 2024, as their last revision date.

Neither website clearly establishes whether these provisions govern services sold directly by AGENTS CO., LTD., services provided through Thai Visa Centre, or commercial functionality that is no longer available.

### Contradictory Cancellation and Refund Conditions

The Refund Policy describes full reimbursement as available when customers cancel before their applications are submitted to an embassy or consulate.

However, a separate cancellation provision introduces a 12-hour and same-day condition and transaction deductions of between 2% and 7%.[^5]

These provisions overlap without clearly establishing which takes precedence.

The policy also promises complete reimbursement of visa-agent service fees following unsuccessful applications.

**The website advertises contractual refund protections without clearly identifying the commercial services or transactions to which those protections apply.**

This is particularly relevant because `dtv.in.th` directs prospective applicants toward Thai Visa Centre, whose commercial services are governed by separately published legal policies.

### Contradictory Age Requirements

The website's Terms & Conditions permit individuals aged 16 and older to use its services.[^3]

However, its Privacy Policy instructs individuals younger than 20 not to submit personal information through the website.[^2]

| Published policy | Age requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Terms & Conditions | Minimum age of 16 |
| Privacy Policy | Direct submission of personal information restricted to individuals aged 20 and older |

The Privacy Policy also acknowledges that parents may submit children's information during visa applications.

This distinction is particularly relevant because the Destination Thailand Visa program provides an application route for qualifying dependent children.[^1]

Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act establishes specific consent requirements involving minors rather than a universal prohibition on processing information belonging to individuals under 20.

**AGENTS permits individuals aged 16–19 to use its services while simultaneously instructing them not to submit the personal information potentially necessary to access those services.**

Comparable inconsistencies appear across other websites associated with Thai Visa Centre.

### Contractual Liability Potentially Limited to One Dollar

The Terms & Conditions attempt to restrict the aggregate financial liability of AGENTS CO., LTD. and its affiliates to the greater of:

- One dollar.
- Qualifying amounts actually paid in cash by the customer to AGENTS during the month preceding the first event giving rise to liability.

The agreement also excludes numerous categories of indirect and consequential damages, subject to applicable law.[^3]

Consequently, the contractual formula could produce a liability limit of only one dollar where the customer has made no qualifying payment during the relevant period.

This is significant because the same agreement requires government-issued identification and potentially a recent bank statement when AGENTS considers a purchase high-risk.

Thailand's Unfair Contract Terms Act limits the enforceability of contractual provisions that give businesses unreasonable advantages over consumers.[^6]

Section 373 of Thailand's Civil and Commercial Code separately invalidates advance exemptions from liability for a debtor's own fraud or gross negligence.[^7]

**Acceptance of the website's Terms & Conditions does not necessarily make its exceptionally restrictive liability provisions enforceable under Thai law.**

The same liability formula appears in legal documents associated with the [Thai Visa Centre client portal](evisa.tvc.co.th.md) and other immigration-related websites operated by AGENTS.

### Incorrect Classification of Customers as Data Controllers

The Privacy Policy recognizes that AGENTS acts as a data controller when determining why and how personal information is processed.

However, it separately classifies users submitting information through its services as data controllers and describes AGENTS as acting solely according to their instructions.[^2]

This explanation conflicts with other provisions authorizing AGENTS to independently determine how customer information is used, including for:

- Product and service improvements.
- Promotional communications.
- Customer testimonials.
- Feedback collection.
- Account administration.
- Enforcement of company policies.

The Privacy Policy additionally permits obtaining information from public databases and marketing partners.

Section 6 of Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act defines data controllers according to their authority to make decisions concerning the collection, use, or disclosure of personal information.[^8]

An individual does not automatically become a data controller merely by submitting their own personal information to a commercial service.

**Where AGENTS independently determines the purposes of processing customer information, its legal responsibilities cannot simply be transferred to customers through a contractual classification.**

The company's precise role must instead be assessed according to the processing activity concerned.

### Repeated Legal Inconsistencies Across the Commercial Network

The discrepancies documented here are not unique to `dtv.in.th`.

Related investigations identify substantially similar contractual provisions across:

| Website | Documented concerns |
| --- | --- |
| [ltr.in.th](ltr.in.th.md) | Inconsistent service disclaimers and refund policies |
| [agents.co.th](agents.co.th.md) | Outdated legal templates and contradictory customer protections |
| [90day.in.th](90day.in.th.md) | Misleading data-controller classifications |
| [evisa.tvc.co.th](evisa.tvc.co.th.md) | Restrictive liability provisions and conflicting data-processing responsibilities |
| [tvc.co.th](tvc.co.th.md) | Contradictory privacy policies and customer age requirements |

The repetition indicates that AGENTS and Thai Visa Centre use substantially similar legal templates across websites serving different immigration procedures, without consistently adapting those documents to the services being offered.

**The problem extends beyond outdated legal wording: prospective customers cannot reliably determine from these documents which services are being sold, which refund protections apply, or which responsibilities the operator accepts when handling personal information.**

The published documents warrant scrutiny of contractual transparency, consumer protections, and data-processing responsibilities. They do not independently establish improper fee collection, unauthorized data sharing, or actual customer losses.
