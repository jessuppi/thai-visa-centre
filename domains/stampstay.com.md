# stampstay.com

`stampstay.com` is a commercial Thailand immigration risk-assessment website offering automated travel-history analysis, visa guidance, and paid entry-risk reports.[^1]

The website claims to have analyzed more than **500 immigration cases** to identify patterns associated with entry refusals and visa scrutiny.[^1]

## Unverified Immigration Risk Statistics

StampStay advertises a **$79 immigration risk analysis** that includes an estimated probability of entry, primary risk factors, and recommended strategies. Priority reports cost $149.[^1][^2]

Its articles also publish precise statistics about immigration refusals, including claims that **42% involve travel-pattern abuse** and **19% involve insufficient documentation**.[^3]

However, the website does not publish the underlying case records, sampling methods, or independent validation needed to assess the reliability of these figures.

The service explicitly acknowledges that it has no official access to Thai immigration authorities and cannot guarantee the accuracy of its predictions.[^4]

## Inconsistent Pricing Information

StampStay's current Terms of Service advertise reports costing **$79 or $149**, while its Legal Disclaimer still describes a **$12 Sanity Check** service.[^2][^4]

The discrepancy raises questions about whether its published legal documentation reflects the services currently being sold.

## Customer Data Collection

StampStay's Privacy Policy describes collecting names, nationalities, travel histories, employment information, and income details from paying customers.

It states that personal information is retained for 12 months, anonymized travel-pattern data indefinitely, and payment records for seven years.[^5]

## Unidentified Legal Operator, Unspecified Jurisdiction, and Conflicting Privacy Disclosures

StampStay's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, both dated March 23, 2026, identify the service by its trading name but do not identify the natural person or registered company legally responsible for its operations. The published contact details consist of a Gmail address and the website URL. Neither document specifies a registered business address or country of incorporation.[^2][^5]

### Contractual Obligations Without an Identified Jurisdiction

StampStay charges $79 for standard assessments and $149 for priority delivery. Its terms seek to limit liability to the purchase amount, exclude responsibility for immigration outcomes and consequential losses, and require customers to indemnify its operators and affiliates in specified circumstances.[^2]

Section 10 refers to the law of the jurisdiction where StampStay operates and provides for binding arbitration or proceedings in its local courts if negotiations fail. However, it never names the jurisdiction.

**Customers cannot determine from the published terms which country's laws or courts the service purports to select, or which legal person is accepting their payment and assuming contractual obligations.**

This omission does not establish that the service is unregistered or that its contractual provisions are unenforceable. It prevents prospective customers from assessing those questions using the published documents alone.

### Inconsistent Privacy Disclosures

The homepage promotes its free risk checker using the assurance **"No personal data required."**[^1] The About page nevertheless explains that the tool collects nationality, visa history, entry frequency, duration of stay, and other travel-pattern information, with an optional email address. It says risk-check records are retained for up to 12 months before anonymization.[^6]

These statements need to distinguish the absence of mandatory identifying details from the collection of information about individual travelers. Travel histories may constitute personal data when linked to an identifiable individual. The paid service expressly collects additional identifying information under its Privacy Policy.[^5]

| Subject | About page | Privacy Policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email delivery | Sender.net | Resend |
| Individual records | Assurance against third-party sharing | Discloses payment, email, storage, analytics, and analysis service providers |
| Email retention | Subscription records kept until unsubscribed | Names and email addresses retained for 12 months, then deleted |

The different email providers could serve different functions, and newsletter subscriptions could be subject to a separate retention arrangement. Likewise, processing by contracted service providers is distinct from selling data or permitting recipients to use it for their own purposes. However, the documents do not clearly reconcile these distinctions.[^5][^6]

The Privacy Policy also acknowledges possible transfers to the United States and European Union and claims to use contractual and technical safeguards for international processing. It does not identify the legal person responsible for administering these arrangements.[^5]

### GDPR Transparency and Controller Identification

StampStay expressly invokes GDPR compliance and offers privacy rights to EU and UK users.[^5][^6]

Article 13(1)(a) of the EU General Data Protection Regulation requires an applicable controller collecting personal data directly from individuals to disclose its identity and contact details, along with those of its representative where applicable.[^7]

A contact mailbox provides a means of communication but does not, by itself, identify the person or entity determining the purposes and means of processing.

Whether the EU GDPR applies depends on the circumstances of the processing, including the territorial-scope provisions in Article 3. StampStay's published commitment to GDPR principles does not independently establish its legal compliance. The lack of a clearly identified operator makes that commitment difficult to assess.

### Express Denial of Affiliation With Chad Scira

StampStay's About page contains an independence notice expressly denying affiliation with visa agents, visa agencies, and individual visa consultants, including Chad Scira. It also states that the service does not act as an intermediary or submit visa applications.[^6]

This denial is relevant to the scope of the repository's investigation and should be preserved alongside the website's other public representations.

**The reviewed documents do not establish any connection between StampStay and Thai Visa Centre or Chad Scira.** Nor does the inclusion of a specific denial provide evidence of such a connection.

The substantive concerns established here are the lack of a clearly identified legal operator, the unspecified governing jurisdiction, and unreconciled privacy disclosures. These issues matter independently of any alleged affiliation with another immigration business.
