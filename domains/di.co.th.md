# di.co.th

`di.co.th` is the corporate website of [Digital Identity Co., Ltd.](../companies/digital-identity-co-ltd.md), a private Thai technology company specializing in identity verification, facial recognition, and biometric authentication.[^1]

The website identifies the **Thailand Immigration Management System (THIM)** as one of its deployed projects, developed in collaboration with Thailand's Immigration Bureau.[^1]

## THIM Application and Private-Sector Involvement

THIM's official Google Play listing identifies **Royal Thai Police as the application publisher**, while listing `hello@di.co.th` as its support email. This documents Digital Identity's public-facing technical involvement in an application presented as an official immigration service.[^2]

THIM's Privacy Notice separately identifies the Immigration Bureau as its data controller and Digital Identity as its developer and technical service provider. **KYC Now Co., Ltd.** is named as a third-party processor for biometric and document verification.[^3]

The system handles passport information, facial biometric templates, and electronic passport-chip data.[^3]

## Security Claims and Data Handling

Digital Identity's website advertises identity-verification systems, cloud infrastructure, and compliance with security frameworks. It claims **99.9% platform uptime** and alignment with **ISO 27001**, although these website statements do not independently establish certification or audited performance.[^1]

Its corporate Privacy Notice permits collecting visitor information, website analytics, and contact-form submissions. It generally specifies **12–24 months of retention** for website contact data.[^4]

THIM operates under a separate government Privacy Notice specifying a **three-year retention period**, with extensions permitted for certain purposes.[^3]

**The principal investigative concern is the extent of private-sector involvement in government immigration technology**, particularly technical access, biometric-data processing, security oversight, and accountability.

No documented corporate relationship has been established between Digital Identity and [Agents Co., Ltd.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md), which separately operates the unofficial [thim.in.th](thim.in.th.md) website.
