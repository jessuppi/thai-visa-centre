# di.co.th

`di.co.th` is the corporate website of [Digital Identity Co., Ltd.](../companies/digital-identity-co-ltd.md), a private Thai technology company specializing in identity verification, facial recognition, and biometric authentication.[^1]

The website identifies the **Thailand Immigration Management System (THIM)** as one of its deployed projects, developed in collaboration with Thailand's Immigration Bureau.[^1]

## THIM Application and Private-Sector Involvement

THIM's official Google Play listing identifies **Royal Thai Police as the application publisher**, while listing `hello@di.co.th` as its support email. This documents Digital Identity's public-facing technical involvement in an application presented as an official immigration service.[^2]

THIM's Privacy Notice separately identifies the Immigration Bureau as its data controller and Digital Identity as its developer and technical service provider. **KYC Now Co., Ltd.** is named as a third-party processor for biometric and document verification.[^3]

The system handles passport information, facial biometric templates, and electronic passport-chip data.[^3]

## Security Claims and Data Handling

Digital Identity's website advertises identity-verification systems, cloud infrastructure, and compliance with security frameworks. It claims **99.9% platform uptime** and alignment with **ISO 27001**, although these website statements do not independently establish certification or audited performance.[^1]

Its corporate Privacy Notice permits collecting visitor information, website analytics, and contact-form submissions. It generally specifies **12–24 months of retention** for website contact data.[^4]

THIM operates under a separate government Privacy Notice specifying a **three-year retention period**, with extensions permitted for certain purposes.[^3]

**The principal investigative concern is the extent of private-sector involvement in government immigration technology**, particularly technical access, biometric-data processing, security oversight, and accountability.

No documented corporate relationship has been established between Digital Identity and [Agents Co., Ltd.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md), which separately operates the unofficial [thim.in.th](thim.in.th.md) website.

## Security Framework Alignment Versus Independent Certification

Digital Identity's corporate website advertises 99.9% platform uptime, continuous security monitoring, and alignment with ISO 27001 information-security standards.[^1]

However, alignment with an international standard is not equivalent to independently audited certification.

The International Organization for Standardization identifies ISO/IEC 27001:2022 as establishing requirements for information-security management systems, against which organizations may obtain independent certification.[^5]

Digital Identity's reviewed corporate website does not identify a certification body, certificate number, validity period, or certified organizational scope.

### AWS Infrastructure and Application-Level Responsibility

Amazon Web Services' May 28, 2026 announcement confirms that THIM uses cloud infrastructure hosted in its Bangkok region, incorporating encryption, threat detection, and centralized security-monitoring services.[^6]

These technologies provide important security capabilities. However, their deployment does not independently establish that the entire immigration application, its administrative interfaces, or its external biometric processors have undergone comprehensive security certification.

### Automated Immigration Risk Assessment

THIM's official Privacy Notice additionally acknowledges automated biometric matching, document authentication, and security risk scoring.[^3]

The notice promises human oversight, appeal mechanisms, manual intervention, and regular algorithmic auditing.

However, it does not identify the independent auditors, audit frequency, published assessment results, or measured biometric error rates.

These omissions are relevant because the application processes sensitive identity information for government immigration purposes.

### Unresolved Security Accountability

Digital Identity and the Immigration Bureau should clarify whether independent security certification covers THIM, which external processors fall within that scope, and how biometric accuracy and automated immigration risk assessments are independently evaluated.

The available evidence does not establish that THIM lacks certification, suffers from security vulnerabilities, or has experienced unauthorized disclosure of personal information.

It establishes that the reviewed public security assurances do not independently verify the extent of certification or auditing across the complete immigration platform.
