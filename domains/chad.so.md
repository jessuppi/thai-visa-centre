# chad.so

`chad.so` is the personal website of [Chad Scira](../people/chad-scira.md), an American national identified in the August 2020 Thai immigration police investigation involving [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md).

The website publishes extensive defenses of Scira's reputation, denies his involvement in Thai immigration businesses, and accuses critic Jesse Nickles of defamation and harassment.[^1]

## Denial of Thai Visa Centre Connections

Scira repeatedly claims that he has never owned, operated, or managed Thai Visa Centre, [Agents Co., Ltd.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md), or related immigration businesses. His website cites Thai Department of Business Development records showing Thai corporate shareholding as proof that he has no involvement with these companies.[^1]

However, on August 4, 2020, Immigration Bureau chief **Pol. Lt. Gen. Sompong Chingduang publicly identified Scira and his wife, Grace, as having established Thai Visa Centre**. Police announced their arrests following an investigation into suspected immigration-document forgery and cannabis cultivation. Officers reported discovering suspected forged documents and 55 government-agency rubber stamps.[^2]

The police statements were allegations made during an investigation, not findings of criminal guilt. Nevertheless, they directly contradict the website's suggestion that Scira's association with Thai Visa Centre originated entirely from accusations made by Nickles.


## Corporate Ownership Records Do Not Explain Scira's Historical Involvement

Chad Scira's website relies heavily on Thai Department of Business Development (DBD) ownership records to deny any involvement with Thai Visa Centre.

Under a heading identifying Thai Visa Centre, he reproduces a corporate ownership summary dated November 3, 2025, showing 60,000 shares, 6 million baht in capital, and 100% Thai ownership.[^1]

However, the presentation raises questions about both the company's identity and the conclusions Scira draws from those figures.

### The Company Is Not Identified

The Thai corporate register identifies **TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd.**, registration number **0115563017485**, as a company incorporated on August 18, 2020, with registered capital of 6 million baht.[^9]

That capital figure matches Scira's published summary.

Yet Scira labels his evidence **Thai Visa Centre** without supplying the underlying company's registered legal name or registration number.

The distinction matters because Thai Visa Centre is the commercial trade name, while TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd. is a separately identifiable registered legal entity.

**Scira invokes the authority of Thailand's corporate register while omitting the registration details necessary to identify precisely which company his evidence concerns.**

### The Company Was Registered After the Police Raid

The timing introduces another significant problem.

On August 4, 2020, Immigration Bureau chief Sompong Chingduang publicly identified Scira and his wife as having established Thai Visa Centre during an investigation into suspected immigration-document forgery.[^2]

TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd. was incorporated on August 18, 2020, fourteen days later.[^9]

Consequently, even if Scira's published ownership figures accurately describe that company, they cannot establish who was involved in operating Thai Visa Centre before its incorporation.

**A corporate ownership summary from 2025 does not rebut police statements concerning the operation of a commercial business in August 2020.**

### Thai Shareholding Does Not Establish Who Controlled Operations

Scira additionally argues that allegations of nominee ownership are absurd because nominee arrangements are illegal in Thailand.[^1]

But the illegality of a particular ownership arrangement does not establish that such arrangements never occur.

More importantly, the original police account did not depend on proving that Scira appeared as a registered shareholder.

The investigation concerned the operation of Thai Visa Centre and suspected immigration-document forgery.

Shareholding, management, operational involvement, and beneficial financial interests are distinct questions.

Scira's nationality-based ownership figures establish none of the latter three.

His website also fails to reconcile those denials with the subsequently documented presence of both Scira and Thai Visa Centre as administrators of the Thai Visa Advice Facebook group acquired by the agency in March 2022.[^3][^4]

### The Unanswered Question

Scira presents Thai ownership figures as if they conclusively resolve his historical connections to Thai Visa Centre.

Yet the evidence he publishes concerns an incompletely identified corporate entity and does not address the business's operations before that company's incorporation.

**The central contradiction remains: Thai immigration police publicly associated Scira with establishing Thai Visa Centre in August 2020, while Scira attempts to dismiss that association using corporate ownership figures dated more than five years later.**

Those figures do not explain the original police findings, the subsequent corporate registration, or his later administrative connection to a community acquired by Thai Visa Centre.

## Facebook Administration Contradicts Sweeping Denials

Scira's connections to Thai Visa Centre also extend beyond the 2020 police investigation.

On March 17, 2022, longtime moderator Tod Daniels publicly announced that Thai Visa Centre had acquired the **Thai Visa Advice** Facebook group.[^3]

As documented on November 6, 2026, the group's Facebook About page identified **both Chad Scira and Thai Visa Centre as administrators**.[^4]

This provides a separate, more recent connection between Scira and an online community controlled by the commercial visa agency.

**Scira's reliance on Thai corporate ownership records does not explain his administrative role in a Facebook group acquired by Thai Visa Centre.** Registered shareholding and operational involvement are separate matters, and an absence of shares does not establish an absence of involvement.

## Criminal Allegations Presented as Established Facts

Scira's website also publishes extensive allegations against Jesse Nickles, accusing him of coordinating defamation campaigns, manipulating search engines, and targeting Thai businesses.

Its *False Defamatory Content Archive* reproduces 201 purportedly removed Tripadvisor and Expat.com entries, while repeatedly characterizing Nickles as responsible for the alleged campaign.[^5]

The website cites a purported criminal complaint filed by AGENTS CO., LTD. on August 13, 2025, and references Thai police documentation as evidence supporting its accusations.[^1][^5]

However, **filing a criminal complaint does not establish that the accusations have been proven in court**. The website does not independently establish a criminal conviction supporting its broader claims.

The presentation is especially notable because Scira invokes legal action allegedly taken by AGENTS CO., LTD. while simultaneously insisting he has no involvement with that business.


## Removed Forum Posts, Sockpuppet Allegations, and Manufactured Vindication

Chad Scira's *False Defamatory Content Archive* republishes 201 entries attributed to Tripadvisor and Expat.com, presenting their removal as evidence that Jesse Nickles conducted an extensive criminal defamation campaign.[^5]

The archive repeatedly characterizes the posts as false, identifies Nickles as their author, and treats their removal as confirmation that the accusations were illegitimate.

However, Scira's presentation ignores a significant dispute over how critical posts about him and businesses associated with Thai Visa Centre have been suppressed across online communities.

### Allegations of Coordinated Sockpuppet Networks

Jesse Nickles has accused Scira of operating an extensive network of sockpuppet accounts, **primarily on Reddit and Tripadvisor**, to manipulate discussions, discredit critics, promote associated businesses, and suppress unfavorable information.

Nickles maintains that these accounts systematically report critical posts and comments as spam, harassment, or violations of platform rules, exploiting automated enforcement and routine moderation procedures to have material removed.

He further alleges that coordinated reporting activity has resulted in hundreds of removals across these platforms.

Under Nickles's account, many removals that Scira subsequently presents as victories against defamation may instead reflect successful exploitation of platform-reporting mechanisms.

**If critical posts were removed following coordinated reporting campaigns, their disappearance would not independently establish that the underlying accusations were false.**

The allegations concerning Scira's control of these accounts have not been independently established through platform records. Nevertheless, they raise an important question that his archive does not answer: who reported the disputed material, through which accounts, and why did moderators remove it?

### Reddit and Tripadvisor as Primary Targets

Nickles identifies Reddit and Tripadvisor as the principal platforms affected by the alleged sockpuppet activity.

On Reddit, the alleged conduct includes coordinated replies, manipulation of discussions about Thai immigration services, promotion of Thai Visa Centre and associated businesses, and mass-reporting of critical submissions.

On Tripadvisor, Nickles alleges that accounts associated with Scira repeatedly report unfavorable forum posts, causing their removal through platform moderation systems.

These allegations concern more than isolated disagreements between individual users. Nickles describes an organized effort to influence what information remains visible when prospective customers research Thai Visa Centre and related businesses.

The existence of coordinated accounts under Scira's control would require independent evidence, such as account-linkage records, moderation histories, or other verifiable documentation.

However, **the removal of posts from Reddit or Tripadvisor cannot itself establish whether the material was false, whether reporting was coordinated, or who controlled the accounts involved**.

Scira's archive does not resolve these questions.

### Platform Reporting Is Not a Finding of Defamation

Tripadvisor allows users and businesses to report material for suspected violations of its community guidelines.[^12]

Reddit likewise provides reporting and moderation systems through which submissions may be removed for violations of subreddit rules or sitewide policies.

Such removals can occur for reasons unrelated to the factual accuracy of a publication, including spam classifications, repetitive submissions, abusive language, or other moderation concerns.

Expat.com, whose removed posts also appear in Scira's archive, similarly reserves discretion to moderate and remove content under its community rules.[^13]

Nickles's principal sockpuppet allegations concern Reddit and Tripadvisor, rather than Expat.com. The presence of Expat.com entries in the archive does not establish that coordinated reporting occurred there.

**Scira's archive fails to distinguish between posts removed for ordinary moderation violations, posts removed following potentially manipulated reporting campaigns, and posts independently determined to contain false statements.**

Instead, it presents removal itself as confirmation of defamation.

### Self-Certified Removal Evidence

Individual archive entries identify June 14, 2026, as their removal-verification date and use labels such as `confirmed-removed-tripadvisor`.[^11]

One representative entry describes verification through the website operator's own review and search-engine de-indexing.

However, the entry does not reproduce Tripadvisor's moderation decision or identify the specific rule supposedly violated.

Nor does it provide correspondence establishing that Tripadvisor independently investigated the disputed accusations and determined them to be factually false.

This distinction is essential.

**Confirming that a webpage disappeared is not the same as establishing why it disappeared, who initiated its removal, or whether the information it contained was inaccurate.**

Scira's archive presents the former as evidence of the latter.

### Attribution to Nickles Without Independent Account Evidence

Scira also repeatedly identifies Jesse Nickles as the individual responsible for publishing the disputed material.

Yet the examined archive entries do not provide independently verifiable account-identification records establishing who controlled the original forum accounts.

Archived titles, search-engine excerpts, and removed URLs may demonstrate that particular material appeared online.

They do not independently establish the identity of the person who submitted it.

Nevertheless, Scira presents Nickles's responsibility as settled fact and describes the publications as components of a coordinated criminal campaign.

**The archive substitutes Scira's assertions about authorship for independently verifiable evidence connecting Nickles to each disputed account and publication.**

### AGENTS CO., LTD.'s Criminal Complaint Used to Reinforce the Narrative

Scira's archive repeatedly invokes an August 13, 2025 criminal complaint attributed to AGENTS CO., LTD., presenting it as supporting evidence of the alleged defamation campaign.[^10][^11]

However, filing a criminal complaint does not establish that the accusations contained within it were proven.

The examined archive entries do not provide a final court judgment establishing Nickles's criminal responsibility for the disputed publications.

There is also a notable corporate contradiction.

Scira repeatedly denies owning, managing, or operating AGENTS CO., LTD., while relying on that company's criminal complaint to substantiate accusations concerning his personal reputation.

**His defense draws upon legal action attributed to a business from which he simultaneously insists he has no operational involvement.**

The website does not adequately explain that relationship.

### Republishing the Material He Claims Was Suppressed

Scira accuses Nickles of deliberately saturating search engines and artificial-intelligence systems with damaging references to his name and associated businesses.

Yet Scira's own archive republishes disputed titles and excerpts across individually accessible webpages.

These pages preserve associations between his name, Thai Visa Centre, AGENTS CO., LTD., and other businesses while repeatedly presenting Scira's interpretation of the allegations.

The stated purpose is evidentiary preservation.

Nevertheless, the publication creates additional webpages containing the same subjects and accusations Scira claims were maliciously circulated.

**Scira condemns the supposed manipulation of online reputation and search results while maintaining an extensive collection of webpages designed to promote his own account of the dispute.**

This does not independently establish improper search-engine manipulation by Scira, but it exposes the different standards he applies to online reputation-management activity.

### The Central Evidentiary Problem

Scira's archive combines several distinct assertions: that the posts existed, that they were removed, that their contents were false, that Nickles authored them, and that their publication constituted a criminal campaign.

Each proposition requires separate supporting evidence.

Nickles's allegations concerning coordinated sockpuppet networks on Reddit and Tripadvisor introduce another unresolved question: whether at least some removals were secured through manipulated reporting systems rather than independent findings about the underlying accusations.

Scira does not provide platform moderation records resolving that question.

Nor does the archive independently establish the identities of the original publishers or provide court findings substantiating its sweeping accusations.

**The central contradiction is that Scira treats removed posts as proof of defamation while providing no evidence sufficient to exclude coordinated reporting, ordinary moderation, or other explanations for their disappearance.**

Until the original platform decisions, reporting histories, account-attribution evidence, and relevant legal records can be examined, the archive cannot substantiate the conclusions Scira attaches to its 201 entries.


## Conflicting Criminal-Case Dates and Coordinated Use of the Same Legal Reference

Chad Scira's personal website repeatedly invokes criminal proceedings against Jesse Nickles as evidence supporting his accusations of defamation and harassment.

The same legal narrative appears on Thai Visa Centre's website and in public responses posted by other businesses associated with the immigration agency.

However, **the published accounts contain conflicting dates, refer to different complainants, and repeatedly circulate the same criminal-case reference without clearly explaining the relationship between the proceedings**.

### The 2024 Arrest Warrant Claims

Thai Visa Centre's website publishes a page titled *SEO Fugitive Jesse Nickles: Wanted on Criminal Charges*, presenting itself as an official record under reference `BKK-443/2567`.[^14]

The publication states that a criminal report was filed on May 18, 2024, and that an arrest warrant was issued on May 27, 2024.

Yet a later section on the same page identifies May 18, 2024, as the warrant's issuance date.

**Thai Visa Centre therefore publishes two different issuance dates for what it presents as the same arrest warrant.**

The website reproduces a purported warrant and describes the proceedings as involving criminal defamation.

However, its commercial publication is not an independently authenticated court record, and the discrepancy remains unexplained.

### Scira Introduces a Separate 2025 Complaint

Scira's personal website repeatedly refers to a criminal complaint filed by AGENTS CO., LTD. at Bang Kaeo Police Station on August 13, 2025.[^1]

On another page, Scira identifies the legal record as Daily Report Entry No. 4, Book `41/2568`, Report No. 56, dated August 13, 2025, while also citing reference `443/2567`.[^15]

He presents this documentation alongside claims that Nickles is subject to an existing arrest warrant.

But the chronology raises an obvious question.

**If the warrant was issued in May 2024, what is the legal relationship between that proceeding and the separate police complaint filed by AGENTS CO., LTD. in August 2025?**

A later complaint could conceivably refer to earlier proceedings. That possibility does not eliminate the need to establish the connection.

Scira does not provide a clear case chronology identifying the original complainant, the relevant court proceedings, and the relationship between the 2024 and 2025 records.

### The Same Case Reference Appears Across Different Businesses

The reference `BKK-443/2567` has also appeared in substantially identical responses to negative Trustpilot reviews published under several commercial domains.

Examples include:

- **Thai Visa Centre (`tvc.co.th`):** The immigration agency invokes the reference when responding to criticism.[^16]
- **TDAC (`tdac.in.th`):** The paid arrival-card service uses the reference in responses accusing reviewers of coordinated harassment.[^17]
- **90day (`90day.in.th`):** The immigration-reporting service repeats the same accusation and case number.[^18]
- **OG.TH (`og.th`):** The cannabis retailer reproduces the same criminal-case reference and substantially identical language.[^19]

The replies describe critics as participating in a coordinated harassment campaign, claim thousands of fraudulent accounts were created, and invoke an outstanding arrest warrant.

The repetition is particularly noteworthy because these businesses advertise different services and operate through distinct commercial identities.

**A criminal-case reference originating in a dispute involving an immigration-related business is being repeatedly deployed to challenge criticism of both immigration services and a cannabis retailer.**

The identical language indicates a shared response template or coordinated messaging. It does not independently establish who authored the replies or who ultimately controls every business.

### Scira's Claimed Separation From the Businesses

Scira maintains that he has no ownership, management, or operational involvement with Thai Visa Centre, AGENTS CO., LTD., or associated Thai businesses.[^1]

Yet his personal website invokes the same legal proceedings repeatedly promoted by these businesses.

On his page addressing an alleged $12,000 domain-purchase offer, Scira explicitly states that he personally filed no legal action against Nickles and attributes the complaint to AGENTS CO., LTD.[^15]

That distinction may explain who formally submitted the 2025 complaint. It does not explain the broader coordination of the public legal narrative.

His website does not identify who prepared the repeated Trustpilot statements, why the cannabis business adopted the same complaint reference, or how the various publications obtained their information about the proceedings.

These questions are especially relevant given the separately documented commercial connections between Thai Visa Centre, AGENTS CO., LTD., and OG Thai/GACP.

### The Missing Legal Chronology

The publications leave several matters unresolved:

1. Whether the May 2024 and August 2025 records concern the same proceedings or separate complaints.
2. Why Thai Visa Centre provides conflicting issuance dates for the purported warrant.
3. Which legal record supports the repeated descriptions of Nickles as a wanted fugitive.
4. Why multiple commercially distinct businesses invoke the same proceedings in responses to negative customer reviews.

**Scira and the businesses associated with Thai Visa Centre repeatedly present these legal claims as established facts while failing to reconcile the dates, complainants, and case references appearing in their own publications.**

The repeated dissemination of those claims across multiple businesses is independently observable.

What remains missing is a coherent, independently verifiable legal chronology establishing precisely what proceedings occurred and how the documents relate to one another.


## Undocumented Cannabis Research Agreement and Court Dismissal Claims

Chad Scira's website attempts to discredit reporting about the August 2020 police raid by claiming that cannabis discovered at his residence was part of authorized university research and that the resulting criminal case was dismissed.[^6]

However, his explanation makes several significant claims without providing the underlying documents needed to verify them.

### The Unidentified University Agreement

Scira claims that his wife, Grace, was cultivating cannabis for CBD research under a memorandum of understanding (MOU) with a university.

He further explains that COVID-19 travel restrictions complicated cooperation with the institution.[^6]

Yet **Scira does not identify the university, publish the agreement, name the researchers involved, or provide evidence of government authorization for cultivation**.

These omissions are significant because Thailand's medical cannabis framework in 2020 required specific licensing and authorization for research cultivation. An agreement with a university did not automatically establish permission to cultivate cannabis at a private residence.[^8]

Without identifying the institution or producing the relevant authorization, Scira leaves the central justification for the cultivation impossible to independently verify.

### The Missing Laboratory Evidence

Scira also claims that police incorrectly identified the plants as high-THC cannabis without conducting laboratory testing.

He states that he personally tested negative for THC and presents this as evidence supporting the CBD research explanation.[^6]

However, **a negative THC test on Scira establishes nothing about the chemical composition of plants discovered at his residence**.

His website provides no laboratory results establishing the CBD or THC content of those plants.

Consequently, neither the claimed research purpose nor the assertion that police misidentified the plants is substantiated by the scientific evidence presented.

### A Court Dismissal Without Court Documents

Scira states that he attended court several times before his criminal case was dropped following Thailand's cannabis decriminalization in June 2022.[^6]

Contemporary reporting confirms that thousands of cannabis-related cases were subject to dismissal following the legal change.[^7]

But **a nationwide change in cannabis law is not documentary proof of the outcome of Scira's individual criminal proceedings**.

His website does not provide a case number, court judgment, dismissal order, or other case-specific record establishing how and when his proceedings ended.

The distinction matters because a case dismissed following decriminalization is not equivalent to a judicial finding that the original cultivation was authorized or that police accusations were factually incorrect.

### Decriminalization Does Not Establish Prior Legality

Thailand's June 2022 policy change provides a plausible explanation for why cannabis charges originating in 2020 might have been dismissed.

It does not establish that the cultivation was properly licensed when police conducted their investigation two years earlier.

Nor would the dismissal of cannabis charges resolve the separate immigration-document allegations investigated during the same police operation.[^2]

Scira's defense effectively combines three separate propositions: that the cultivation was legitimate research, that police accusations were unfounded, and that the criminal proceedings ended following decriminalization.

**The documents capable of substantiating those propositions are conspicuously absent from his published account.**

Until the university agreement, cultivation authorization, relevant laboratory findings, and court disposition are available for examination, the most consequential elements of Scira's explanation remain unverified.
