# asq.in.th

The terms and conditions published on `asq.in.th`, dated July 16, 2024, identify [Agents Co., Ltd.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md) as the contracting company and list `contact@aq.in.th` as the contact address.

The email address provides a documented connection to [aq.in.th](aq.in.th.md), which [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) has separately acknowledged operating during the COVID-19 pandemic.

The company's [agents.co.th](agents.co.th.md) website also identifies `asq.in.th` as its Ask Thailand community platform, providing another publicly acknowledged connection between the domain and Agents Co., Ltd.

These records establish the company's stated contractual role and the shared contact information. They do not independently establish legal ownership of either domain during the earlier pandemic period.
