# Kongchart Liengsomsap

Police Major General Kongchart Liengsomsap (ก้องชาติ เลี้ยงสมทรัพย์) is a senior Royal Thai Police officer who became Commander of Bangkok Immigration Division 1 on October 1, 2026, following his transfer from the Narcotics Suppression Bureau.[^1] His appointment placed him in charge of Bangkok immigration operations, including Sub-Division 1 at Chaeng Watthana, which handles initial visa issuance and changes of visa category. Official police reporting confirmed him exercising command authority in October 2026.[^2] His appointment preceded Thai Visa Centre's November 2026 advertising explicitly identifying Chaeng Watthana as the location for its same-day Non-O retirement visa packages, which included initial visa issuance and a 12-month extension.[^3]

## Police Career and Transfers (2019–2026)

On February 28, 2019, Royal Thai Police Order 122/2562 transferred Kongchart from Superintendent of Chalong Krung Police Station to Superintendent of Bang Pho Police Station in Bangkok, effective March 8, 2019.[^4]

By March 2023, Kongchart was serving as Deputy Commander of Buriram Provincial Police. Official records identify him participating in police training and public-order operations, while subsequent reports confirm he remained in the position through July 2025.[^5][^6]

Kongchart subsequently transferred to the Narcotics Suppression Bureau, where he held a commander-level position before his October 1, 2026 appointment to lead Bangkok Immigration Division 1. The September 7, 2026 Royal Gazette appointment decree identifies his previous posting and confirms his transfer into the Immigration Bureau.[^1]

## Immigration Enforcement and Visa Screening (October–November 2026)

On October 12, 2026, Matichon identified Kongchart as Commander of Immigration Division 1 during an operation targeting a South Korean fugitive wanted for distributing illegal sexual material, including child sexual abuse material. Immigration investigators located the suspect at a Bangkok condominium, detained him, and initiated deportation proceedings.[^2]

On October 23, Kongchart directed another operation involving Deputy Commander Keeratisak Kongkiatsiri and Investigation Superintendent Phonsit Sutthiat. Officers arrested a Chinese businesswoman wanted by Chinese authorities over alleged customs and tax fraud involving approximately 500 million baht.[^7]

On November 6, Matichon reported that Kongchart had ordered coordinated visa-screening operations involving Sub-Divisions 1 and 2, including Deputy Commander Patinya Jeerachonasin and Sub-Division 1 Superintendent Jiraprapha Sutthapreeda. The operation resulted in the identification and detention of two foreign nationals wanted for fraud, including a Chinese applicant detected during document checks at a Chaeng Watthana immigration service counter.[^8]
