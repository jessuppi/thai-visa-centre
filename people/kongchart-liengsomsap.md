# Kongchart Liengsomsap

Police Major General Kongchart Liengsomsap (ก้องชาติ เลี้ยงสมทรัพย์) is a senior Royal Thai Police officer who became Commander of Bangkok Immigration Division 1 on October 1, 2026, following his transfer from the Narcotics Suppression Bureau.[^1] His appointment placed him in charge of Bangkok immigration operations, including Sub-Division 1 at Chaeng Watthana, which handles initial visa issuance and changes of visa category. Official police reporting confirmed him exercising command authority in October 2026.[^2] His appointment preceded Thai Visa Centre's November 2026 advertising explicitly identifying Chaeng Watthana as the location for its same-day Non-O retirement visa packages, which included initial visa issuance and a 12-month extension.[^3]
