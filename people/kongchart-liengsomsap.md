# Kongchart Liengsomsap

Police Major General Kongchart Liengsomsap (ก้องชาติ เลี้ยงสมทรัพย์) is a senior Royal Thai Police officer who became Commander of Bangkok Immigration Division 1 on October 1, 2026, following his transfer from the Narcotics Suppression Bureau.[^1] His appointment placed him in charge of Bangkok immigration operations, including Sub-Division 1 at Chaeng Watthana, which handles initial visa issuance and changes of visa category. Official police reporting confirmed him exercising command authority in October 2026.[^2] His appointment preceded Thai Visa Centre's November 2026 advertising explicitly identifying Chaeng Watthana as the location for its same-day Non-O retirement visa packages, which included initial visa issuance and a 12-month extension.[^3]

## Police Career and Transfers (2019–2026)

On February 28, 2019, Royal Thai Police Order 122/2562 initially assigned Kongchart to transfer from Superintendent of Chalong Krung Police Station to Superintendent of Bang Pho Police Station in Bangkok.[^4] However, subsequent Order 140/2562 revised his appointment, assigning him instead as Superintendent of Ban Dan Police Station in Buriram Province.[^12]

Ban Dan Police Station's official historical records confirm that Kongchart assumed command on March 15, 2019, establishing that the revised Buriram appointment took effect rather than the original Bangkok posting.[^13]

Royal Thai Police Order 674/2562, dated November 27, 2019, promoted Kongchart from Superintendent of Ban Dan Police Station to Deputy Commander of Buriram Provincial Police, effective December 13, 2019. His appointment at Ban Dan therefore lasted approximately nine months.[^14]

On November 2, 2020, Provincial Police Commander Rutthaphon Naowarat assigned Kongchart, who supervised narcotics enforcement, to investigate reports of a drug dealer using a nine-year-old child to deliver methamphetamine in Satuek District. Kongchart directed a coordinated police operation that resulted in an adult suspect's arrest and the seizure of 100 methamphetamine tablets. Investigators reported that the child had been paid to make deliveries.[^15]

Kongchart remained Deputy Commander of Buriram Provincial Police through at least July 2025, according to subsequent official police records.[^5][^6]

On September 5, 2025, Thansettakij reported that the Royal Gazette had announced Kongchart's promotion from Police Colonel to Police Major General as part of a nationwide round of senior police promotions.[^16]

By June 11, 2026, Kongchart was serving as Commander attached to the Narcotics Suppression Bureau (ผู้บังคับการประจำ บช.ปส.), according to a contemporary Daily News police profile.[^17] This establishes his transfer from Buriram Provincial Police to the Narcotics Suppression Bureau by June 2026, although the exact transfer date remains undocumented.

The September 7, 2026 Royal Gazette appointment decree subsequently identified his Narcotics Suppression Bureau posting and confirmed his appointment as Commander of Bangkok Immigration Division 1, effective October 1, 2026.[^1]

## Immigration Enforcement and Visa Screening (October–November 2026)

On October 12, 2026, Matichon identified Kongchart as Commander of Immigration Division 1 during an operation targeting a South Korean fugitive wanted for distributing illegal sexual material, including child sexual abuse material. Immigration investigators located the suspect at a Bangkok condominium, detained him, and initiated deportation proceedings.[^2]

On October 23, Kongchart directed another operation involving Deputy Commander Keeratisak Kongkiatsiri and Investigation Superintendent Phonsit Sutthiat. Officers arrested a Chinese businesswoman wanted by Chinese authorities over alleged customs and tax fraud involving approximately 500 million baht.[^7]

On November 6, Matichon reported that Kongchart had ordered coordinated visa-screening operations involving Sub-Divisions 1 and 2, including Deputy Commander Patinya Jeerachonasin and Sub-Division 1 Superintendent Jiraprapha Sutthapreeda. The operation resulted in the identification and detention of two foreign nationals wanted for fraud, including a Chinese applicant detected during document checks at a Chaeng Watthana immigration service counter.[^8]

## Buriram Drug Enforcement Operations (2023–2024)

On December 21, 2023, Kongchart participated in a Buriram Provincial Police press conference announcing the dismantling of two major drug-trafficking networks. The operation resulted in seven arrests and the seizure of nearly 300,000 methamphetamine tablets, three firearms, ammunition, and vehicles. Investigators also reported discovering a previously unfamiliar form of methamphetamine tablet requiring further examination.[^9]

On December 17, 2024, Kongchart was identified alongside Provincial Police Commander Rutthaphon Naowarat during another major anti-narcotics operation. Following the discovery of 54,000 methamphetamine tablets connected to a motorcycle accident on December 12, Rutthaphon ordered Kongchart to urgently expand the investigation. Police subsequently reported arrests across three trafficking networks, with 17 suspects detained and 407,413 methamphetamine tablets seized.[^10][^11]
