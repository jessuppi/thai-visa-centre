# Patinya Jeerachonasin

Police Colonel Patinya Jeerachonasin (ปฏิญญา จีรชนาสิน) is a senior Royal Thai Police officer who has served as Deputy Commander of Bangkok Immigration Division 1 since November 2021, following his transfer from the Immigration Bureau's investigation division.[^1] His responsibilities have included overseeing Sub-Division 1 at Chaeng Watthana, which handles initial visa issuance and changes of visa category. Official reports continued to identify him in this supervisory role in November 2026, spanning multiple changes in division leadership.[^2] His tenure overlaps with Thai Visa Centre's increasingly expedited Non-O retirement visa services, including its 2026 advertising of same-day processing at Chaeng Watthana.[^3]
