# Patinya Jeerachonasin

Police Colonel Patinya Jeerachonasin (ปฏิญญา จีรชนาสิน) is a senior Royal Thai Police officer who has served as Deputy Commander of Bangkok Immigration Division 1 since November 2021, following his transfer from the Immigration Bureau's investigation division.[^1] His responsibilities have included overseeing Sub-Division 1 at Chaeng Watthana, which handles initial visa issuance and changes of visa category. Official reports continued to identify him in this supervisory role in November 2026, spanning multiple changes in division leadership.[^2] His tenure overlaps with Thai Visa Centre's increasingly expedited Non-O retirement visa services, including its 2026 advertising of same-day processing at Chaeng Watthana.[^3]

## Career and 2021 Promotion

On March 8, 2019, Patinya transferred from the intelligence analysis and special equipment unit of Provincial Police Region 5 to become Superintendent of Sub-Division 4 within the Immigration Bureau's Investigation Division.[^4]

A November 24, 2020 police order records his transfer from the administrative division of Sisaket Provincial Police back to the Immigration Bureau's Investigation Division, where he became Superintendent of Sub-Division 2.[^5]

On November 12, 2021, Royal Thai Police Order 583/2564 appointed him Deputy Commander of Bangkok Immigration Division 1, placing him in a senior leadership position overseeing immigration operations in the capital.[^1]

## Sub-Division 1 Oversight (2024–2026)

The Immigration Bureau's 2024 personnel directory explicitly assigned Patinya supervisory responsibility for Sub-Division 1 (กก.1 บก.ตม.1), which handles initial visa issuance and changes of visa category at Chaeng Watthana.[^6]

On December 24, 2025, Patinya was identified among the deputy commanders serving under Immigration Division 1 Commander Prasart Khemaprasit during an enforcement operation at the Chaeng Watthana Government Complex.[^7] On November 6, 2026, Matichon identified Patinya alongside Sub-Division 1 Superintendent Jiraprapha Sutthapreeda in a coordinated visa-screening operation under newly appointed Commander Kongchart Liengsomsap.[^2]

These records establish Patinya's continued position within Division 1 leadership across the October 1, 2026 command transition. During this period, Thai Visa Centre advertised expedited initial Non-O retirement visa packages with 48–72-hour processing in September 2025, followed by same-day packages explicitly identifying Chaeng Watthana in November 2026.[^8][^3]
