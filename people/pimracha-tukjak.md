# Pimracha Tukjak

**Pimracha Tukjak** is a representative of [THNIC](../brands/thnic.md) who responded to complaints concerning allegedly unlawful content published on `.th` websites.

On April 17, 2025, Tukjak responded to Jesse Nickles regarding allegedly defamatory material published on [tvc.co.th](../domains/tvc.co.th.md) and [asq.in.th](../domains/asq.in.th.md), including publications accusing him of criminal conduct. She directed Nickles to Thailand's Online Complaint Center 1212, under the Ministry of Digital Economy and Society, and the Technology Crime Suppression Division (TCSD) of the Royal Thai Police.[^1]

On April 17, 2025, THNIC representative Orapin H. confirmed that Nickles's complaint had been forwarded to relevant contacts. When Nickles subsequently asked whether THNIC could forward the matter to Thai police or identify an appropriate police contact, Tukjak provided the contact details for the Online Complaint Center 1212 and TCSD. However, she did not identify which contacts had received the original complaint, whether THNIC had undertaken any independent assessment under its registration policies, or whether further action would follow.[^1]

The distinction became more significant in October 2026, when [Suthida Tepaloon](suthida-tepaloon.md) initially referred Nickles to the same two government authorities after receiving another complaint involving `tvc.co.th`. THNIC subsequently agreed to examine that complaint under its own policies after Nickles cited Sections 12.4 and 12.5. The repeated referrals illustrate how THNIC directed complainants toward external authorities without initially explaining its own procedures for investigating potentially abusive domain use.[^2][^3]
