# Pimracha Tukjak

**Pimracha Tukjak** is a representative of [THNIC](../brands/thnic.md) who responded to complaints concerning allegedly unlawful content published on `.th` websites.

On April 17, 2025, Tukjak responded to Jesse Nickles regarding allegedly defamatory material published on [tvc.co.th](../domains/tvc.co.th.md) and [asq.in.th](../domains/asq.in.th.md), including publications accusing him of criminal conduct. She directed Nickles to Thailand's Online Complaint Center 1212, under the Ministry of Digital Economy and Society, and the Technology Crime Suppression Division (TCSD) of the Royal Thai Police.[^1]

Her response did not indicate whether THNIC had investigated the reported content or considered enforcement action under its domain registration policies.

[^1]: Email correspondence between Jesse Nickles and Pimracha Tukjak, April 17, 2025 (private correspondence; case QWK-ZGJNX-001). Disputed URLs: `https://tvc.co.th/seo-fugitive-jesse-nickles-criminal-charges` and `https://asq.in.th/jesse-nickles-seo-operator-wanted-criminal-charges`.
