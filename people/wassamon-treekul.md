# Wassamon Treekul

**Wassamon Treekul** is a representative of [THNIC](../brands/thnic.md) who responded to a domain abuse complaint concerning an allegedly misleading Thai immigration website.

On April 18, 2025, Treekul responded to Jesse Nickles regarding [tdac.in.th](../domains/tdac.in.th.md), which Nickles alleged was impersonating Thailand's official Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) registration service and collecting travelers' personal information. His complaint also referenced other allegedly misleading websites associated with [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md), including [asq.in.th](../domains/asq.in.th.md), [eta.in.th](../domains/eta.in.th.md), and [dtv.in.th](../domains/dtv.in.th.md).[^1]

Treekul acknowledged receipt of the information and stated that THNIC would review the reported concerns and provide an update if further developments occurred. Her response did not confirm whether THNIC had independently verified the allegations or initiated enforcement action.[^1]

[^1]: Email correspondence between Jesse Nickles and Wassamon Treekul, April 17–18, 2025 (private correspondence; case VRK-ZLCQS-392).
