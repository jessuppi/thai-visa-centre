# Wassamon Treekul

**Wassamon Treekul** is a representative of [THNIC](../brands/thnic.md) who responded to a domain abuse complaint concerning an allegedly misleading Thai immigration website.

On April 18, 2025, Treekul responded to Jesse Nickles regarding [tdac.in.th](../domains/tdac.in.th.md), which Nickles alleged was impersonating Thailand's official Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) registration service and collecting travelers' personal information. His complaint also referenced other allegedly misleading websites associated with [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md), including [asq.in.th](../domains/asq.in.th.md), [eta.in.th](../domains/eta.in.th.md), and [dtv.in.th](../domains/dtv.in.th.md).[^1]

On April 18, 2025, Treekul acknowledged the complaint and confirmed in Thai that THNIC had received the information, would examine the reported concerns, and would provide an update if further developments occurred. **As of November 12, 2026, more than eighteen months later, the available correspondence contained no subsequent update, investigation findings, or enforcement decision.**[^1]

The absence of a documented outcome is particularly significant because **Section 17 of THNIC's Domain Name Registration Policy explicitly addresses phishing and information-security threats**. Nickles had reported a website allegedly impersonating an official Thai immigration service while soliciting travelers' passport details and personal information. Despite Treekul's promise of a review, THNIC provided no explanation of whether it assessed `tdac.in.th` under Section 17, contacted the domain registrant, or considered exercising its authority to suspend or revoke domain services.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Email correspondence between Jesse Nickles and Wassamon Treekul, April 17–18, 2025 (private correspondence; case VRK-ZLCQS-392).
[^2]: https://thnic.or.th/doc/thPolicy-EN-MAR2024.pdf
