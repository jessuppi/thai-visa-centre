# Tod Daniels

**Tod Daniels** is a Bangkok-based contributor to ASEAN NOW, where he holds the forum designation "Thai Visas Forum Expert." Daniels has repeatedly recommended Thai Visa Centre in discussions about Thai immigration services. On November 11, 2025, he publicly praised the company as Thailand's best-managed visa agency while acknowledging that Thai Visa Centre owns the Facebook group he moderates. Although Daniels denied having any personal affiliation with the company, his disclosed relationship raises questions about the independence of his endorsements.

## March 2022: Thai Visa Centre Acquires the Thai Visa Advice Group

On March 17, 2022, Tod Daniels announced that the Thai Visa Advice Facebook group, where he had served as an administrator for years, had been acquired by Thai Visa Centre. Daniels explained that he had not originally created the group and expressed hope that the transition would proceed smoothly for members and moderators.

Despite the group's acquisition by a commercial visa agency, Daniels remained involved in its moderation. In September 2023, he publicly described the group as his hobby and passion project of eight years, asking members to vote on whether he should remain a moderator following a dispute over deleted comments.

These statements establish that Daniels continued exercising moderation authority within a community owned by Thai Visa Centre years before his enthusiastic November 2025 endorsement of the agency. Whether he received compensation for his moderation activities or subsequent recommendations remains unclear.
