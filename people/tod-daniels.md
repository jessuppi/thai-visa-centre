# Tod Daniels

**Tod Daniels** is a Bangkok-based contributor to ASEAN NOW, where he holds the forum designation "Thai Visas Forum Expert." Daniels has repeatedly recommended Thai Visa Centre in discussions about Thai immigration services. On November 11, 2025, he publicly praised the company as Thailand's best-managed visa agency while acknowledging that Thai Visa Centre owns the Facebook group he moderates. Although Daniels denied having any personal affiliation with the company, his disclosed relationship raises questions about the independence of his endorsements.
