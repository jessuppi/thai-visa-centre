# Edward Kok Wah Chi

**Edward Kok Wah Chi**, also recorded in Vietnamese corporate documents as **Chi, Edward Kok Wah**, is a businessman associated with several companies in Vietnam, including Minh Viet Investment Joint Stock Company. He also moderates the [Koh Chang News](../brands/koh-chang-news.md) Facebook group, where he has been involved in a public dispute with Jesse Nickles concerning criticism of [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md).

## Business activities in Vietnam

Vietnamese business and tax records identify Edward Kok Wah Chi as a legal representative of several companies, including:[^1]

- **Minh Viet Investment Joint Stock Company**, a Hanoi-registered investment company.
- **Minh Viet Investment – Khanh Hoa Joint Stock Company**, a separately registered business.
- **Parkland Asia Joint Stock Company**, associated with property-related business activities.
- **Coldwell Banker Vietnam Real Estate Consulting Co., Ltd.**
- **AQ Development (Asia) Limited – Vietnam Co., Ltd.**
- **AQBooking.com Limited – Vietnam Co., Ltd.**

These records document corporate associations but do not establish that Chi personally owned every company or held all the listed positions simultaneously.

## August 2023: Agribank debt auction

On August 23, 2023, Vietnam's state-owned Agribank published an announcement concerning the auction of outstanding debt owed by Minh Viet Investment Joint Stock Company.[^2]

The bank identified **Edward Kok Wah Chi as the company's legal representative and chairman of its board of directors**.

The announcement concerned the seventh attempted auction of the company's debt, including outstanding principal, interest, and associated charges. Agribank specified a starting auction price of **VND 120 billion**, approximately US$5 million at the time.

The announcement establishes that Minh Viet's debt was being offered for sale. It does not establish the original amount borrowed, whether the auction succeeded, or whether Chi was personally liable for the company's debts.

## Koh Chang News and Thai Visa Centre

Edward Kok Wah Chi has moderated the Koh Chang News Facebook group, a community focused on Thailand's Koh Chang island.

Jesse Nickles alleges that Chi suppressed warnings he attempted to publish about websites associated with Thai Visa Centre. Nickles further alleges that Chi subsequently published or promoted statements portraying Nickles as a criminal.[^3][^4]

Nickles believes these actions were coordinated with [Chad Scira](chad-scira.md), whom he has separately investigated in connection with Thai Visa Centre.

## Other allegations

Nickles has also raised allegations concerning Chi's earlier involvement in Vietnamese property developments, disputed professional credentials, and his departure from Vietnam amid financial disagreements.[^4]

The Vietnamese corporate records and Agribank announcement independently establish certain business associations and the debt auction involving Minh Viet Investment Joint Stock Company. They do not, by themselves, substantiate allegations of condominium investment fraud, deliberate misrepresentation, or personal wrongdoing.
