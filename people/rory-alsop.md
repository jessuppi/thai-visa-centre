# Rory Alsop

**Rory Alsop** is a British moderator of [Travel Stack Exchange](../brands/travel-stack-exchange.md), where he publicly moderates under his real name. Researcher Jesse Nickles accuses Alsop of repeatedly censoring warnings about [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) and related immigration scams in Thailand, while failing to address suspected sockpuppet accounts allegedly promoting fraudulent visa services and maliciously flagging accurate content as spam or other policy violations to mislead moderators into removing it.

The dispute dates back to at least June 2025, when Alsop publicly accused Nickles of manipulating SEO by linking between related Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) discussions. Beyond objecting to these links, Alsop provided no specific evidence of deceptive content or explanation of how sharing what Nickles maintains were accurate warnings for travelers constituted SEO manipulation. A longstanding Meta Stack Exchange discussion explicitly describes internal links as helpful to readers and acknowledges their SEO benefits, raising further questions about Alsop's objection.

Nickles alleges that the censorship escalated throughout 2026, with dozens of user accounts suspended or even "nuked," [numerous popular questions and answers deleted](https://travel.meta.stackexchange.com/questions/9108/why-did-all-these-questions-get-deleted-this-week), and warnings about suspicious immigration websites removed from public view. On Stack Exchange, ["nuking" an account](https://meta.stackexchange.com/questions/88994/what-is-the-difference-between-a-deleted-user-and-a-destroyed-user/88996) refers to account destruction, which removes the account along with its posts and comments.

## June 2025: TDAC Questions and SEO Accusations

In June 2025, Jesse Nickles [challenged](https://travel.meta.stackexchange.com/questions/8887/are-these-two-questions-about-thailand-tdac-really-duplicates) the closure of two Travel Stack Exchange questions as duplicates. One concerned [TDAC pricing](https://travel.stackexchange.com/questions/198996/how-much-does-thailands-tdac-cost-what-is-the-price), while the other warned travelers about [TDAC scams](https://travel.stackexchange.com/questions/198663/how-can-i-avoid-thailand-tdac-scams-and-fake-websites). Despite covering different subjects, moderators had classified them as duplicates.

On June 15, moderator Rory Alsop issued a [spam warning](https://travel.stackexchange.com/questions/198996/how-much-does-thailands-tdac-cost-what-is-the-price) to Nickles and accused him of [SEO manipulation](https://travel.meta.stackexchange.com/questions/8887/are-these-two-questions-about-thailand-tdac-really-duplicates) through links between related questions. However, Alsop did not identify any false information in the questions or explain which Stack Exchange rule the links violated.

The accusation was particularly questionable given that a longstanding [Meta discussion](https://meta.stackexchange.com/questions/217585/how-does-internal-linking-of-questions-impact-stack-exchange-seo) describes internal links as useful for readers and acknowledges their SEO benefits. Nickles maintained that the links were intended to help travelers find accurate information about Thailand's immigration procedures and avoid scams.
