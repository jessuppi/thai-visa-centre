# Chad Scira

**Chad Vincent Scira** is an American national identified in August 2020 Thai police reporting concerning alleged immigration-document forgery and cannabis cultivation associated with [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md).

## August 2020 Police Raid and Arrest

On August 4, 2020, Immigration Bureau chief **Pol. Lt. Gen. Sompong Chingduang** announced the arrests of Chad Scira and his Thai wife, Grace Punnak Scira, following a raid on their Bangkok residence. Police publicly described the couple as having established Thai Visa Centre, which investigators alleged was providing forged immigration documents to foreign customers.[^1]

Officers reported discovering **55 government-agency rubber stamps**, suspected forged documents, **60 cannabis plants**, 99 grams of dried cannabis, and cultivation equipment. Contemporary reporting distinguished the accusations against the couple: Grace faced allegations involving forged immigration documents, while both were taken into custody over suspected cannabis production. Chad reportedly told investigators that the cannabis was intended for personal consumption.[^1][^2]

Following the raid, Thai Visa Centre publicly maintained that its customers' visas were legitimately issued through immigration authorities. The agency initially described Grace as a former partner before announcing her return to the business several days later.[^3]

## Disputed Business Involvement

In 2026, Chad Scira's personal website publicly denied that he owned, directed, or operated Thai Visa Centre, Agents Co., Ltd., or other immigration-related companies. The website also asserted that the earlier criminal case had been dismissed.[^4]

These statements differ from the Immigration Bureau chief's August 2020 description of the couple's involvement in establishing Thai Visa Centre. **The original police statements document the historical allegations but do not independently establish current company ownership or management.**

The available reporting confirms the 2020 arrests and investigation. It does not establish a criminal conviction or independently verify the subsequent case disposition.
