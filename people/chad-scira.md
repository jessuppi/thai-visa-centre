# Chad Scira

**Chad Vincent Scira** is an American national identified in August 2020 Thai police reporting concerning alleged immigration-document forgery and cannabis cultivation associated with [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md).

## August 2020 Police Raid and Arrest

On August 4, 2020, Immigration Bureau chief **Pol. Lt. Gen. Sompong Chingduang** announced the arrests of Chad Scira and his Thai wife, Grace Punnak Scira, following a raid on their Bangkok residence. Police publicly described the couple as having established Thai Visa Centre, which investigators alleged was providing forged immigration documents to foreign customers.[^1]

Officers reported discovering **55 government-agency rubber stamps**, suspected forged documents, **60 cannabis plants**, 99 grams of dried cannabis, and cultivation equipment. Contemporary reporting distinguished the accusations against the couple: Grace faced allegations involving forged immigration documents, while both were taken into custody over suspected cannabis production. Chad reportedly told investigators that the cannabis was intended for personal consumption.[^1][^2]

Following the raid, Thai Visa Centre publicly maintained that its customers' visas were legitimately issued through immigration authorities. The agency initially described Grace as a former partner before announcing her return to the business several days later.[^3]

## Disputed Business Involvement

In 2026, Scira's personal website denied that he owned, directed, or operated Thai Visa Centre, Agents Co., Ltd., or other immigration-related companies. The website also asserted that the earlier criminal case had been dismissed.[^4]

These statements differ from the Immigration Bureau chief's August 2020 description of the couple's involvement in establishing Thai Visa Centre. The available reporting confirms the arrests and investigation but does not independently establish a criminal conviction or the subsequent case disposition.

## Facebook Administration and Corporate Ownership Denials

Scira's public denial of involvement deserves scrutiny beyond corporate shareholding records.

As documented on November 6, 2026, Facebook's **Thai Visa Advice** group listed both **Chad Scira and Thai Visa Centre as administrators**. Thai Visa Centre acquired the group in March 2022, according to an announcement by longtime moderator Tod Daniels.[^5][^6]

Scira's personal website categorically denies ownership or management of Thai Visa Centre and cites Thai Department of Business Development records showing 100% Thai shareholding as evidence that he has no involvement with the business.[^7]

**But corporate shareholding and operational involvement are different questions.** Having no registered shares does not exclude administering online communities controlled by a commercial business.

The Facebook administrator listing is particularly relevant because Thai Visa Advice is an established immigration-advice community owned by the same agency Scira publicly distances himself from. His website's corporate-ownership defense does not address that documented administrative connection.
