# Jiraprapha Sutthapreeda

Police Colonel Jiraprapha Sutthapreeda (จิรประภาพ สุทธปรีดา) is a Royal Thai Police officer who served as Superintendent of Chachoengsao Provincial Immigration in March 2025 before transferring to Bangkok Immigration Division 1 later that year.[^1] By December 25, 2025, he was serving as Superintendent of Sub-Division 1 at Chaeng Watthana, responsible for operations including initial visa issuance and changes of visa category.[^2] He remained in that position in November 2026, working under Deputy Commander Patinya Jeerachonasin.[^3] His appointment coincided with Thai Visa Centre's increasingly expedited Non-O retirement visa services, including its 2026 advertising of same-day processing at Chaeng Watthana.[^4]

## Immigration Career and Transfers (2020–2025)

On November 24, 2020, Royal Thai Police Order 626/2563 transferred Jiraprapha from the Southern Border Provinces Investigation Division, where he headed Interrogation Sub-Division 1, to become Superintendent of Ayutthaya Provincial Immigration.[^5]

On January 13, 2023, Police Order 24/2566 reassigned him from Ayutthaya to head Sisaket Provincial Immigration, under Immigration Division 4.[^6]

On February 28, 2025, Immigration Bureau Order 54/2568 transferred Jiraprapha from Sisaket to Chachoengsao Provincial Immigration. Official records confirm he was serving there on March 18, 2025, but by December 25 he had become Superintendent of Sub-Division 1 at Chaeng Watthana, placing his Chachoengsao assignment at less than ten months.[^1][^2][^7]
