# Jiraprapha Sutthapreeda

Police Colonel Jiraprapha Sutthapreeda (จิรประภาพ สุทธปรีดา) is a Royal Thai Police officer who served as Superintendent of Chachoengsao Provincial Immigration in March 2025 before transferring to Bangkok Immigration Division 1 later that year.[^1] By December 25, 2025, he was serving as Superintendent of Sub-Division 1 at Chaeng Watthana, responsible for operations including initial visa issuance and changes of visa category.[^2] He remained in that position in November 2026, working under Deputy Commander Patinya Jeerachonasin.[^3] His appointment coincided with Thai Visa Centre's increasingly expedited Non-O retirement visa services, including its 2026 advertising of same-day processing at Chaeng Watthana.[^4]

## Immigration Career and Transfers (2020–2025)

On November 24, 2020, Royal Thai Police Order 626/2563 transferred Jiraprapha from the Southern Border Provinces Investigation Division, where he headed Interrogation Sub-Division 1, to become Superintendent of Ayutthaya Provincial Immigration.[^5]

On January 13, 2023, Police Order 24/2566 reassigned him from Ayutthaya to head Sisaket Provincial Immigration, under Immigration Division 4.[^6]

On February 28, 2025, Immigration Bureau Order 54/2568 transferred Jiraprapha from Sisaket to Chachoengsao Provincial Immigration. Official records confirm he was serving there on March 18, 2025, but by December 25 he had become Superintendent of Sub-Division 1 at Chaeng Watthana, placing his Chachoengsao assignment at less than ten months.[^1][^2][^7]

## Sub-Division 1 Leadership and Visa Screening (2025–2026)

On December 25, 2025, Jiraprapha was publicly identified as Superintendent of Sub-Division 1 during an official visit by a Shenzhen immigration delegation to Chaeng Watthana. He participated alongside Deputy Commander Narongwet Oonsungnoen and the superintendents of Sub-Divisions 2 and 3, confirming his position within Bangkok Immigration Division 1's leadership by the end of 2025.[^2]

On November 6, 2026, Matichon identified Jiraprapha and Deputy Commander Patinya Jeerachonasin as the officers overseeing Sub-Division 1 during a coordinated immigration-screening operation ordered under Commander Kongchart Liengsomsap. The operation involved checking foreign applicants against criminal records and coordinating with immigration investigators.[^3]

The report described a Chinese national wanted for fraud who was identified during document checks at a Sub-Division 1 service counter on November 5. Officers subsequently detained the suspect, while a separate operation involving Sub-Division 2 identified an Indian national wanted for fraud in Chonburi.[^3]

## December 2019 Highway Police Investigation

On December 3, 2019, Jiraprapha was identified as Superintendent of Highway Police Division 7 following a widely publicized confrontation between highway police officers and a pickup truck driver at a checkpoint in Rattaphum District, Songkhla Province. Video footage of the incident raised questions about the officers' conduct after the vehicle's tire burst during the confrontation.[^8]

Jiraprapha ordered a formal investigation with findings to be reported within seven days and temporarily reassigned an officer involved in the incident to another highway police station. Police initially maintained that the tire had burst accidentally, while authorities stated that disciplinary proceedings would follow if the investigation established misconduct.[^8][^9]

## Retirement Visa Processing Responsibilities (2025–2026)

Immigration Bureau Order 15/2568, issued January 23, 2025, assigned Sub-Division 1 of Bangkok Immigration Division 1 responsibility for receiving initial visa applications and requests to change visa categories. Retirement-purpose applications required approval from the Division 1 Commander, while officers holding the rank of inspector or higher within Sub-Division 1 were authorized to sign the resulting visa endorsements.[^10]

Jiraprapha's appointment as Sub-Division 1 Superintendent by December 2025 placed him in charge of the unit responsible for processing these applications, under the approval authority of Commander Prasart Khemaprasit and, from October 1, 2026, Commander Kongchart Liengsomsap.[^2][^3][^10]

The order required applicants to submit visa issuance or category-change requests at least 15 days before their existing permission to stay expired. Separately, a July 2025 retirement-visa checklist published by Pathum Thani Immigration specified a 15-day processing period, a 2,000-baht government fee, financial verification, and an initial permitted stay of no more than 90 days.[^10][^11]

By November 2026, Thai Visa Centre was advertising same-day initial Non-O retirement visa issuance and a 12-month extension at Chaeng Watthana. The advertised turnaround was substantially shorter than the processing period published by Pathum Thani Immigration, although the latter was a provincial guideline rather than a confirmed Bangkok service standard.[^4][^11]
