# Chanat Thepla

R.T.T. Chanat Thepla is a Royal Thai Police officer identified as having received a 2024 complaint from Thai Visa Centre alleging that Jesse Nickles had issued fake checks to the agency. The Royal Thai Police subsequently confirmed the complaint to Nickles's lawyer. Nickles denies issuing or authorizing the checks and states that he was outside Thailand at the time. Although Thepla received the report, the available evidence does not establish whether he independently investigated its authenticity, knew of any alleged fabrication, or was involved beyond documenting the complaint.[^1][^2]
