# Chanat Thepla

R.T.T. Chanat Thepla is a Royal Thai Police officer who reportedly received a 2024 complaint from Thai Visa Centre alleging that Jesse Nickles had issued fake checks as payment to the agency. The Royal Thai Police later confirmed the complaint to Nickles's lawyer.[^1][^2]

Nickles denies issuing or authorizing any such checks and states that he was outside Thailand when they were supposedly written. He maintains that he has never conducted business with Chad Scira, Grace Scira, Thai Visa Centre, or any of their associated companies. His only related interaction occurred during the COVID-19 pandemic, when he says he was misled into contacting a hotel advertised on `asq.in.th`, a website that later became associated with their operations. Nickles states that this inquiry was not a business transaction with the website's operators.

Although Thepla received the complaint, the available evidence does not establish whether he independently investigated the allegations, knew the checks were allegedly fabricated, or played any role beyond recording the report.
