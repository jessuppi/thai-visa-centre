# Priscilla Yeung

**Priscilla Yeung** is the co-founder and CEO of [Issa Compass](../brands/issa-compass.md), a commercial immigration-services platform operated by Singapore-registered [Issara Platforms Pte. Ltd.](../companies/issara-platforms-pte-ltd.md) and Thailand-registered [Issa Systems Co., Ltd.](../companies/issa-systems-co-ltd.md).[^1]

She co-founded the business with Aaron Yip and has served as a public spokesperson for its immigration technology, commercial expansion, and customer-service claims.

## Public Promotion of Immigration Technology

On October 9, 2026, Issa Compass announced that its AI-powered verification system had expanded to cover all 94 Thai embassy and consulate locations worldwide.[^2]

Yeung personally promoted the announcement, claiming the expanded coverage provided consistent real-time verification regardless of the embassy handling an application.

The press release claimed that the platform checked every Thai embassy and consulate against current, location-specific immigration requirements.

**Issa Compass presents comprehensive global verification as an established technical capability without publishing the underlying embassy-level data, verification methodology, or independent accuracy results.**

The distinction matters because applicants are paying for expertise in requirements that can determine whether their visas are approved or rejected.

The company also claims to track unpublished embassy-specific requirements that ordinary applicants cannot readily verify.[^3]

These claims are central to Issa Compass's marketing of its AI technology as superior to conventional immigration consultants.

## Promotional Approval and Customer Statistics

In April 2026, Issa Compass announced that it had surpassed 100,000 platform users, maintained a 99% approval rate for pre-qualified visa applications, and reached $1 million in annualized revenue within five months.[^4]

The company has repeatedly presented these figures as evidence of its credibility.

However, its published approval statistics exclude applicants who fail its internal pre-qualification screening.

Issa Compass does not disclose the total number of applications assessed, the number rejected before submission, or independently verified government approval records.

**A 99% success rate calculated only after the company selects which applications qualify is not equivalent to a 99% approval rate for everyone who purchases its services.**

The company has also alternated between describing its reach in terms of users, consultations, and clients without providing consistent definitions or underlying figures.

These promotional statistics are published by the company Yeung leads, rather than independently audited performance reports.

## Money-Back Guarantee Versus Contractual Restrictions

Issa Compass markets a money-back guarantee as financial protection for customers purchasing visa assistance.

However, its Terms of Service, updated November 13, 2026, reserve substantial control over whether customers actually qualify for that protection.[^5]

Under Section 7.3.7, applicable to purchases beginning November 14, 2026:

- Customers can pay before their guarantee eligibility is confirmed.
- The company's legal team can subsequently reject guarantee coverage.
- Customers abandoning the application can receive service credit instead of a cash refund.
- The credit is valid for only 12 months.
- The Initial Assessment Fee remains non-refundable.
- The company can withhold detailed reasons for rejecting guarantee eligibility.

**A customer can therefore pay for a plan advertised with refund protection, have that protection denied afterward, and be left with time-limited credit instead of their money back.**

For purchases beginning August 29, 2026, the standard guarantee also excludes government visa fees.

These restrictions sharply narrow the financial protection suggested by the company's money-back marketing.

Issa Compass's own consumer guidance classifies service-credit-only guarantees as weak protection, yet its contract permits precisely that remedy.[^6]

## Public Accountability

Yeung is publicly identified as Issa Compass's CEO and has personally promoted the technical capabilities underpinning its commercial services.

The business relies on three major credibility claims:

1. Comprehensive, real-time verification of immigration requirements.
2. Exceptional visa approval rates and substantial customer volume.
3. Financial protection through a money-back guarantee.

**Its published evidence and contractual terms do not support the uncomplicated picture of reliability presented by those marketing claims.**

Issa Compass should substantiate its approval statistics, explain how its claimed real-time verification is measured, and reconcile its money-back advertising with the restrictions imposed after customers pay.

These are questions of commercial transparency and accountability for a business selling professional assistance with consequential immigration decisions.
