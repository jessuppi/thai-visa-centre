# Dechathorn Saengsap

Dechathorn Saengsap (เดชาธร แสงศัพท์) is a senior editor at Thailand's Daily News newspaper, serving as its head of website news since at least 2022. In April 2025, Daily News published an article introducing Thailand's Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) system that included a link to the unofficial `tdac.in.th` website alongside the government's official registration portal. This was particularly concerning because TDAC registration is free, while `tdac.in.th` promotes commercial registration services. Jesse Nickles says he subsequently contacted Daily News to warn about the misleading website but received no response. The incident raises questions about the newspaper's editorial oversight of immigration-related information and its handling of subsequent warnings.[^1][^2][^3]
