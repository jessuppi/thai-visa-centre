# Atthaphon Sirilatthayakorn

Police Lieutenant Colonel Atthaphon Sirilatthayakorn (อรรถพล ศิริลัทธยากร) is a Royal Thai Police officer who served as head of Chainat Provincial Immigration. A January 2023 Immigration Bureau transfer order assigned him to Chainat from the investigation and suppression division of Immigration Division 2. Official Chainat Immigration records confirm that he remained in charge in December 2024 and January 2025, when he oversaw meetings concerning the office's annual integrity and transparency assessment.[^1][^2]

## Family and Business Interests

Atthaphon is a son of Itthi Sirilatthayakorn, a former Thai parliamentarian and deputy minister, and a brother of politician Atthakorn Sirilatthayakorn, who has held several ministerial positions.[^3][^4]

In January 2025, investigative outlet Isra News reported that Atthaphon held a 15% ownership stake in Atthasiri Co., Ltd. (บริษัทอรรถศิริ จำกัด), a family-owned real estate company in Chachoengsao Province, as of April 30, 2024. His brothers Atthakan and Atthaphong held the remaining 85%, with stakes of 45% and 40%, respectively.[^3]

Isra News also examined a 19-million-baht loan that their father had made to the company in July 2015, as disclosed in his official asset declaration, and subsequently published details of the loan agreement.[^3][^5]
