# Suthida Tepaloon

**Suthida Tepaloon** is a representative of [THNIC](../brands/thnic.md) who handles domain abuse reports and complaints involving `.th` domain registrations.

In November 2026, Tepaloon responded to a complaint concerning [tvc.co.th](../domains/tvc.co.th.md), confirming that THNIC had notified the domain registrant. She stated that the domain complied with THNIC's registration policy and that the registrar lacked authority to investigate the legality or accuracy of content published on the website.[^1]

[^1]: Written correspondence from Suthida Tepaloon of THNIC (November 2026; private correspondence).
