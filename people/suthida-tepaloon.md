# Suthida Tepaloon

**Suthida Tepaloon** is a representative of [THNIC](../brands/thnic.md) who handles domain abuse reports and complaints involving `.th` domain registrations.

On October 21, 2026, Jesse Nickles submitted a complaint to THNIC concerning [tvc.co.th](../domains/tvc.co.th.md), alleging that the website published photographs of him without consent alongside defamatory statements. In subsequent correspondence, he alleged that corrupt Thai immigration officers had unlawfully leaked the photographs from restricted airport CCTV surveillance systems to [Chad Scira](../people/chad-scira.md), who then manipulated them using AI and published them to harass and defame him. Tepaloon initially referred Nickles to Thailand's Online Complaint Center 1212 and Technology Crime Suppression Division. After Nickles cited Sections 12.4 and 12.5 of THNIC's Domain Name Registration Policy, she confirmed on October 22 that THNIC would review the complaint under its registration rules.[^3]

On October 26, Tepaloon stated that THNIC was gathering information for presentation to management and other relevant parties. On October 29, she confirmed that the complaint had been forwarded to the domain registrant with a request to review and update the disputed webpage. She did not confirm whether the registrant had responded or taken corrective action.[^4]

On November 5, 2026, Tepaloon confirmed that THNIC's executive management and Legal Team were jointly reviewing the complaint. On November 10, she stated that THNIC had formally notified the domain registrant and determined that [tvc.co.th](../domains/tvc.co.th.md) complied with its registration policy because it matched the legally registered company name [TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd.](../companies/tvc-visa-agent-co-ltd.md). She characterized the allegations of defamation and privacy violations as a dispute between private parties, stating that THNIC lacked authority to determine the legality or accuracy of website content.[^1]

Tepaloon further stated that THNIC would maintain the domain's normal operation unless presented with a court order or an official order from a competent Thai government authority explicitly directing its suspension. Nickles challenged this position on November 10, citing Sections 12.4, 12.5, 14.1.4.2, and 14.2 of THNIC's Domain Name Registration Policy, which address investigations and action against domains used in bad faith. He requested an explanation of why these provisions did not apply, but no substantive response to those specific questions had been received as of November 12. THNIC's response also did not clarify which legal entity operated the services advertised under the [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) name, for which no corresponding Thai company registration has been identified.[^1][^2]
