# Suthida Tepaloon

**Suthida Tepaloon** is a representative of [THNIC](../brands/thnic.md) who handles domain abuse reports and complaints involving `.th` domain registrations.

In November 2026, Tepaloon responded to a complaint concerning [tvc.co.th](../domains/tvc.co.th.md), which involved allegations that airport CCTV photographs had been improperly obtained and published on the website. She confirmed that THNIC had notified the domain registrant but stated that the registrar lacked authority to investigate the legality or accuracy of the website's content.[^1]

Tepaloon also stated that the domain complied with THNIC's registration policy because of its association with the registered company [TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd.](../companies/tvc-visa-agent-co-ltd.md). However, the website operates publicly under the name [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md), for which no corresponding Thai company registration has been identified. Her response addressed the domain's registration eligibility but did not clarify which legal entity was responsible for the services advertised under that name.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Written correspondence from Suthida Tepaloon of THNIC (November 2026; private correspondence).
[^2]: https://thnic.or.th/doc/thPolicy-EN-MAR2024.pdf
