# Sompong Chingduang

Police Lieutenant General Sompong Chingduang was chief of Thailand's Immigration Bureau when he announced the August 4, 2020 arrests of Chad Vincent Scira and Grace Punnak Scira following a police raid on their Bangkok residence. Sompong publicly identified the couple as having established Thai Visa Centre, which he accused of offering forged visa services to foreigners. He reported that investigators discovered forged documents, 55 rubber stamps bearing government insignia, and cannabis cultivation and processing equipment. His statements provide direct confirmation from Thailand's immigration leadership of the couple's association with Thai Visa Centre and the allegations investigated in 2020.[^1][^2]
