# Thai Visa Centre

Thai Visa Centre is an unregistered trade name used to advertise Thai visa and immigration-assistance services. According to the Thai corporate records examined for this investigation, no company has ever been registered in Thailand under the exact legal name "Thai Visa Centre." The brand has instead been associated with separately registered corporate entities.

In a [May 2025 ASEAN NOW discussion](../threads/2025-05-01-asean-now-fake-tdac-emerges.md), the agency's forum account identified [Agents Co., Ltd.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md) as the operator of [tdac.in.th](../domains/tdac.in.th.md) and [tdac.agents.co.th](../domains/tdac.agents.co.th.md).

In a [May 2026 Reddit discussion](../threads/2026-05-reddit-thailand-90-day-online-reporting.md), the agency acknowledged offering paid 90-day immigration reporting services through [90day.in.th](../domains/90day.in.th.md).

On its own `tvc.co.th/scam` page, the agency also stated that it operated [aq.in.th](../domains/aq.in.th.md) and [tp.in.th](../domains/tp.in.th.md) during the COVID-19 pandemic.

These statements document the agency's publicly claimed operational connections. They do not independently establish the legal ownership of the domains or the complete relationships between the corporate entities involved.
