# Issa Compass

**Issa Compass** is an unregistered trade name used to advertise commercial visa and immigration-assistance services, particularly Thailand's Destination Thailand Visa (DTV).

The brand operates through two separately registered companies: [Issara Platforms Pte. Ltd.](../companies/issara-platforms-pte-ltd.md) in Singapore and [Issa Systems Co., Ltd.](../companies/issa-systems-co-ltd.md) in Thailand.[^1]

## Corporate Identity and Registration Claims

Issa Compass's Privacy Policy explicitly states **"We trade as Issa Compass."** The two companies have different registration numbers and legal responsibilities.[^2]

Nevertheless, the website's guide to identifying fraudulent visa agencies presents Issa Compass as a registered software company operating in Singapore and Thailand.[^3]

**The registered entities are Issara Platforms and Issa Systems, not a company named Issa Compass.** Their corporate registrations should not be confused with registration of the advertised trade name.

## Customer Information and Responsibility

The company's Privacy Policy identifies Issara Platforms as the controller of customers' digital visa files, while Issa Systems handles physical documents and in-person services in Thailand.[^2]

Passport copies, financial records, and other sensitive immigration documents are therefore processed through a cross-border arrangement involving two distinct legal entities.

The policy states that customer information is stored primarily in Singapore and accessed from both countries.[^2]
