# Digital Broker Ventures

**Digital Broker Ventures (DBV)** is a Thailand-based venture-building and digital marketing business associated with the founding and early ownership of [The Thaiger](the-thaiger.md).

The Thaiger's official company history identifies Digital Broker Ventures as the new owners behind its transformation from the Phuket Gazette into a digital news publisher in 2018.[^1]

## Media Ownership and Commercial Activities

Digital Broker Ventures states that it helped migrate more than **25,000 articles** from the Phuket Gazette and developed The Thaiger's online publishing platform.[^2]

Its portfolio describes expanding The Thaiger through SEO, sponsored articles, real estate advertising, financial products, insurance referrals, and commercial partnerships.[^2]

The same publisher subsequently acquired [ASEAN NOW](asean-now.md) in April 2024 and promoted commercial immigration services associated with [Thai Visa Centre](thai-visa-centre.md).

These overlapping publishing, advertising, and referral activities are relevant to evaluating the commercial interests behind immigration-related coverage.

## Related Businesses and Management

Digital Broker Ventures identifies **Michael Kenner** as its chief executive officer and [Darren Lyons](../people/darren-lyons.md) as its chief marketing officer.[^3]

Its portfolio includes FazWaz, MyMediTravel, RE Marketing, and several other online brokerage and lead-generation businesses.[^4]

The relationships establish a broader commercial network behind The Thaiger's development, rather than an independent publishing venture operating in isolation.

## 2026 Ownership Change

The Thaiger states that **The Thaiger Pte. Ltd. became wholly owned by Dot Property Pte. Ltd. in 2026**, placing the publisher within the LIFULL Connect group.[^1]

Digital Broker Ventures' historical role should therefore be distinguished from the publisher's current disclosed ownership.

## News Publishing as a Commercial Customer-Acquisition Strategy

Digital Broker Ventures' published corporate strategy explicitly identifies news publishing as a mechanism for supporting its wider portfolio of digital brokerage businesses.[^5]

Its About page describes several commercial advantages available to portfolio companies through its media operations:

- Search-engine backlinks improving the visibility of associated businesses.
- Referral traffic from news publications to commercial websites.
- Distribution of press releases and review content.
- Targeted access to geographically relevant audiences.

These advantages are presented as part of an integrated business-development strategy rather than merely conventional newspaper advertising.

### Integration With Real Estate Brokerage

Digital Broker Ventures' FazWaz portfolio description identifies the development of property-related articles using market data generated by its brokerage platform.

The company describes this content as attracting additional search-engine traffic and facilitating partnerships with The Thaiger's property-news operations.[^6]

Its separate Thaiger portfolio description identifies a FazWaz-powered property portal, sponsored content, financial products, insurance offerings, and travel services among the publisher's commercial features.[^2]

These disclosures establish that editorial content, search visibility, and commercial customer acquisition were deliberately integrated into the group's historical business model.

### Historical Evidence of Shared Commercial Objectives

In its 2019 corporate update, *Preparing for 2020*, Digital Broker Ventures reported that The Thaiger's organic readership in August 2019 was approximately 30 times higher than in December 2018.

The company attributed this growth to search-engine optimization, marketing, data-driven publishing, and technical development.

It explicitly identified the publisher's growing audience as creating additional opportunities for Digital Broker Ventures and described its expansion into Vietnamese-language news as benefiting the wider portfolio.[^7]

These readership figures are company-reported and have not been independently audited.

### Relevance to Subsequent Immigration Promotions

The documented strategy predates The Thaiger's April 2024 acquisition of ASEAN NOW and its subsequent promotion of immigration services associated with Thai Visa Centre and AGENTS CO., LTD.

It also provides historical context for The Thaiger's later expansion into commercial visa referrals and [immigration-related data collection](../domains/thethaiger.com.md).

The group's historical business model does not independently establish that particular immigration articles were commissioned, that sponsors controlled editorial decisions, or that Digital Broker Ventures held financial interests in the advertised visa agencies.

**Nevertheless, Digital Broker Ventures expressly identified the commercial value of using news publications to generate customer traffic and promote associated businesses.**

That documented strategy is relevant when evaluating the distinction between independent editorial coverage and commercial promotion within the broader publishing network.
