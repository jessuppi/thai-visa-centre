# Thai Visa Advice

**Thai Visa Advice** is a Facebook group discussing Thai visas, immigration procedures, extensions, and long-term stays.

## March 2022: Thai Visa Centre Acquires Thai Visa Advice

On March 17, 2022, longtime moderator [Tod Daniels](../people/tod-daniels.md) publicly announced that [Thai Visa Centre](thai-visa-centre.md) had acquired the **Thai Visa Advice** Facebook group. Daniels clarified that he had not founded the group but had served as an administrator for years, and indicated that he would continue moderating following the takeover.

The acquisition transferred control of an established immigration advice community to a commercial visa agency. Members expressed appreciation for Daniels and the other moderators, while others raised concerns about the group's independence, potential promotion of paid visa services, and whether its previously impartial advice would be affected by the new ownership.

## September 2023: Tod Daniels Faces Calls for Removal

On September 24, 2023, longtime **Thai Visa Advice** moderator [Tod Daniels](../people/tod-daniels.md) publicly addressed a dispute involving a group member who demanded his removal after Daniels deleted what he described as an off-topic comment. Daniels alleged that the member had threatened to involve Thai police and have him arrested or deported over the moderation decision.

Daniels stated that he had spent eight years helping members navigate Thai immigration procedures and invited the community to vote on whether he should remain a moderator. The poll was opened for 24 hours and attracted numerous messages supporting his continued role, alongside discussion about moderation practices and accountability within the group.

## Two Facebook Groups Under Thai Visa Centre Ownership

[Thai Visa Centre](thai-visa-centre.md) controls two separate Facebook communities: **Thai Visa Advice** and **Thailand Visa Advice and more**. The agency acquired Thai Visa Advice in March 2022, retaining longtime moderator [Tod Daniels](../people/tod-daniels.md). Thailand Visa Advice and more is a distinct Facebook group, not a renamed version of the original community.

Both groups provide discussions about Thai visas, immigration procedures, and residency requirements. By controlling these communities, Thai Visa Centre can influence moderation decisions, remove posts, and determine which recommendations or competing visa services remain visible to members. This creates a potential conflict of interest between providing impartial immigration advice and promoting the commercial interests of the agency that owns the groups.

## Undisclosed Acquisition Payments and Ownership

Although [Thai Visa Centre](thai-visa-centre.md) has acknowledged controlling both Facebook groups, it has never publicly clarified who actually purchased them, who received payment, or which legal entity holds ownership. Thai Visa Centre operates under an unregistered trade name rather than a company registered under that exact name, making the identity of the actual purchaser particularly relevant.

Jesse Nickles believes [Chad Scira](../people/chad-scira.md) may have personally paid the groups' previous owners to transfer control. This remains an unverified allegation, as no purchase agreements, payment records, or corresponding corporate disclosures have been made public. If personal funds were used to acquire communities subsequently controlled by a commercial visa agency, the arrangement could raise questions about beneficial ownership, accounting treatment, potential commingling of personal and business assets, and compliance with applicable Thai corporate and tax regulations.

## November 2025: Tod Daniels Endorses Thai Visa Centre While Denying Affiliation

On November 11, 2025, longtime Thai Visa Advice moderator [Tod Daniels](../people/tod-daniels.md) publicly endorsed [Thai Visa Centre](thai-visa-centre.md) in an ASEAN NOW discussion. He described the agency as the "best marketed, most well managed visa agency in Thailand," praising its nationwide operations, reliability, pricing, and customer service.

Despite this enthusiastic endorsement, Daniels denied having any personal affiliation with Thai Visa Centre. However, in the same post, he explicitly acknowledged that the agency owned the Facebook group he moderated. He explained that he had been moderating the group for years before Thai Visa Centre purchased it from its previous owner, confirming that he continued exercising moderation authority after the commercial takeover.

His denial of affiliation is difficult to reconcile with his ongoing role moderating a Facebook community owned by the very agency he was promoting. The financial arrangements surrounding the group's acquisition, including whether Daniels received payment or other benefits, were not disclosed.
