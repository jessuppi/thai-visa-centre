# THNIC

**T.H.NIC Co., Ltd. (THNIC)** provides domain registration services for Thailand's `.th` namespace and handles complaints concerning domain registrations and alleged abuse.

## Earlier Abuse Complaints (2025)

On April 13, 2025, Jesse Nickles contacted THNIC concerning allegedly defamatory material published on [tvc.co.th](../domains/tvc.co.th.md) and [asq.in.th](../domains/asq.in.th.md). On April 17, THNIC representative Orapin H. acknowledged the complaint and confirmed that it had been forwarded to relevant contacts. Another representative, Pimracha Tukjak, directed Nickles to Thailand's Online Complaint Center 1212 and Technology Crime Suppression Division to report allegedly unlawful website content.[^1]

On April 18, THNIC representative Wassamon Treekul acknowledged a separate complaint concerning [tdac.in.th](../domains/tdac.in.th.md), which Nickles alleged was misleading travelers by presenting itself as an official Thai government resource. Treekul stated that THNIC would review the information and provide updates if further developments occurred. The correspondence confirms THNIC received these complaints but does not establish that it independently verified the allegations or took enforcement action.[^2]

[^1]: Email correspondence with Orapin H. and Pimracha Tukjak, April 13–17, 2025 (private correspondence; case QWK-ZGJNX-001). Disputed URLs: `https://tvc.co.th/seo-fugitive-jesse-nickles-criminal-charges` and `https://asq.in.th/jesse-nickles-seo-operator-wanted-criminal-charges`.
[^2]: Email correspondence with Wassamon Treekul, April 17–18, 2025 (private correspondence; case VRK-ZLCQS-392).
