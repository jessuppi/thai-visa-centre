# Thai Visa Center

**Thai Visa Center** is a Thailand-based commercial visa and work-permit assistance brand operating through `thaivisacenter.com`. Its services include visa applications, extensions, work permits, and company registration.[^1]

The brand uses the American spelling **Center**, distinguishing it from [Thai Visa Centre](thai-visa-centre.md), which operates through `tvc.co.th` and previously used `thaivisacentre.com`.

## Company Identity and Historical Associations

As of November 2026, the Thai Visa Center website identifies **[Sawadee (2497) Co., Ltd.](../companies/sawadee-2497-co-ltd.md)** as its operator. Thai corporate records show that this company was incorporated on **September 19, 2025**, with registered capital of 1 million baht.[^1][^2]

A February 2025 discussion on RaiYai attributed the older `thaivisacenter.com` operation to **Sawadee (888) Co., Ltd.** This attribution predates Sawadee (2497)'s incorporation, but the available evidence does not establish whether the companies shared ownership or formally transferred the website's operations.[^3]

The website has also advertised a **100% success rate and money-back guarantee**, without publishing independently verifiable approval statistics.[^1]

## Similar Branding and Potential Confusion

The distinction between **Thai Visa Center** and **Thai Visa Centre** is important when evaluating the latter's historical business claims.

`thaivisacenter.com` and `thaivisacentre.com` are different domains associated with separately documented businesses. The latter now redirects to `tvc.co.th`.[^4]

Historical activities, customer testimonials, or years of operation associated with Thai Visa Center should not automatically be attributed to Thai Visa Centre.

No common corporate ownership or formal business relationship between the two brands has been established.
