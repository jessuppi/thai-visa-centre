# THNIC Foundation

**Thai Network Information Center Foundation (THNIC Foundation)** is a Thai nonprofit organization responsible for administering Thailand's `.th` and `.ไทย` domain namespaces and establishing their registration policies. On April 1, 2008, the Foundation appointed [T.H.NIC Co., Ltd.](../companies/t-h-nic-co-ltd.md) as the official domain registrar and Thai Name Server Co., Ltd. as the registry operator.[^1]

The Foundation's Domain Name Registration Policy includes provisions governing complaints, bad-faith domain use, phishing, and domain suspension or revocation. These provisions have become central to questions concerning [THNIC](thnic.md)'s handling of abuse complaints submitted in April 2025 and October–November 2026, particularly its failure to explain why its published enforcement powers were not exercised or considered applicable.[^2][^3]

## Formal Complaint Against Registrar Enforcement (November 2026)

On November 12, 2026, Jesse Nickles submitted a formal complaint to the THNIC Foundation requesting an independent review of T.H.NIC Co., Ltd.'s handling of repeated domain abuse reports involving websites associated with [Thai Visa Centre](thai-visa-centre.md) and [Agents Co., Ltd.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md). The complaint cited the registrar's handling of reports dating back to February 2025, including allegations involving `eta.in.th`, `dtv.in.th`, `asq.in.th`, `tdac.in.th`, and `tvc.co.th`.[^4]

Nickles challenged the registrar's November 10, 2026 position that it could not suspend `tvc.co.th` without an explicit court or government order, arguing that this interpretation had not been reconciled with Sections 12.4, 12.5, 14.1.4.2, 14.2, and 17 of the Foundation's published Domain Name Registration Policy. He requested that the Foundation independently examine the registrar's decisions, determine whether the relevant bad-faith-use and information-security provisions had been considered, clarify the circumstances requiring a court order, and explain its oversight procedures.[^4]

**The complaint specifically requested a written determination addressing the registrar's handling of the April 2025 phishing report concerning `tdac.in.th`, as well as the broader pattern of complaints, referrals, and unresolved enforcement questions.** It represented a formal escalation to the organization responsible for the policies the registrar had been asked to enforce.[^4]
