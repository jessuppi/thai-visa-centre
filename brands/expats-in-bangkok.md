# Expats in Bangkok

**Expats in Bangkok** is a public Facebook group for expatriates living in or interested in Bangkok, Thailand. In August 2025, the group hosted a [discussion](../threads/2025-facebook-thai-visa-centre-legitimacy.md) supposedly questioning the legitimacy of Thai Visa Centre, followed by several favorable comments from Facebook accounts that Jesse Nickles suspects were fake profiles or coordinated sockpuppets promoting the agency. Nickles reports being banned from the group after posting criticism of Thai Visa Centre. The alleged coordination and responsibility for the ban remain unverified.
