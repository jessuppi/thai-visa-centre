# Travel Stack Exchange

Travel Stack Exchange is a community-moderated question-and-answer website covering international travel, immigration procedures, visa requirements, and related practical matters.

## July 2026 Mass Deletion of Travel Questions

On July 21, 2026, a Travel Meta Stack Exchange discussion questioned the removal of numerous travel-related questions following the deletion of a contributor's account.[^1]

The identified questions covered:

- Vietnamese visa exemptions for Phu Quoc Island.
- Ferry connections between Cambodia and Vietnam.
- In-country extensions of Thailand's Destination Thailand Visa (DTV).
- Airline footwear restrictions.
- International flights from Hua Hin Airport.

A moderator explained that the moderation team and Stack Exchange administrators strongly suspected that the contributions originated from an individual engaged in persistent platform abuse.

The moderator characterized the resulting enforcement strategy as a **"scorched Earth" campaign**, rather than an individual assessment of each contribution's quality.

## Rejection of Content Preservation

On July 27, another participant proposed preserving useful questions through community undeletion or removing their association with the original contributor.

The moderator rejected this approach, explaining that retaining the material could encourage further prohibited participation.

However, established users were permitted to publish replacement questions and request that existing answers be transferred to them.[^1]

## Contrast With Published Content Preservation Principles

Travel Stack Exchange's general deletion guidance distinguishes between low-quality content and questions containing useful contributions from other community members.[^2]

Its separate guidance concerning author-requested deletion emphasizes preserving valuable answers and identifies account disassociation as a possible alternative to removing useful questions.[^3]

These general principles do not prevent moderators from removing material associated with suspected account abuse.

Nevertheless, the July 2026 discussion establishes that the platform applied an enforcement strategy in which the suspected identity of a contributor took precedence over the independent usefulness of particular travel questions.

## Relevance to Immigration Information

Removing questions concerning visa eligibility and immigration procedures can also remove useful answers supplied by unrelated contributors.

This is particularly relevant to investigations concerning the availability of independent information about Thai immigration services.

The public moderation discussion does not establish that Thai Visa Centre requested the deletions, influenced the moderation team, or had any commercial arrangement with Stack Exchange.

**It does establish that relevant immigration discussions were removed under an acknowledged account-based enforcement strategy rather than an exclusively content-based assessment.**
