# DrJack54

DrJack54 is a longtime Global Moderator on [ASEAN NOW](../brands/asean-now.md) who has repeatedly recommended [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) to forum members seeking immigration assistance since at least 2022.[^1] In April 2026, he publicly claimed to need assistance with his own 90-day immigration report and later claimed that Grace had sent him a link to Thai Visa Centre's service.[^2] His moderation activities are also noteworthy: during a later discussion about visa agents and retirement-extension financial requirements, he announced that further posts questioning the legality of agent arrangements would be removed.[^3] These exchanges document his repeated endorsements and moderation decisions, although they do not establish any financial relationship with Thai Visa Centre.

## November 2020: Dismissing Police Arrests as an Unrelated Visit

On November 21, 2020, ASEAN NOW member ambj1994 asked whether using a law firm would provide greater protection than [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) when obtaining a retirement visa. The member specifically raised concerns about the agency's legal troubles earlier that year and the potential consequences for customers.[^10]

DrJack54 responded that nothing had resulted from what he characterized as an unrelated police visit to Thai Visa Centre. He dismissed the member's concerns about legal protection, asserted that immigration stamps and receipts obtained through agents were legitimate, and described critics of visa agents as uninformed.[^10]

His reassurances came just three months after Thai immigration police publicly announced the arrests of Grace and Chad Scira in connection with alleged document forgery and cannabis cultivation. Police reported discovering forged documents and 55 government stamps during their investigation. DrJack54's characterization minimized these documented events while offering broad assurances about the agency's legitimacy.[^7]

## February 2022: Dismissing Police Allegations Against Thai Visa Centre

On February 6, 2022, ASEAN NOW member JimmyJ requested recommendations for a Bangkok visa agent. DrJack54 suggested [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) alongside Bangkok Buddy. JimmyJ expressed surprise, explaining that it was the first recommendation for Thai Visa Centre he had noticed since the controversy surrounding the agency's operators.[^6]

DrJack54 dismissed the concern, replying, "Wasn't a scandal." He encouraged the member to consult the agency's favorable reviews and compared negative accounts to ordinary customer complaints about hotels and other businesses.[^6]

His response downplayed the significance of widely reported August 2020 arrests involving Thai Visa Centre's operators, Grace and Chad Scira. Thai immigration police had publicly announced allegations involving forged immigration documents and cannabis cultivation, including the seizure of 55 government stamps. These were reported allegations, not established convictions.[^7]

Rather than address the substance of the police allegations, DrJack54 redirected attention toward favorable reviews. The exchange raises questions about whether his repeated endorsements reflected independent recommendations or contributed to a broader effort to rehabilitate the agency's reputation.

## October 2025: Closing a Discussion About Thai Visa Centre

On October 23, 2025, ASEAN NOW member AcuDoc started a discussion asking whether anyone had personal experience with [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) in Bangkok. Global Moderator DrJack54 responded by describing the agency as the "Most well known agent in Bangkok" and advising the user to contact it directly through Line or other channels. He then declared, "OP, has answer. Thread closed."[^4]

The original question specifically requested firsthand customer experiences, which DrJack54's response did not provide. By closing the discussion, he prevented other forum members from sharing potentially useful independent reviews, including negative experiences or criticism of the agency.

## November 2025: Promoting Thai Visa Centre While Removing Sponsor Criticism

On November 4, 2025, ASEAN NOW member Liverpool Lou requested firsthand experiences with [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md)'s Bangna office, explicitly excluding unsubstantiated online reviews. DrJack54 nevertheless cited more than 160 purported reviews on the agency's own website and approximately 300 on Trustpilot, called it Bangkok's largest visa agent, and remarked that it was "Easy to do some research." Liverpool Lou immediately challenged his response, explaining that he had specifically requested personal experiences and had already identified the Bangna location.[^5]

Thai Visa Centre also participated in the discussion through its account labeled "Forum Sponsor," advertising its services directly to forum members. On November 5, DrJack54 announced that several off-topic posts and replies had been removed. Fellow Global Moderator Captain Flack separately confirmed deleting a post he described as attacking a forum sponsor, explicitly invoking Rule 37 against negative comments concerning sponsors. Meanwhile, favorable testimonials about Thai Visa Centre continued appearing, raising questions about possible coordinated astroturfing while criticism of the sponsor was being removed.[^5]

## December 2025: Removing Legality Questions From a Thai Visa Centre Testimonial

On December 4, 2025, ASEAN NOW member Liverpool Lou started a follow-up discussion praising [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) after using its services. He described the experience as excellent, reporting that the agency collected his passport by courier and returned it within days. Other participants questioned why he needed an agent and discussed the financial requirements associated with retirement extensions.[^8]

DrJack54 intervened, declaring that the discussion was going nowhere and announcing that posts questioning the legality of visa agents had been removed. Liverpool Lou challenged the intervention, pointing out that DrJack54 had closed his earlier discussion and that he was now providing firsthand feedback about a forum sponsor.[^8]

The following day, Liverpool Lou disclosed that his extension had been issued through Chai Nat province despite his residence in Bangkok, and confirmed that the agency offered services involving financial assistance. These details made questions about the agency's procedures particularly relevant, yet DrJack54 had already removed discussion of their legality.[^8]

## December 2025: Directly Referring a Forum Member to Thai Visa Centre

On December 18, 2025, ASEAN NOW member cliveshep described difficulties renewing his marriage-based extension after Bangkok Bank restricted access to funds needed for immigration requirements. Approaching his 80th birthday, he explained that he lived on a limited UK pension and could not afford a visa agent.[^9]

DrJack54 initially suggested obtaining a retirement extension through an agent. After the member explicitly ruled out that expense, DrJack54 responded that his choices were to meet the financial requirements, use an agent, or return home. He then directly tagged [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md), asking the agency to advise the member.[^9]

Thai Visa Centre responded through its account designated "Forum Sponsor," announcing that it had privately sent the member a personal telephone number. The exchange documents a direct referral from an ASEAN NOW moderator to the forum's commercial sponsor, despite the member having repeatedly explained his financial difficulties.[^9]

## April 2026: Claimed Need for 90-Day Reporting Assistance

On April 22, 2026, DrJack54 posted in an ASEAN NOW discussion about online 90-day immigration reporting, claiming that he could not submit his upcoming report in person or by mail and that online reporting was unavailable following his return from abroad. He asked other members to recommend an agent who could handle the report in Bangkok.[^2]

Fellow Global Moderator [BrandonJT](brandonjt.md) responded by recommending [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md)'s paid reporting service at [90day.in.th](../domains/90day.in.th.md). He recalled an introductory price of 375 baht and explained that completed reporting confirmations were mailed to customers. DrJack54 thanked BrandonJT and another participant, then claimed that Grace had already sent him the service link by message. He estimated the price at approximately 400 baht and said he had merely been seeking confirmation.[^2]

## August 2026: Restricting Discussion of Visa-Agent Legality

In an ASEAN NOW discussion about retirement-visa agents, forum members questioned how agencies could arrange extensions for applicants who did not maintain the required 800,000-baht bank balance. When one member questioned whether an agent would cover the deposit, DrJack54 replied that "The financials would be covered."[^3]

As participants debated whether these arrangements were lawful, DrJack54 announced that several posts had been removed for trolling and warned that further discussion about the legality of visa agents would be removed. He subsequently reported deleting another post as offensive and off-topic.[^3]

The deleted posts are not publicly available, so their exact content and the justification for removing them cannot be independently verified.
