# Choice Group Asia Co., Ltd.

**Choice Group Asia Co., Ltd.** (บริษัท ช้อยซ์ กรุ๊ป เอเซีย จำกัด) is a Thai media and advertising company incorporated on **April 7, 2011**, under registration number **0205554009008**. Thai corporate records list registered capital of **4 million baht** and classify its business activities under advertising services.[^1]

## March 2014 Advertising Partnership Before the Acquisition

Choice Group Asia's commercial relationship with ThaiVisa.com began before its September 2015 acquisition of the platform.

On March 24, 2014, ThaiVisa announced a joint advertising package with Inspire, a digital media business belonging to Choice Group Asia.[^4]

The advertised package targeted businesses seeking access to expatriate audiences throughout Thailand, with particular emphasis on Bangkok and Pattaya.

The published discussion clarified that the package cost 9,000 baht per month under a six-month contract, representing a total contractual commitment of 54,000 baht.

The announcement claimed a combined newsletter audience exceeding 280,000 subscribers.

Inspire co-founder Dan Cheeseman and ThaiVisa managing director Barry Main described the arrangement as encompassing both commercial advertising and shared promotional content.

## September 2015 Acquisition of ThaiVisa.com

On September 1, 2015, Choice Group Asia announced its acquisition of **ThaiVisa.com and its associated media platforms** from Thaivisa Expat Group Co., Ltd.[^2]

The acquisition expanded a media portfolio that included Inspire Digital Media Group and Pattaya Channel TV. **Dan Cheeseman** was appointed managing director of the acquired ThaiVisa Media Group.[^2]

ThaiVisa.com subsequently developed into [ASEAN NOW](../brands/asean-now.md), retaining its established expatriate discussion forums and immigration-related content.

## Expansion of the Commercial Media Network

Following its acquisition of ThaiVisa in September 2015, Choice Group Asia continued expanding its commercial media portfolio.

On May 29, 2017, the company announced a partnership with Siam Media Solutions to distribute Livv Thailand Magazine across Bangkok, Pattaya, and Hua Hin, targeting tourists through hotels, airports, shopping centers, and other commercial venues.[^5]

These historical arrangements demonstrate that coordinated advertising across multiple publications was an established element of ThaiVisa's business model well before The Thaiger's 2024 acquisition.

The Thaiger's subsequent joint advertising packages continued this broader commercial approach under new ownership.

**The historical distinction is important because the commercialization of ThaiVisa's expatriate audience predates its rebranding as ASEAN NOW and its later sponsorship relationship with Thai Visa Centre.**

The available evidence does not establish that Thai Visa Centre participated in the 2014 advertising partnership or the 2017 magazine venture.

## April 2024 Sale to The Thaiger

On April 19, 2024, [The Thaiger](../brands/the-thaiger.md) announced that it had acquired **ASEAN NOW's digital assets from Choice Group Asia Co., Ltd.** The announcement described a combined audience of approximately 5 million monthly users and confirmed plans to retain the forum's existing moderators and staff.[^3]

The transaction transferred control of one of Thailand's largest English-language expatriate communities to a publisher that subsequently offered joint advertising packages across both platforms.

This ownership history is relevant to the investigation into [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md), which later advertised as an ASEAN NOW forum sponsor and benefited from moderation policies restricting criticism of advertisers.

The documented transaction involved ASEAN NOW's digital assets rather than an acquisition of Choice Group Asia itself.

## 2020 Advertorial Sales and Targeted Visa Agency Promotions

On March 3, 2020, during Choice Group Asia's ownership of ThaiVisa.com, the platform's sales team published a discounted advertising offer costing 6,000 baht per month.[^6]

The package included pinned advertorials, newsletter placements, and Facebook promotions.

The sales announcement explicitly described the advantage of advertorials:

> They are perceived by many as a genuine article, not so much an advert.

The publisher also promoted the permanent search-engine visibility of these commercial publications and invited advertisers to supply their own headlines, promotional content, photographs, pricing information, and destination URLs.

This establishes that the perceived editorial authenticity of paid content was explicitly marketed as a commercial advantage.

### August 2020 Advertising Campaign Targeting Visa Agencies

On August 14, 2020, ThaiVisa's sales team announced a dedicated 45-day advertising package exclusively targeting visa agencies, priced at 7,500 baht.[^7]

The campaign included:

- Two newsletter placements, targeting a claimed 335,000 subscribers.
- Two Facebook promotions.
- Two promotional posts in regional discussion forums selected by the advertiser.

The announcement encouraged visa agencies to capitalize on increased demand surrounding the approaching expiration of Thailand's pandemic-era visa amnesty.

The campaign was announced just ten days after the August 4, 2020 police raid involving Thai Visa Centre's founders.

On September 15, 2020, ThaiVisa subsequently published an advertorial specifically promoting Thai Visa Centre, as documented in the separate [ASEAN NOW domain investigation](../domains/aseannow.com.md).

The available evidence does not establish whether Thai Visa Centre purchased the specific August advertising package.

Nevertheless, these historical announcements document a deliberate commercial strategy of selling editorial-style exposure and search-engine visibility to businesses, including immigration agencies.

This strategy predates The Thaiger's acquisition of ASEAN NOW by four years and provides important historical context for the platform's subsequent sponsor-protection policies and commercial immigration coverage.
