# T.H.NIC Co., Ltd.

**T.H.NIC Co., Ltd.** is Thailand's sole registrar for `.th` and `.ไทย` domain names, appointed by the [THNIC Foundation](../brands/thnic-foundation.md) on April 1, 2008. Operating under the [THNIC](../brands/thnic.md) name, the company administers domain registrations and is responsible for applying the Foundation's registration policies, including provisions governing domain abuse, bad-faith use, and suspension or revocation.[^1][^2]

Beginning February 27, 2025, T.H.NIC Co., Ltd. received repeated domain abuse complaints from Jesse Nickles concerning websites associated with [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) and [Agents Co., Ltd.](agents-co-ltd.md). The complaints involved alleged government impersonation, misleading domain registrations, unsolicited commercial advertising, and disputed publications about Nickles. Although THNIC representatives acknowledged several complaints and promised reviews, the available correspondence left substantial questions unanswered concerning the registrar's investigations, enforcement decisions, and application of its published policies.[^3]

On November 12, 2026, Nickles submitted four additional formal complaints requesting policy reviews concerning [tdac.in.th](../domains/tdac.in.th.md), [agents.co.th](../domains/agents.co.th.md) and its TDAC subdomain, [90day.in.th](../domains/90day.in.th.md), and seven immigration-related domain registrations. He separately requested that the [THNIC Foundation](../brands/thnic-foundation.md) independently examine the registrar's handling of complaints dating back to February 2025. These submissions sought documented policy determinations, clarification of enforcement powers, and accountability for previously unresolved cases.[^4]
