# T.H.NIC Co., Ltd.

**T.H.NIC Co., Ltd.** is Thailand's sole registrar for `.th` and `.ไทย` domain names, appointed by the [THNIC Foundation](../brands/thnic-foundation.md) on April 1, 2008. Operating under the [THNIC](../brands/thnic.md) name, the company administers domain registrations and is responsible for applying the Foundation's registration policies, including provisions governing domain abuse, bad-faith use, and suspension or revocation.[^1][^2]

In April 2025 and October–November 2026, T.H.NIC Co., Ltd. received multiple complaints from Jesse Nickles concerning allegedly unlawful activity involving [tvc.co.th](../domains/tvc.co.th.md), [asq.in.th](../domains/asq.in.th.md), and [tdac.in.th](../domains/tdac.in.th.md). Although representatives acknowledged the complaints and promised reviews, the company's responses left significant questions unanswered about its enforcement decisions and application of its published policies.[^3]
