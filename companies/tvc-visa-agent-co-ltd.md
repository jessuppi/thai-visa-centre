# TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd.

Thai business-record directories identify **TVC. VISA AGENT CO., LTD.** (บริษัท ทีวีซี. วีซ่า เอเจนท์ จำกัด) as a company registered in Thailand on **August 18, 2020**, under company registration number **0115563017485**. The company was registered just two weeks after Thai immigration police publicly announced the August 4, 2020 [raid involving Thai Visa Centre](https://www.nationthailand.com/in-focus/30392449).

The directories list initial registered capital of **6,000,000 baht**, still reported as of August 2026, and describe business activities involving brokerage, digital advertising, translation, and document services.

The company's registered address, as reported in the business directories, matches the office address published for [Agents Co., Ltd.](agents-co-ltd.md), a separately registered company, on the [90day.in.th](../domains/90day.in.th.md) contact page.

The shared address establishes a documented location connection but does not independently prove common ownership, corporate control, or responsibility for the [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) brand.
