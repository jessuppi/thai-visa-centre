# TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd.

**TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd.** (บริษัท ทีวีซี. วีซ่า เอเจนท์ จำกัด) is a Thai company incorporated on **August 18, 2020**, under registration number **0115563017485**, with registered capital of **6 million baht**.[^1]

The company was incorporated just 14 days after Thai immigration police announced the August 4, 2020 raid involving [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md), [Chad Scira](../people/chad-scira.md), and Grace Scira.[^2]

Corporate records list brokerage, advertising, translation, and document services among its business activities.

The company also shares a documented business location with [Agents Co., Ltd.](agents-co-ltd.md), a separately registered company that Thai Visa Centre identifies as the operator of its digital immigration platforms.[^3]

## Domain Registration and Corporate Identity

In November 2026, [THNIC](../brands/thnic.md) representative [Suthida Tepaloon](../people/suthida-tepaloon.md) defended the registration of [`tvc.co.th`](../domains/tvc.co.th.md), explaining that the domain corresponded to the legally registered name TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd.

However, THNIC did not establish that this company was responsible for the services advertised through the website.

Thai Visa Centre's own website identifies its physical visa operation as **Thai Visa Centre**, while naming AGENTS CO., LTD. as its digital operations company.

No Thai company registered under the exact legal name Thai Visa Centre has been identified in this investigation.

## Unidentified Contracting Company

The Terms & Conditions published on `tvc.co.th`, updated February 9, 2025, explicitly describe the agreement as a legally binding contract between customers and **"THAI VISA CENTRE."**[^4]

The document does not identify TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd., supply its registration number, or clarify which registered company assumes contractual responsibility for customers purchasing immigration services.

The website's Privacy Policy likewise identifies **THAI VISA CENTRE** as the contracting party, while its Data Protection Policy goes further by referring to the trade name as **"the Company."**[^5][^6]

**This creates a basic corporate-accountability problem.** The registered company used to justify the domain's existence is not identified in the website's own customer agreements.

Customers are asked to enter legally binding arrangements with a business name that the investigation has not identified as a separately registered legal entity.

## Restrictive Liability Terms

The same Terms & Conditions contain extensive provisions limiting the operator's financial responsibility.

The agreement attempts to cap aggregate liability at **the greater of US$1 or the amount the customer actually paid during the preceding month**.[^4]

For customers whose payments fall outside that period, the contractual limitation could reduce the operator's claimed liability to just US$1, subject to applicable law.

The agreement also requires customers to waive claims against Thai Visa Centre arising from third-party services, despite the agency relying on external service providers and immigration authorities to complete its advertised work.[^4]

Meanwhile, the website's Refund Policy promises that visa-agent service fees are **100% refundable when applications are denied**, while distinguishing nonrefundable government charges.[^7]

Neither document explains how the broad liability limitation interacts with the refund promise. The refund language also concentrates on applications submitted through embassies and consulates without clearly addressing the agency's substantial domestic retirement-extension business.

**Thai Visa Centre advertises refundable, contract-backed immigration services while its published terms attempt to severely restrict financial liability and fail to identify the registered company responsible for those contracts.**

## Privacy Responsibility

The website's Privacy Policy states that Thai Visa Centre acts as both a data controller and processor, depending on the circumstances.[^5]

However, the document does not identify whether TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd., AGENTS CO., LTD., or another legal entity is responsible for customer records collected through the physical visa agency.

Its separate Data Protection Policy also describes the company's business as centered on **travel and accommodation**, rather than its advertised visa and immigration services.[^6]

These inconsistencies are significant because the agency handles sensitive customer documentation, including passports, financial records, and immigration applications.

The published policies leave customers without a clear explanation of which registered company assumes contractual liability and personal-data protection responsibilities.

## THNIC Complaints

During October–November 2026, Jesse Nickles submitted complaints to THNIC concerning allegedly defamatory material and unauthorized photographs published on `tvc.co.th`.

On October 29, THNIC confirmed that it had requested the domain registrant to review the disputed material.

On November 10, THNIC maintained that the domain complied with registration requirements but did not identify the registrant or establish whether TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd. was responsible for the disputed publication.

The correspondence is documented in the [tvc.co.th domain investigation](../domains/tvc.co.th.md).
