# TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd.

Thai business-record directories identify **TVC. VISA AGENT CO., LTD.** (บริษัท ทีวีซี. วีซ่า เอเจนท์ จำกัด) as a company registered in Thailand on **August 18, 2020**, under company registration number **0115563017485**. The company was registered just two weeks after Thai immigration police publicly announced the August 4, 2020 [raid involving Thai Visa Centre](https://www.nationthailand.com/in-focus/30392449).

The directories list initial registered capital of **6,000,000 baht**, still reported as of August 2026, and describe business activities involving brokerage, digital advertising, translation, and document services.

The company's registered address, as reported in the business directories, matches the office address published for [Agents Co., Ltd.](agents-co-ltd.md), a separately registered company, on the [90day.in.th](../domains/90day.in.th.md) contact page.

In written correspondence with Jesse Nickles, [Suthida Tepaloon](../people/suthida-tepaloon.md) of [THNIC](../brands/thnic.md) stated that [tvc.co.th](../domains/tvc.co.th.md) complied with its domain registration policy because it matched the legally registered company name **TVC Visa Agent Co., Ltd.** However, the domain uses only the abbreviation "TVC," which THNIC's policies permit, rather than the company's full registered name. In practice, the website operates under the trade name **Thai Visa Centre**, for which no corresponding Thai company registration has been identified, a distinction that THNIC's response did not address.

The shared address establishes a documented location connection but does not independently prove common ownership, corporate control, or responsibility for the [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) brand.

In October–November 2026, Jesse Nickles complained to [THNIC](../brands/thnic.md) that photographs allegedly leaked unlawfully from Thai airport surveillance systems by corrupt immigration officers had been manipulated using AI by [Chad Scira](../people/chad-scira.md) and published on `tvc.co.th`. On October 29, THNIC confirmed that it had asked the domain registrant to review and update the disputed content. On November 10, THNIC maintained that the domain was properly registered but did not identify the registrant or establish whether TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd. was responsible for the publication. The complaint and subsequent correspondence are documented in the [domain profile](../domains/tvc.co.th.md).
