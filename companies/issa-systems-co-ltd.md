# Issa Systems Co., Ltd.

**Issa Systems Co., Ltd.** (บริษัท อิสสะ ซิสเต็มส์ จำกัด) is a Thai company incorporated on **December 2, 2022**, under registration number **0105565194414**.

Its registered capital increased from **1 million baht to 4 million baht on May 21, 2026**. The company remains active.[^1]

## Conflicting Business Classifications

Thai corporate records identify immigration consulting among the company's registered activities.

However, its latest financial-statement business description concerns **training employees for restaurants and other service businesses**, classified under conference organization rather than immigration consulting.[^1]

The discrepancy raises questions about how the company's reported activities correspond to its commercial visa services.

## Issa Compass Operations

Issa Systems is one of two companies publicly identified as operating **[Issa Compass](../brands/issa-compass.md)**, alongside Singapore-registered [Issara Platforms Pte. Ltd.](issara-platforms-pte-ltd.md).[^2]

Issa Compass advertises visa applications, document preparation, immigration consultations, and related services across Southeast Asia.

## Customer Data and Singapore Operations

Issa Compass's Privacy Policy identifies **Issara Platforms Pte. Ltd. as the controller of customers' digital visa files**, while Issa Systems handles in-person services and physical documents in Thailand.[^3]

The policy states that customer data is stored primarily in Singapore and accessed from both Singapore and Thailand. Collected information includes passport copies, financial documents, and potentially sensitive health or criminal records.[^3]

This arrangement places responsibility for digital immigration records with the Singapore company rather than the Thai operating entity.
