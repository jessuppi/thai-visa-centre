# Si Phya Publishing Co., Ltd.

**Si Phya Publishing Co., Ltd.** (บริษัท สี่พระยาการพิมพ์ จำกัด), officially registered in English as **SIPHAYA PUBLISHING COMPANY LIMITED**, is a Thai media company incorporated on **July 25, 1963**, under registration number **0105506001541**.

Its registered capital increased to **1 billion baht on February 8, 2006**. Corporate records identify newspaper publishing and distribution as its principal business activities.[^1]

The company publishes Daily News (เดลินิวส์) and operates its associated news website, [dailynews.co.th](../domains/dailynews.co.th.md).

## Professional Media Accountability

Thailand's National Press Council identifies both Daily News and Daily News Online as member publications in its 2024 report concerning professional media ethics.[^2]

The council's professional ethics regulations, issued on June 8, 2021, establish several relevant obligations:[^3]

| Clause | Professional obligation |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Verify reporting and avoid distorting factual information. |
| 8 | Publish information in good faith without pursuing improper personal or group interests. |
| 23 | Correct reporting errors without delay. |
| 30 | Clearly distinguish advertising and revenue-generating material from news coverage. |

These professional standards provide a framework for examining the publisher's contradictory reporting concerning Thailand's Digital Arrival Card (TDAC).

## Uncorrected Immigration Registration Guidance

On April 12, 2025, Daily News published immigration guidance identifying both the government's official TDAC portal and the privately operated `tdac.in.th` website as designated registration channels.[^4]

On March 29, 2026, the newspaper published a separate warning identifying the government portal as the only official registration website.[^5]

Both publications remained publicly accessible when examined on November 25, 2026.

The earlier article continued displaying the private website's link without an accompanying correction explaining its commercial status.

The detailed discrepancy is documented in the [Daily News domain investigation](../domains/dailynews.co.th.md).

**Clause 23 of the National Press Council's ethics regulations provides a particularly relevant professional standard for evaluating the continued publication of this incorrect immigration guidance.**

## Public-Interest Exception to Complaint Deadlines

The National Press Council's charter establishes procedures for submitting complaints to member publications and escalating unresolved matters to the council.[^6]

Clause 23 of the charter generally excludes complaints submitted more than 120 days after the affected individual became aware of the matter, or more than one year after the relevant event.

However, it expressly permits exceptions where the council considers a matter important to the public interest or affecting the standing of the media profession.

The continued availability of incorrect guidance concerning a mandatory immigration procedure provides a potential public-interest basis for requesting examination of the matter.

Acceptance of such a complaint remains within the council's authority.

## Unresolved Editorial Responsibility

The available evidence does not establish that Si Phya Publishing received compensation from Thai Visa Centre or AGENTS CO., LTD., or that its management deliberately promoted the private TDAC website.

Nevertheless, the publisher's documented professional affiliations provide an identifiable accountability mechanism through which the accuracy of its immigration reporting and its handling of corrections can be examined.
