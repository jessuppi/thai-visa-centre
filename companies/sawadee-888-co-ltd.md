# Sawadee (888) Co., Ltd.

**Sawadee (888) Co., Ltd.** is a Thai company name historically associated with visa and work permit services in Bangkok. The website [sawadeevisa.com](https://www.sawadeevisa.com/) identifies the company in its footer and advertises visa extensions, work permits, immigration assistance, and company registration services.

A [February 2, 2025 discussion on RaiYai](https://www.raiyai.com/forum/topic/difference-between-thai-visa-center-and-thai-visa-centre/) also identified Sawadee (888) Co., Ltd. as the operator of [thaivisacenter.com](https://www.thaivisacenter.com/), which uses the **Thai Visa Center** name with American spelling.

As of November 7, 2026, `thaivisacenter.com` instead identifies [Sawadee (2497) Co., Ltd.](sawadee-2497-co-ltd.md) in its footer and contact information. Both company names have appeared in connection with the same business location, suggesting a historical relationship that warrants further investigation.
