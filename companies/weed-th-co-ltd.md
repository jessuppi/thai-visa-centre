# WEED.TH Co., Ltd.

**WEED.TH Co., Ltd.** (บริษัท วีด.ทีเอช จำกัด) is a Thai company incorporated on **February 16, 2023**, under registration number **0115566005267**, with registered capital of **500,000 baht**.[^1]

The company operates the cannabis marketplace [WEED.TH](../domains/weed.th.md), offering dispensary listings, commercial software, and medical cannabis card services.[^2]

## Corporate Activities

Thai corporate records classify the company under **wholesale of industrial machinery and equipment**, with registered activities involving cannabis and hemp cultivation equipment.[^1]

Its public-facing operations extend considerably beyond that description, including medical consultations, prescription services, and a private cannabis membership card.

The company acknowledges that its cannabis card is not a government-issued medical credential and cannot replace a valid prescription.[^3]

## Questionable Privacy Documentation

WEED.TH's published Data Protection Policy describes its business activities as centered on **travel and accommodation**, despite the company operating a cannabis and medical-services platform.[^4]

This apparent copied-policy language raises questions about the care taken in preparing its privacy disclosures, particularly given the medical information and identification documents collected through its consultation services.
