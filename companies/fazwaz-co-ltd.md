# FazWaz (Thailand) Co., Ltd.

**FazWaz (Thailand) Co., Ltd.** is a Thai company incorporated on **March 9, 2018**, under registration number **0105561044787**.

Originally registered as **RE Marketing Co., Ltd.**, the company adopted its current name on **October 2, 2020**. Its registered capital increased to **10 million baht in February 2021**.[^1]

Thai corporate records classify its activities under advertising representation and web hosting, despite the FazWaz brand's focus on real estate services.[^1]

## Acquisition by LIFULL and Dot Property

On February 13, 2023, Japan's publicly listed **LIFULL Corporation** announced an agreement for its subsidiary **Dot Property Co., Ltd.** to acquire 100% of FazWaz (Thailand) from Singapore-based FazWaz Pte. Ltd.[^2]

LIFULL identified FazWaz's technology, customer acquisition systems, and real estate brokerage network as reasons for the acquisition.

FazWaz is now identified as part of LIFULL Connect in the group's published legal documentation.[^3]

## Connections to The Thaiger

[Digital Broker Ventures](../brands/digital-broker-ventures.md), previously involved in developing [The Thaiger](../brands/the-thaiger.md), also identifies FazWaz as a major portfolio project.

Its published portfolio describes developing FazWaz's SEO infrastructure, lead-generation systems, and property news content, including collaborations with The Thaiger.[^4]

In 2026, [The Thaiger Pte. Ltd.](the-thaiger-pte-ltd.md) disclosed that it had become wholly owned by **Dot Property Pte. Ltd.**, also within LIFULL Connect.[^5]

These transactions place FazWaz and The Thaiger within the same corporate group, adding to their documented history of shared technology development, property advertising, and commercial publishing.

## January 2025 Transfer of LIFULL Connect to FazWaz Founders

Although LIFULL acquired FazWaz's Thai operations in 2023, a subsequent corporate restructuring transferred voting control of its international property network to the founders of FazWaz.

On January 17, 2025, LIFULL's board approved the transfer of its entire shareholding in LIFULL CONNECT S.L.U. to a newly established Singapore holding company, **CONNECT NEXT PTE. LTD.**[^6]

The transaction was completed on January 21, 2025, as confirmed in a subsequent announcement to investors.[^7]

### Ownership and Voting Rights

The transaction established the following ownership structure:

| Shareholder of CONNECT NEXT | Shareholding | Voting rights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LIFULL Co., Ltd. | 55% | 0% |
| FazWaz founders | 45% | 100% |

CONNECT NEXT acquired 100% of LIFULL Connect.

The transaction involved no cash consideration. Instead, LIFULL received non-voting preferred shares in the Singapore holding company.

Consequently, LIFULL Connect ceased to be consolidated within the Japanese company's financial statements.[^6][^7]

### Michael Kenner's Corporate Role

The January 17 announcement identifies CONNECT NEXT as a Singapore holding company established on December 17, 2024, with **Michael Stuart Bernard Kenner** serving as chief executive officer.

Before the transaction, the company was wholly owned by FWG HOLDINGS PTE. LTD.

The announcement also identifies Kenner as a FazWaz co-founder and director of both CONNECT NEXT and LIFULL Connect.[^6]

Kenner is separately associated with [Digital Broker Ventures](../brands/digital-broker-ventures.md), which participated in the development of The Thaiger and FazWaz.

International law firm DFDL, which advised on the Vietnamese aspects of the transaction, subsequently confirmed the separation between financial ownership and voting control.[^8]

### Relevance to The Thaiger and ASEAN NOW

In 2026, The Thaiger disclosed that its Singapore operating company had become wholly owned by Dot Property Pte. Ltd., part of the LIFULL Connect group.[^5]

The January 2025 restructuring therefore provides important context for understanding the subsequent ownership of both The Thaiger and ASEAN NOW.

The group's published legal documentation also identifies FazWaz (Thailand) Co., Ltd. as the operating company behind **Thaiger Property**, establishing a separate commercial relationship between the property business and the Thaiger brand.[^3]

The available corporate records establish shared commercial interests and the voting structure created in January 2025. They do not establish that Kenner or other FazWaz founders personally directed immigration-related advertising, forum moderation, or editorial decisions.

The precise current ownership of every intermediate subsidiary and any subsequent changes to the holding company's shareholding remain unverified.
