# Issara Platforms Pte. Ltd.

**Issara Platforms Pte. Ltd.** is a Singapore company incorporated on **February 14, 2023**, under registration number **202305342W**.

Singapore corporate records classify its primary activity as software and application development, with human-resource management as a secondary activity.[^1]

The company operates the [Issa Compass](../brands/issa-compass.md) visa assistance platform alongside Thailand's [Issa Systems Co., Ltd.](issa-systems-co-ltd.md).[^2]

## Control of Customer Immigration Records

Issa Compass's Privacy Policy identifies **Issara Platforms as the controller of customers' digital visa files**, while Issa Systems handles in-person services and physical documents in Thailand.[^3]

The Singapore company processes sensitive immigration information, including passport copies, financial records, and potentially medical or criminal-history documents.

Customer information is stored primarily in Singapore but accessed from both Singapore and Thailand. Some service providers may process information in additional countries.[^3]

## Security and Data Retention

Issa Compass advertises bank-grade encryption, Google Cloud hosting, and compliance with personal-data protection regulations.[^4]

These are the company's published security claims, not independently verified findings about its implementation.

Its Privacy Policy promises account and file deletion upon closure, subject to exceptions for payment, refund, tax, dispute, and other legally required records. **It does not specify a fixed retention period for ordinary customer visa files.**[^3]

These disclosures are relevant to evaluating the protection of sensitive immigration documents handled across multiple jurisdictions.

## Unsubstantiated Monthly Application Volumes and Inconsistent Customer Statistics

On April 9, 2026, Issa Compass announced that it had surpassed **100,000 users**. The same press release described 100,000 consultations, claimed a **99% visa approval rate for pre-qualified applications**, and reported reaching $1 million in annualized revenue within five months.[^5]

However, an October 9 press release described the platform as having served **more than 10,000 clients**. Less than a month later, on November 5, another promotional release claimed the company served **more than 100,000 clients**.[^6][^7]

These statements use substantially different measures of the business's reach. The earlier 100,000 figure referred to users or consultations, while the later announcement explicitly described 100,000 clients. Neither release explains how clients are counted or reconciles the figures.

Issa Compass's own About page further advertises having helped **100,000+ expatriates**, while elsewhere stating that its advisors have helped more than 1,000 expatriates.[^8]

The figures could represent different categories of engagement, but the marketing does not clearly distinguish registrations, automated consultations, paying customers, and completed visa cases.

### Monthly Users Versus Visa Applications

Issa Compass's own immigration guides introduce another distinction between platform usage and application-processing volumes.

Its article *Thailand's Growing Expat Economy: Immigration Insights from Issa Compass's 10,000+ Monthly Users*, published April 15, 2026 and updated November 14, 2026, describes data collected from more than 10,000 monthly platform users. It also refers to inbound users when discussing application trends.[^9]

However, its separate guide explaining how it prepares applications before embassy submission describes the platform as processing applications for more than 10,000 expatriates each month. The same article repeats the figure when describing its application-preparation services.[^10]

The difference is significant because a platform user, consultation recipient, paying client, application in preparation, and application actually submitted to government authorities are not necessarily equivalent.

| Published source | Claimed volume |
| --- | --- |
| April 9, 2026 press release | More than 100,000 users; 100,000 consultations.[^5] |
| Expat economy article, published April 15, 2026 | More than 10,000 monthly platform users.[^9] |
| Application-preparation guide | Applications processed for more than 10,000 expatriates monthly.[^10] |
| October 9, 2026 press release | More than 10,000 clients served.[^6] |
| November 5, 2026 press release | More than 100,000 clients served.[^7] |

The monthly figure would imply an annualized volume exceeding 120,000 if maintained for a full year, but that is an illustration of scale, not a verified annual total.

The October announcement's lower-bound figure of more than 10,000 clients does not mathematically exclude a much higher total. Nor does monthly platform usage establish the number of distinct customers served over a year, since the same person may use the service in multiple months.

**The publications nevertheless use similar numerical claims to describe materially different measures of activity without explaining the relationship between them.**

In particular, the application-processing claim does not establish that the company submits more than 10,000 applications to immigration authorities each month, or that those applications represent paying customers or completed visa decisions.

### Unverified Approval Rate

The company's repeatedly advertised **99% approval rate** is also difficult to assess independently.

Issa Compass restricts the statistic to applications it has already pre-qualified, rather than all applicants seeking assistance. It does not publicly disclose the total number of applications included, the number rejected during pre-qualification, or an independently audited record of visa decisions.[^5]

**The problem is not simply the size of these numbers, but how they are presented as evidence of credibility.** Issa Compass promotes impressive customer volumes and approval statistics without supplying the underlying records needed to establish what those figures actually represent.

### Issa Compass's Own Standard for Verifiable Experience

The company's consumer guide *How to Verify a Thailand Visa Service's Credentials Before You Hand Over Your Passport and Personal Documents* advises customers to look for independently verifiable reviews, accountable service providers, and a track record that can be independently checked.[^11]

Public reviews can help consumers evaluate individual experiences, but their existence does not independently establish monthly application volumes, total paying customers, or an approval-rate denominator.

To substantiate its promotional statistics, Issa Compass would need to explain:

- Whether monthly users are unique active users, prospective applicants, consultation recipients, or customers with active applications.
- Whether application volumes include self-service users, unfinished applications, repeat applications, or only submissions to government authorities.
- How cumulative client figures differ from platform registrations and consultations.
- How many pre-qualified applications received government decisions during the period used to calculate its 99% approval rate, and how customers excluded during pre-qualification are counted.

The reviewed publications do not supply this reconciliation. The company's own consumer advice therefore provides a relevant standard against which to evaluate the transparency of its marketing.

**The available evidence establishes inconsistent terminology and insufficiently substantiated performance statistics, not that the figures were fabricated or that the company failed to serve the stated number of users.**
