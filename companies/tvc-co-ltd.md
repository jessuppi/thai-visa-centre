# TVC Co., Ltd.

**TVC CO., LTD.** (บริษัท ทีวีซี จำกัด) was a Thai limited company incorporated on **October 1, 2025**, under registration number **0105568195331**, with registered capital of **1,000,000 baht**.[^1]

The company was registered in Bangkok's Chatuchak district. Its declared business activities involved **internet hosting, livestreaming, and electronic broadcasting services**, rather than visa or immigration assistance.[^1][^2]

## Registration and Dissolution

According to Creden Data's corporate records, TVC CO., LTD. was **dissolved on March 24, 2026**, less than six months after incorporation. Its liquidation was recorded as completed on **May 8, 2026**.[^2]

The company's registration and closure occurred during a period when [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) was expanding its network of immigration-related websites and digital services.

## Relationship to Thai Visa Centre

TVC CO., LTD. should be distinguished from [TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd.](tvc-visa-agent-co-ltd.md), registration number **0115563017485**, which was incorporated on **August 18, 2020**, with registered capital of **6,000,000 baht**.[^3]

The latter company has been identified by [THNIC](../brands/thnic.md) in connection with the `tvc.co.th` domain, while [Agents Co., Ltd.](agents-co-ltd.md) has been publicly identified as the operator of Thai Visa Centre's digital immigration platforms.[^4]

Although the similar company names warrant examination, **the available records do not establish whether TVC CO., LTD. shared ownership or management with these businesses**. Historical shareholder and director filings would be needed to establish any corporate relationship.
