# Sawadee (2497) Co., Ltd.

**Sawadee (2497) Co., Ltd.** (บริษัท สวัสดี(2497) จำกัด) is a Thai company incorporated on September 19, 2025, with visa and work-permit consulting among its registered business activities. The company is publicly identified on `thaivisacenter.com`, which operates under the **Thai Visa Center** name.[^1][^2]

## Corporate records

Thai corporate records list Sawadee (2497) Co., Ltd. as an active company with registered capital of ฿1 million. Its registered business activities include providing consulting services relating to visas and work permits.[^1]

The company's registered office is in Bangkok's Wang Thonglang district. The `thaivisacenter.com` website advertises a different street address within the same district.[^1][^2]

## Website association

As of November 7, 2026, `thaivisacenter.com` explicitly identifies **Sawadee (2497) Co., Ltd.** in its website footer.[^2]

The website advertises immigration-related services, including visa applications, extensions, work permits, and company registration. It uses the **Thai Visa Center** branding, with the American spelling of "Center," rather than **Thai Visa Centre**.[^2]

The website also contains promotional content and customer references resembling material published on `sawadeevisa.com`. These similarities are documented in the separate profile of [Sawadee (888) Co., Ltd.](sawadee-888-co-ltd.md).

## Historical connections

A February 2, 2025 discussion on RaiYai identified [Sawadee (888) Co., Ltd.](sawadee-888-co-ltd.md) as the company behind `thaivisacenter.com`.[^3]

That discussion predates the incorporation of Sawadee (2497) Co., Ltd. by more than seven months. The earlier attribution and the website's subsequent identification of Sawadee (2497) document different public associations over time.

However, the forum attribution has not been independently established, and the available records do not demonstrate a formal transfer of operations, common ownership, or legal succession between the companies.

Sawadee (2497) is also legally distinct from [Sawadeevisa (888) Co., Ltd.](sawadeevisa-888-co-ltd.md), which was incorporated in 2016 under the name Kindex Co., Ltd.
