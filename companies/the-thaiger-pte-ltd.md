# The Thaiger Pte. Ltd.

**The Thaiger Pte. Ltd.** is a Singapore company incorporated on **May 22, 2019**, under registration number **201916373W**.[^1]

The company's published Terms of Use identify it as the legal operator of [The Thaiger](../brands/the-thaiger.md), a Thailand-focused digital news publisher.[^2]

## ASEAN NOW Acquisition and Advertising

On April 19, 2024, The Thaiger announced its acquisition of [ASEAN NOW](../brands/asean-now.md) from **Choice Group Asia Co., Ltd.** The announcement did not identify which legal entity completed the acquisition.[^3]

Following the acquisition, The Thaiger advertised joint promotional packages across both platforms, including sponsored articles, pinned forum discussions, and commercial announcements.[^4]

During 2025, both publications promoted immigration services associated with [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md). ASEAN NOW moderators also publicly acknowledged removing criticism of forum sponsors.

## Visa Referrals and Customer Data

The Thaiger's Terms of Use explicitly include **visa assistance** among its third-party referral services.[^2]

Its Privacy Policy permits sharing customer information with service providers following inquiries, analyzing communications, and using personal information for marketing purposes.[^5]

These arrangements raise questions about **commercial influence over immigration coverage, undisclosed referral relationships, and the handling of customer information**.

The published policies establish that visa referrals form part of the publisher's commercial activities, although they do not identify the recipients of individual customer inquiries.
