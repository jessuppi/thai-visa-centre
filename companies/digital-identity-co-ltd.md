# Digital Identity Co., Ltd.

**Digital Identity Co., Ltd.** (บริษัท ดิจิทัล ไอเด็นติตี้ จำกัด) is a Thai technology company incorporated on **November 13, 2024**, under registration number **0135567034490**. Its registered activities include identity verification, passport authentication, and facial recognition services.[^1]

The company's initial registered capital was **1 million baht**, subsequently increased to **10 million baht on April 28, 2026**.[^1]

## Corporate Ownership and Share Transfers

A November 7, 2024 Stock Exchange of Thailand filing identified Digital Identity as a joint venture between:

- **KYC Now Co., Ltd. — 75%**
- **East Coast Furnitech PCL — 25%**

The filing described plans to develop identity-verification technology, including passport and facial verification systems.[^2]

On **May 15, 2025**, East Coast Furnitech sold its entire 25% holding to KYC Now. The transaction was publicly disclosed the following day, establishing that KYC Now acquired the remaining shares previously held by the publicly listed company.[^3]

## THIM Immigration Platform

On May 28, 2026, Amazon Web Services announced that Digital Identity had developed the **Thailand Immigration Management System (THIM)** in partnership with Thailand's Immigration Bureau, using AWS infrastructure in Bangkok.[^4]

THIM's published Privacy Notice identifies Digital Identity as its application developer and technical service provider. It separately names **KYC Now Co., Ltd. as a processor handling biometric and document verification**.[^5]

The system processes sensitive immigration information, including passport records, facial biometric templates, and electronic passport-chip data.[^5]

Separately, [Agents Co., Ltd.](agents-co-ltd.md), which is associated with [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md), registered the domain [thim.in.th](../domains/thim.in.th.md) on May 29, 2026, and used it to promote paid TDAC submission services.

The timing and shared THIM branding warrant examination, although **no corporate or contractual relationship between Digital Identity and Agents Co., Ltd. has been established**.
