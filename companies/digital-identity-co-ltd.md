# Digital Identity Co., Ltd.

**Digital Identity Co., Ltd.** (บริษัท ดิจิทัล ไอเด็นติตี้ จำกัด) is a Thai technology company incorporated on **November 13, 2024**, under registration number **0135567034490**. Its registered activities include identity verification, passport authentication, and facial recognition services.[^1]

The company's initial registered capital was **1 million baht**, subsequently increased to **10 million baht on April 28, 2026**.[^1]

## Corporate Ownership and Share Transfers

A November 7, 2024 Stock Exchange of Thailand filing identified Digital Identity as a joint venture between:

- **KYC Now Co., Ltd. — 75%**
- **East Coast Furnitech PCL — 25%**

The filing described plans to develop identity-verification technology, including passport and facial verification systems.[^2]

On **May 15, 2025**, East Coast Furnitech sold its entire 25% holding to KYC Now. The transaction was publicly disclosed the following day, establishing that KYC Now acquired the remaining shares previously held by the publicly listed company.[^3]

## THIM Immigration Platform

On May 28, 2026, Amazon Web Services announced that Digital Identity had developed the **Thailand Immigration Management System (THIM)** in partnership with Thailand's Immigration Bureau, using AWS infrastructure in Bangkok.[^4]

THIM's published Privacy Notice identifies Digital Identity as its application developer and technical service provider. It separately names **KYC Now Co., Ltd. as a processor handling biometric and document verification**.[^5]

The system processes sensitive immigration information, including passport records, facial biometric templates, and electronic passport-chip data.[^5]

Separately, [Agents Co., Ltd.](agents-co-ltd.md), which is associated with [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md), registered the domain [thim.in.th](../domains/thim.in.th.md) on May 29, 2026, and used it to promote paid TDAC submission services.

The timing and shared THIM branding warrant examination, although **no corporate or contractual relationship between Digital Identity and Agents Co., Ltd. has been established**.

## Discrepancies in THIM's App Store Privacy Disclosures

The official THIM application is distributed through Google Play and Apple's App Store under the Royal Thai Police's developer identity.

However, its Google Play listing identifies Digital Identity's corporate domain, `di.co.th`, as the application's support contact, further documenting the company's operational involvement.[^6]

### Apple's Incomplete Privacy Labels

As of November 25, 2026, THIM's Apple App Store privacy disclosure identifies the user's name as the only category of collected information linked to their identity.[^7]

Email addresses, telephone numbers, user identifiers, and diagnostic information are classified as information not linked to the user's identity.

The disclosure does not separately identify the collection of biometric information or passport records.

However, Sections III and IV of THIM's official Privacy Notice, version 3.0, dated October 9, 2026, describe the collection of passport details, facial biometric templates, machine-readable passport information, electronic passport-chip data, and potentially location information.[^5]

Apple's developer documentation expressly includes biometrics within its sensitive-information category and requires developers to account for data collected by their third-party partners.[^8]

**The application's published App Store privacy labels do not adequately reflect the scope of identity verification and sensitive-data processing described in its official Privacy Notice.**

### Google Play's Data-Sharing Declaration

THIM's Google Play listing states that no data is shared with third parties.[^6]

The application's Privacy Notice nevertheless identifies several categories of potential recipients, including government agencies, law-enforcement authorities, and contracted technology providers such as KYC Now Co., Ltd.[^5]

Google's Data Safety rules contain exceptions for service providers processing information on behalf of developers and certain legally required disclosures.[^9]

Consequently, the Google Play declaration does not independently establish a policy violation. However, travelers should not interpret it as an assurance that their information remains exclusively within the Immigration Bureau.

### Biometric Processing and Automated Risk Assessment

The official Privacy Notice establishes a general three-year retention period calculated from collection or the most recent processing activity, subject to specified extensions.

Section XIV also identifies automated security risk scoring among the application's processing activities, alongside biometric matching and document authentication.

The notice provides for human oversight, immigration-officer appeals, manual intervention, and algorithmic auditing.[^5]

However, it does not identify the specific criteria used in automated risk assessments or clarify the respective technical responsibilities of Digital Identity and KYC Now in developing and maintaining these systems.

The available evidence does not establish unauthorized access, unlawful disclosure, or improper automated immigration decisions.

Nevertheless, the discrepancies warrant clarification given the sensitivity of the records involved and the potentially significant consequences of immigration risk assessments.

**The Immigration Bureau and its contracted technology providers should ensure that public app-store disclosures accurately communicate the categories of personal and biometric information collected through THIM.**

## Commercial Partnerships and Monetization of Immigration Services

Thailand's official THIM application has expanded beyond immigration administration into commercial promotions, financial services, telecommunications, transportation, retail, hospitality, and healthcare.

On November 2, 2026, WorldTechPro Co., Ltd. announced that it was coordinating a network of more than 40 commercial partners providing services and promotional benefits through THIM.[^10]

Named participants include:

- True Corporation.
- Grab Thailand.
- The Mall Group.
- WiPay.
- Minor International and Minor Food Group.
- Bangkok Dusit Medical Services (BDMS).

The announcement identifies WorldTechPro as responsible for recruiting and coordinating commercial partners for the immigration application.

### Financial Products Promoted Through THIM

On September 28, 2026, WiPay announced a promotional campaign specifically targeting foreign THIM users.[^11]

Travelers receiving promotional codes through the immigration application could purchase Wi Wallet prepaid Visa cards for 1,000 baht, with temporary transaction-fee exemptions and conditional cashback benefits.

On November 11, 2026, The Mall Group separately announced a commercial strategy connecting THIM, Wi Wallet, and WORPT to increase spending by international visitors.[^12]

THIM users can obtain shopping vouchers and promotional benefits before arriving in Thailand.

**The official immigration application therefore also functions as a distribution channel for selected commercial businesses.**

### Shared Biometric Technology Provider

The relationship becomes particularly relevant when examining WORPT, a commercial cryptocurrency-conversion platform operated by Wipay Co., Ltd. and Waan Exchange Co., Ltd.

WORPT's Privacy Notice explicitly identifies KYC Now Co., Ltd. as a processor responsible for passport authentication, electronic passport-chip processing, facial matching, and liveness detection.[^13]

The same company is identified as a biometric and document-verification processor in THIM's official Privacy Notice.[^5]

KYC Now was also Digital Identity's original majority shareholder and acquired East Coast Furnitech's remaining 25% interest in May 2025.[^2][^3]

These disclosures establish an overlapping technology provider serving both government immigration infrastructure and commercial financial services targeting international travelers.

They do not establish that immigration records are transferred between the two platforms or that their biometric databases are interconnected.

### Commercial Transparency and Purpose Limitation

Section IX of THIM's official Privacy Notice identifies immigration administration, identity verification, security screening, government recordkeeping, and service improvement among its processing purposes.[^5]

It does not expressly identify commercial advertising, financial-product marketing, or retail customer acquisition as separate processing purposes.

Displaying general promotional offers does not necessarily require disclosing immigration records to participating businesses.

Nevertheless, the expansion of commercial partnerships raises important accountability questions:

- How are commercial partners selected?
- Do participating businesses pay referral fees or commissions?
- Which organizations receive revenue from commercial activities within THIM?
- Are promotional offers personalized using immigration information?
- How are government and commercial biometric-processing environments kept separate?

The available evidence does not establish unlawful data sharing, improper procurement, or undisclosed financial compensation.

**However, the integration of commercial promotions into an official immigration application warrants greater transparency concerning financial arrangements, partner selection, and the separation of government records from commercial customer data.**
