# Sawadeevisa (888) Co., Ltd.

**Sawadeevisa (888) Co., Ltd.** (บริษัท สวัสดีวีซ่า(888) จำกัด) is a Thai company incorporated on **May 2, 2016**, under registration number **0105559069387**.

The company was originally registered as **Kindex Co., Ltd.** and changed its name to Sawadeevisa (888) on **December 16, 2021**. Thai corporate records list it as active, with registered capital of **1 million baht**.[^1]

## Registered Business Activities

The company's registered activities include visa applications, work permits, and immigration-related consulting.

However, its most recently reported financial-statement business classification is **wholesale of other food products**, rather than immigration services.[^1]

This discrepancy does not establish which activities the company currently conducts.

## Distinction From Sawadee (888) Co., Ltd.

[Sawadee (888) Co., Ltd.](sawadee-888-co-ltd.md) is a separate company incorporated on **November 20, 2014**, under registration number **0105557172466**.

Corporate records show that the older company was classified as abandoned on **October 2, 2020**.[^2]

Nevertheless, the commercial website `sawadeevisa.com` continues to identify **Sawadee (888) Co., Ltd.** in its copyright footer while advertising visa extensions, work permits, and company registration services.[^3]

The website also advertises a **100% success rate and money-back guarantee**, without independently verifiable approval statistics.[^3]

## Corporate Identity and Branding

The continued use of the older company's name on an active commercial website creates uncertainty about which registered entity currently provides the advertised services.

The two companies have different registration numbers, incorporation dates, and reported corporate statuses.

A third company, [Sawadee (2497) Co., Ltd.](sawadee-2497-co-ltd.md), is now identified on the related `thaivisacenter.com` website.

The available records establish these separate corporate identities but do not establish a formal transfer of operations or common ownership.
