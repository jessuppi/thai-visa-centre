# Agents Co., Ltd.

The company's website at [agents.co.th](../domains/agents.co.th.md) identifies **AGENTS CO., LTD.** as registered in Thailand on **December 12, 2019**, under company registration number **0115562031107**. These details are self-published by the company.

In a [May 2025 ASEAN NOW discussion](../threads/2025-05-01-asean-now-fake-tdac-emerges.md), [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) publicly identified AGENTS CO., LTD. as the operator of [tdac.in.th](../domains/tdac.in.th.md) and [tdac.agents.co.th](../domains/tdac.agents.co.th.md).

During the discussion, the agency also described a paid early-submission service for Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) applications through tdac.in.th.

These statements document the agency's publicly claimed operational relationship with the company. They do not independently establish legal ownership of either domain or the full extent of the company's activities.

The company's website also lists [90day.in.th](../domains/90day.in.th.md) as a 90-day immigration reporting service and [asq.in.th](../domains/asq.in.th.md) as its Ask Thailand community platform. These are the company's own descriptions, not independent verification of domain ownership.
