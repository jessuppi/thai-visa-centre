# Agents Co., Ltd.

**AGENTS CO., LTD.** (บริษัท เอเจนท์ จำกัด) is a Thai limited company incorporated on **December 12, 2019**, under registration number **0115562031107**. [Thai corporate records](https://www.dataforthai.com/company/0115562031107/) list the company as active, with registered capital of **10 million baht**.

Its registered business activities include brokerage and agency services involving visas and work permits for foreign nationals. Its latest filed business description concerns website programming and online hotel reservation services.

## Connection to Thai Visa Centre

In a [May 2025 ASEAN NOW discussion](../threads/2025-05-01-asean-now-fake-tdac-emerges.md), [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) publicly identified AGENTS CO., LTD. as the operator of [tdac.in.th](../domains/tdac.in.th.md) and [tdac.agents.co.th](../domains/tdac.agents.co.th.md).

During the discussion, the agency also described a paid early-submission service for Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) applications through tdac.in.th.

## Associated Domains

A [THNIC WHOIS record](https://www.whoxy.com/90day.in.th) dated October 2, 2026 identifies AGENTS CO., LTD. as the registered domain-holder organization for [90day.in.th](../domains/90day.in.th.md).

The company's website describes 90day.in.th as an immigration reporting service and identifies [asq.in.th](../domains/asq.in.th.md) as its Ask Thailand community platform.
