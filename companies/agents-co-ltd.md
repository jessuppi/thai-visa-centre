# Agents Co., Ltd.

**AGENTS CO., LTD.** (บริษัท เอเจนท์ จำกัด) is a Thai limited company incorporated on **December 12, 2019**, under registration number **0115562031107**. Thai corporate records list the company as active, with registered capital of **10 million baht**.[^1]

Its registered business activities include brokerage and agency services involving visas and work permits for foreign nationals. Its latest filed business description concerns website programming and online hotel reservation services.[^1]

## Connection to Thai Visa Centre

[Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) has publicly identified AGENTS CO., LTD. as the operator of websites used for its immigration-related services. In a [May 2025 ASEAN NOW discussion](../threads/2025-05-01-asean-now-fake-tdac-emerges.md), the agency's forum account named AGENTS CO., LTD. as the operator of [tdac.in.th](../domains/tdac.in.th.md) and [tdac.agents.co.th](../domains/tdac.agents.co.th.md), and described a paid early-submission TDAC service.[^2]

The agency's own website also describes AGENTS CO., LTD. as its digital operations arm, responsible for TDAC, e-visa, and 90-day reporting platforms.[^7]

In a [May 2026 Reddit discussion](../threads/2026-05-reddit-thailand-90-day-online-reporting.md), Thai Visa Centre acknowledged charging 375–500 baht for assisted immigration reporting through [90day.in.th](../domains/90day.in.th.md).[^6] A THNIC WHOIS record independently identifies AGENTS CO., LTD. as the registered holder of that domain.[^3]

## Associated Domains

A THNIC WHOIS record dated October 2, 2026 identifies AGENTS CO., LTD. as the registered domain-holder organization for [90day.in.th](../domains/90day.in.th.md).[^3]

A THNIC WHOIS record dated October 24, 2026 also identifies AGENTS CO., LTD. as the registered domain-holder organization for [thim.in.th](../domains/thim.in.th.md), which was registered on May 29, 2026.[^5]

The company's website describes 90day.in.th as an immigration reporting service and identifies [asq.in.th](../domains/asq.in.th.md) as its Ask Thailand community platform.[^4]
