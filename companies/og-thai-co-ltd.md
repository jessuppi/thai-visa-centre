# OG Thai Co., Ltd.

**OG Thai Co., Ltd.**, now known as **GACP Co., Ltd.**, is a Thai company incorporated on August 21, 2020, under registration number **0105563123196**.

The company changed its name on **June 19, 2025**. Corporate records list it as active, with registered capital of **40 million baht**.[^1]

## OG.TH and Herb Plug Clinic

The cannabis delivery website [OG.TH](../domains/og.th.md) identifies OG Thai Co., Ltd. in its published legal policies.[^2]

Separately, OG.TH identifies GACP Co., Ltd. as the operator of **Herb Plug Thai Traditional Medicine Clinic**, which provides medical consultations and prescription services integrated with the website.[^3]

The published information does not fully explain the commercial and operational arrangements between the cannabis storefront and the clinic.
