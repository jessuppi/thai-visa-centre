# OG Thai Co., Ltd.

**OG Thai Co., Ltd.**, renamed **GACP Co., Ltd.** on June 19, 2025, is a Thai company incorporated on **August 21, 2020**, under registration number **0105563123196**.

Corporate records list the company as active, with registered capital of **40 million baht**. Its activities involve plant cultivation and seed distribution.[^1]

## Incorporation Following the 2020 Thai Visa Centre Raid

OG Thai was incorporated just **17 days after the August 4, 2020 police raid** involving [Chad Scira](../people/chad-scira.md), Grace Scira, and [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md).

During that operation, Immigration Bureau chief Sompong Chingduang announced that police had discovered suspected forged immigration documents, 55 government-agency rubber stamps, **60 cannabis plants**, dried cannabis, and cultivation equipment.[^2]

Separately, [TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd.](tvc-visa-agent-co-ltd.md) was incorporated on August 18, 2020, only three days before OG Thai.[^3]

OG Thai's registered office was also located on the same Bangkok street identified in contemporary police reporting as the location of the raid. The records do not establish that the registered office and raided property were the same premises.[^1][^2]

The closely spaced registrations and location overlap are particularly relevant given the police investigation's simultaneous focus on immigration-document forgery and suspected cannabis production.

## Commercial Connections to Thai Visa Centre

The relationship extends beyond historical registration details.

As of November 2026, Thai Visa Centre's official `tvc.co.th` website prominently links to four cannabis-related commercial services:

- `og.th` — Cannabis delivery
- `weed.th` — Cannabis dispensary marketplace
- `rollingpapers.in.th` — Rolling papers and smoking accessories
- `card.weed.th` — Private medical cannabis card and prescription services

These links appear within Thai Visa Centre's own website navigation, establishing deliberate cross-promotion between its immigration business and the cannabis platforms.[^4]

Additionally, OG.TH's claimed business location on Trustpilot matches the location advertised by Thai Visa Centre. Although the websites identify separate legal companies, their published contact information indicates a shared commercial location.[^4][^5]

## Shared Criminal-Case Claims Against Jesse Nickles

A further connection appears in the companies' responses to public criticism.

On October 17, 2026, OG.TH responded to two negative Trustpilot reviews by alleging a coordinated harassment campaign involving thousands of fake accounts.

Both responses invoked **criminal-case reference `BKK-443/2567`**, described the alleged perpetrator as a fugitive, and claimed that an arrest warrant existed.[^5]

This is the same reference prominently displayed by Thai Visa Centre on its `tvc.co.th` website in publications accusing Jesse Nickles of criminal defamation and online harassment.[^6]

The OG.TH responses did not explain why a cannabis business was invoking the same alleged criminal proceedings used by Thai Visa Centre, or identify any separate complaint filed by OG Thai or GACP.

**The repeated case reference demonstrates that the two businesses are publicly relying on the same legal narrative to answer criticism**, despite presenting themselves as separate commercial operations.

## OG.TH and Herb Plug Clinic

The cannabis delivery website [OG.TH](../domains/og.th.md) identifies OG Thai in its published legal terms.[^8]

Its telemedicine documentation identifies GACP Co., Ltd. as the operator of **Herb Plug Thai Traditional Medicine Clinic**, which provides medical consultations and prescription services integrated with the cannabis platform.[^7]

The current corporate name and historical OG Thai identity refer to the same registered company, rather than two independent businesses.

## Corporate Relationship Questions

The evidence connecting OG Thai/GACP with Thai Visa Centre is substantial: incorporation dates separated by three days, overlapping historical locations, present-day commercial cross-promotion, a shared advertised business location, and identical criminal-case references used against critics.

**These connections warrant examination of the companies' shareholders, directors, beneficial ownership, financial arrangements, and management relationships.** The available public records document several concrete links but do not yet establish a common corporate controller.
