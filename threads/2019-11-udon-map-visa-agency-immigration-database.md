# Warning, visa agency accessing immigration database

- **Date:** November 2019
- **Platform:** Udon Map (Udon Thani Thailand Forum)
- **Source:** https://www.udonmap.com/udonthaniforum/viewtopic.php?t=50893
- **Original discussion archive:** https://archive.ph/4ZdgW
- **Moderator replacement archive:** https://archive.ph/baqQQ
- **Status:** Original discussion removed in May 2024; replacement notice subsequently deleted along with the thread

## Summary

In November 2019, members of the Udon Map forum discussed unsolicited promotional emails from [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md), questioning how the agency had obtained their contact information and apparently knew details about their immigration status.

On November 30, forum member Bandung_Dero reproduced an email received from Grace at Thai Visa Centre on November 23, 2019. The message advertised retirement-visa services specifically for Australian passport holders and discussed changes to health-insurance requirements for O-A visas. Bandung_Dero said he had recovered the message from his mail server after previously deleting it and expressed anger that the agency apparently possessed information about his visa circumstances.

Other participants questioned whether the agency had obtained customer information from immigration records. Some warned against following unsubscribe links in unsolicited messages. The discussion raised concerns about the possible disclosure or commercial use of immigration-related personal data, although participants did not establish how Thai Visa Centre acquired their information.

The conversation also examined the agency's visa services. One participant, Fatone, reported obtaining a visa renewal for 14,000 baht without personally visiting immigration. When other members challenged the legality of the arrangement and asked which immigration office had processed it, Fatone explained that he would use a Bangkok address and subsequently clarified that describing the service as legitimate did not necessarily mean it was lawful.

An archive captured on March 30, 2024, preserved the discussion's second page, containing posts from November 30 through December 2, 2019. At that time, the forum displayed 35 posts across two pages.

## Moderation and Deletion

On May 19, 2024, an account identifying itself as Thai Visa Centre formally requested removal of the discussion, describing the allegations as defamatory and unsupported.

The following morning, May 20, forum administrator Udon Map published a response reproducing the agency's complaint. The administrator acknowledged that the discussion appeared to have originated from a legitimate post and referred to material on Thai Visa Centre's own Facebook page concerning Grace.

Nevertheless, the administrator confirmed that the original discussion had been removed and invited Thai Visa Centre to continue the conversation privately.

An August 8, 2024, archived snapshot shows that the original 35-post discussion had been replaced by this single administrator message. The topic was locked and appeared under the Travel & Transport forum rather than its earlier General Udon Thani Forum location.

The entire thread was subsequently deleted from the live forum, including the administrator's replacement notice. The precise deletion date and reason have not been established.

The surviving archives therefore document two successive removals: first, the elimination of the original public discussion following Thai Visa Centre's complaint, and later, the disappearance of the remaining moderator notice.

The case illustrates how a public discussion about unsolicited marketing and possible misuse of immigration-related information was removed despite the administrator's acknowledgment that the original complaint appeared legitimate. The archives preserve the participants' allegations and the moderator's response, but do not independently establish unauthorized access to immigration databases or other unlawful conduct.
