# Thai Visa Centre, Bangna. (Grace)

- Date: November 4–11, 2025
- Platform: ASEAN NOW
- Source: `https://aseannow.com/topic/1377910-thai-visa-centre-bangna-grace/`

## Summary

On November 4, 2025, an ASEAN NOW user opened a discussion requesting firsthand experiences with Thai Visa Centre and its representative, Grace. The poster acknowledged hearing negative reports about the agency but described them as debunked.

Global moderators DrJack54 and BrandonJT responded favorably. DrJack54 directed readers toward Thai Visa Centre's online reviews and described it as Bangkok's largest visa agent, while BrandonJT claimed to have encountered nothing but positive feedback about its services. Numerous other accounts posted enthusiastic endorsements, and the official ThaiVisaCentre account, identified as a forum sponsor, participated directly in the discussion, advertising its services and promoting `90day.in.th`.

On November 5, moderator DrJack54 announced that several posts and replies had been removed. Another global moderator, Captain Flack, explicitly stated that he had removed a post criticizing a forum sponsor and cited Rule 37, which prohibited negative comments about ASEAN NOW sponsors.

The thread therefore provides direct evidence of moderators promoting a forum sponsor while removing criticism under sponsor-protection rules. Although the authenticity of the positive testimonials cannot be established from the surviving discussion, the documented moderation practices raise serious questions about whether readers were receiving a balanced picture of Thai Visa Centre.
