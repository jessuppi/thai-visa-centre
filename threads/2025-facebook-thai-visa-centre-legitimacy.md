# Is the Thai Visa Centre a legitimate company in Bangkok?

- **Date:** 2025 (exact date unverified)
- **Platform:** Facebook (Expats in Bangkok - Forum)
- **Source:** https://www.facebook.com/groups/1206669499358683/posts/32024901500442079/

## Summary

A Facebook post supposedly questioning the legitimacy of [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) attracted numerous positive endorsements, criticism from Jesse Nickles, and a response from the agency's official account.

Nickles suspects the thread was **staged promotional content**, with the original question and several favorable comments allegedly posted by fake Facebook profiles or sockpuppet accounts associated with Thai Visa Centre. Many endorsements were brief and generic, while others claimed positive experiences with the agency. The suspected coordination remains unverified.

Nickles posted a detailed warning accusing Thai Visa Centre and Chad Scira of unsolicited email spam, online harassment, denial-of-service attacks, doxxing, fraudulent complaints, and filing a false police report against him. He also questioned the agency's corporate registration and years-in-business claims, referencing Scira's reported 2020 arrest involving alleged visa forgery and illegal cannabis production.

Thai Visa Centre's official Facebook account responded with a link to its Google-listed office location.

**Nickles reports being banned from the Facebook group shortly afterward.** The group listed Paul Jackson Bruton as administrator and David Sweetway as moderator, although responsibility for the ban and its justification remain unconfirmed.

The incident raises concerns about alleged astroturfing, fabricated customer endorsements, and the suppression of criticism in Thailand-focused Facebook communities.
