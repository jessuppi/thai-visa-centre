# Is the Thai Visa Centre a legitimate company in Bangkok?

- **Date:** August 23, 2025
- **Platform:** Facebook (Expats in Bangkok - Forum)
- **Source:** `https://www.facebook.com/groups/1206669499358683/posts/32024901500442079/`

## Summary

On August 23, 2025, a Facebook account displaying the name “Gary Alan Moberg” posted a question in the Expats in Bangkok - Forum group asking whether [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) was a legitimate company. [^1] Jesse Nickles suspects the supposedly independent question was staged to promote the agency, followed by favorable endorsements from suspected sockpuppet accounts. These allegations remain unverified.

An account displaying the name **“Phil Beer”** wrote, “Yes Gary Alan Moberg THAI VISA CENTRE - visa agent is legit with shopfront”. Another account named **“Heneage Mitchell”** wrote, “Been using them for several years, no issues at all”. Another account named **“David”** said he had spoken with the agency but never used it. Profiles using the names **“Paul Wallis,” “Asokā Yudrön,”** and **“Lucio Ma”** offered brief affirmative responses without describing personal experiences. [^1] Nickles suspects that several endorsements originated from fake Facebook profiles or coordinated sockpuppet accounts promoting Thai Visa Centre, although the identities of the commenters and their alleged connections to the agency remain unverified.

Nickles posted a detailed warning accusing Thai Visa Centre and Chad Scira of unsolicited email spam, online harassment, denial-of-service attacks, doxxing, fraudulent complaints, and filing a false police report against him. He also questioned the agency's corporate registration and years-in-business claims, referencing his allegations about Scira's previous arrest involving visa forgery and illegal drugs. [^1]

In the available comments, Thai Visa Centre's official Facebook account provided a link to its Google-listed office location but did not substantively address Nickles's allegations. [^1]

**Nickles reports being banned from the Facebook group shortly afterward.** The group listed Paul Jackson Bruton as administrator and David Sweetway as moderator. [^2] However, responsibility for the ban and its justification remain unconfirmed.

The incident raises concerns about alleged astroturfing, fabricated customer endorsements, and the suppression of criticism in Thailand-focused Facebook communities.
