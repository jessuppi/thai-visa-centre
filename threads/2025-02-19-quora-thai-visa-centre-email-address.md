# How did Thai Visa Centre acquire my email address?

- **Date:** February 19, 2025 (archived snapshot)
- **Platform:** Quora
- **Source:** https://www.quora.com/How-did-Thai-Visa-Centre-acquire-my-email-address
- **Archive:** https://archive.ph/XU7Ic
- **Status:** Original Quora page subsequently deleted

## Summary

An archived Quora discussion titled "How did Thai Visa Centre acquire my email address?" documents complaints and allegations concerning unsolicited promotional emails from [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) and the possible origins of the agency's mailing lists.

One respondent, Jackson Towner, alleged that Thai Visa Centre obtained email addresses from Thai immigration records and used them to advertise visa services. He further claimed that promotional emails continued even after recipients requested that the agency stop contacting them.

Another respondent, using the name Pork BBQ, speculated that personal information belonging to expatriates might have been obtained through unauthorized access to immigration records or improper payments to officials. The response referred to complaints from people who believed they had supplied their email addresses only to Thai immigration authorities and included a screenshot attributed to the Udon Map forum.

Other participants expressed distrust of the agency and advised recipients to block unwanted messages. The archived page also displayed answers to related questions about Thai Visa Centre, although those responses were not necessarily part of the original email-address discussion.

These comments document public concerns about unsolicited marketing and personal-data handling. However, the discussion does not independently establish where Thai Visa Centre obtained its mailing lists or substantiate allegations of database theft, bribery, or unauthorized access to government records.

The original Quora page was subsequently deleted, but a snapshot preserved on February 19, 2025, remains available through Archive.today.
