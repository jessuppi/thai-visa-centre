# Grace @ Thai Visa Centre back

- **Date:** August 12, 2020
- **Platform:** ASEAN NOW (formerly ThaiVisa.com)
- **Replies:** 53
- **Source:** `https://aseannow.com/topic/1177344-grace-thai-visa-centre-back/`

## Summary

On August 12, 2020, just eight days after Thai police announced the arrests connected to [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md)'s alleged visa forgery operation, forum members reported receiving emails from the agency announcing that it was continuing business as usual. One participant reproduced an email dated August 10 from someone identifying herself as Grace, who reassured customers that recent news reports were nothing to worry about and claimed that Thai Immigration had verified the agency as legitimate.

The announcement drew criticism from forum participants who questioned how Thai Visa Centre could resume operations so quickly following a police investigation involving allegedly forged immigration documents and 55 government-agency rubber stamps. Commenters also raised concerns about unsolicited marketing emails, possible access to immigration contact information, and contradictory statements about whether Grace remained employed by the agency.

Several participants questioned whether the investigation had been "quietly" resolved, while others defended the agency or cautioned against unsupported accusations. The discussion provides contemporaneous evidence of Thai Visa Centre's efforts to reassure customers and resume marketing immediately after the August 2020 arrests, including its claim of official immigration approval without supporting documentation presented in the thread.
