# Thai Visa Centre & Immigration

- Date: August 4–6, 2026
- Platform: ASEAN NOW
- Source: `https://aseannow.com/topic/1401947-thai-visa-centre-immigration/`

## Summary

An August 2026 ASEAN NOW discussion began when a [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) customer asked whether retirement visa extensions arranged through the agency would be accepted by immigration officers when applying for other services, including a Certificate of Residence or opening a bank account.

The original poster, TonBrow, subsequently explained that their primary concern was whether the visa stamps obtained through Thai Visa Centre were legitimate. They acknowledged that the agency sent passports to immigration offices in Thailand's northeastern Isan region and worried that presenting those stamps at their local immigration office might expose irregularities.

The customer also disputed another participant's claims about the agency's services, stating that Thai Visa Centre had never provided them with TM30 documentation and charged 500 baht for each 90-day report.

Global moderator DrJack54 questioned which immigration office issued the extension, where the customer lived, and where their 90-day reports were processed. Other participants explained that immigration officers could scrutinize extensions issued outside a person's province of residence, particularly where discrepancies suggested an agent had arranged extensions without meeting normal financial requirements.

The official ThaiVisaCentre account, identified as a forum sponsor, participated in the discussion and advertised its ability to arrange residence certificates using digital passport copies. Several other accounts strongly defended the agency, although their independence was not established.

The discussion documents a customer's firsthand concerns about out-of-province visa processing, questions about the legitimacy of agency-arranged extensions, and the potential consequences of presenting those documents directly to immigration authorities. No evidence was presented establishing that the customer's stamps were forged.
