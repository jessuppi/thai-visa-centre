# Fake TDAC Website Emerges as New System Debuts in Thailand

- Date: May 1–7, 2025
- Platform: ASEAN NOW
- Source: `https://aseannow.com/topic/1359413-fake-tdac-website-emerges-as-new-system-debuts-in-thailand/`

## Summary

A May 2025 ASEAN NOW discussion about fraudulent Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) websites included several revealing statements from the official ThaiVisaCentre account, identified as a forum sponsor.

During a discussion about third-party websites charging travelers to submit Thailand's free TDAC form, [Thai Visa Centre](../brands/thai-visa-centre.md) criticized one such website as misleading and potentially unlawful. The agency argued that commercial TDAC providers must clearly disclose their private ownership, obtain customer consent, and explain their fees.

In the same discussion, Thai Visa Centre acknowledged operating [`tdac.in.th`](../domains/tdac.in.th.md), initially describing it as an informational website that provided no paid services and emphasized that TDAC registration was free.

However, the agency subsequently edited its comment to announce that `tdac.in.th` had launched a paid early-application service. It advertised an $8 fee for applications submitted ahead of the government's standard 72-hour registration window, while offering free submissions within that window.

In another response, Thai Visa Centre explicitly identified both `tdac.in.th` and [`tdac.agents.co.th`](../domains/tdac.agents.co.th.md) as websites operated by [AGENTS CO., LTD.](../companies/agents-co-ltd.md), while acknowledging that Thailand's official government registration portal was `tdac.immigration.go.th`.

The discussion provides direct public evidence linking Thai Visa Centre, AGENTS CO., LTD., and the privately operated TDAC websites. It also documents the agency's transition from describing `tdac.in.th` as purely informational to advertising paid submission services, while simultaneously criticizing other third-party TDAC websites for misleading travelers. The agency's claims of legitimacy and legal compliance were its own statements, not government endorsements.
